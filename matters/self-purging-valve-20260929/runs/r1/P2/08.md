#### N0 — nucleus

**Novelty.** Closest single reference: ref-02 (FICTIONAL EP 3 456 789 B1). An examiner would read: seat formed by an elastomeric ring (claim 1; row N1) that "deflects outward to enlarge the flow passage" ([0019]; row N0.2), is "deflectable by the poppet during opening" (claim 4; row N0.2) and "returns under its own elasticity" to seal ([0020]; row N0.4). The objection relies on reading "over-travel" as any poppet travel during which the ring deflects, and "purge gap" as whatever flow path the deflection opens. Rated **moderate**: it fails on N0.1 (no second threshold beyond a normal stroke) and on N0.3 (the deflection enlarges the main passage; no secondary flow path beside it), but only if the claim says so in structural terms. A claim that recites "a gap opened by deflection of the seat" without tying it to a travel beyond the normal stroke and to a path distinct from the main passage would be anticipated.

Second-closest: US4044791A (row N0.1 two pressure regimes; row N0.3 secondary passageways 54 opened above "a predetermined value"). Rated **weak** for novelty: nothing deflects radially (row N0.2 absent) and the secondary path is a fixed bore.

**Inventive step.**
1. *ref-02 + US4044791A.* ref-02 supplies a seat deflected by the poppet and recovering to seal; US4044791A teaches that above a predetermined input pressure a second, secondary flow path opens in addition to the primary one (claim 1). Examiner's motivation: higher flow at higher input pressure, which US4044791A states as its purpose. Rated **moderate**: the motivation is flow rate, not cleaning, and the combination still gives a secondary path through the poppet rather than a gap opened by deflection of the seat. The examiner would have to redesign, not combine.
2. *ref-02 + US5918628A.* US5918628A teaches that the poppet should "stroke long enough such that particles will pass easily between the valve head and the valve seat" (Abstract). Examiner's argument: extending the travel of ref-02's poppet so the lip deflects further clears contamination, a known aim. Rated **moderate**: it gives cleaning motivation for extended travel, but the particle clearance is along the main passage and there is no second threshold.
3. *Field reference + ref-02 + US3570510A.* ref-01 and US20210323363A1 state the problem (sealant fouling the core; row F). US3570510A cleans a valve seat with air through an annular slit (row N0.3). ref-02 supplies the deflecting seat. Rated **weak–moderate**: three references, the air supply in US3570510A is a separate stored supply under a control means, and nothing links the slit to poppet travel. A hindsight argument, but three-reference combinations of this kind are routinely made at the EPO (problem-solution) and USPTO (KSR "predictable use of prior-art elements").
4. *Common general knowledge (assumption A1).* That blowing air past a fouled seat cleans it is likely to be asserted as CGK. It does not itself supply the self-opening structure.

#### N1 — one-piece seat ring as the deflectable element

**Novelty.** ref-02 claim 1: one elastomeric ring both seals and deflects (row N1 explicit). N1 adds nothing over ref-02 that N0 does not; it stands or falls with the N0 distinctions. **Moderate.**
**Inventive step.** As N0. N1 also excludes the two-piece seat, which weakens coverage without improving the position. **Moderate.**

#### N2 — tubular seat ring with convoluted wall; purge gap to the core body bore

**Novelty.** No charted reference shows a tubular seat ring whose convoluted wall converts axial load into radial expansion. **Weak.**
**Inventive step.** N0 combination + EP3171056A1 (SMA sealing ring "in a wave-like shape comprising at least one peak ... and at least one trough", claim 1) or US20040194970A1 (seal body expanded radially, claim 38). Argument: a wave or fold is a known way to give a ring radial compliance. Rated **weak–moderate**: EP3171056A1's wave runs radially in a static, thermally activated ring and converts nothing; US20040194970A1 expands by wire stimulus, not by axial load. The examiner's better line is a routine-design argument (assumption A2): a corrugated tube expands when compressed axially, which a skilled person knows. The annular purge gap to the core body bore is not shown anywhere; but its enablement is open (03 Q2), and an unenabled distinction is worth little.

#### N3 — purge shoulder; two-rate characteristic

**Novelty.** None. **Weak.**
**Inventive step.** N0 combination + US4044791A (two separate springs 15 and 16 give two pressure regimes) + US1934572A (a tyre valve core already carries two spring-biased valves with different thresholds) + CGK that two-rate compression springs exist (assumption A3). Argument: once a second threshold is wanted, a two-rate return spring is an obvious alternative to two springs. Rated **moderate**. The purge shoulder is a routine load path once the ring is to be loaded by the poppet (**moderate**); it carries weight only as the structural link "contacts the seat ring only in over-travel".

#### N4 — superelastic NiTi seat ring, Af below 5 °C, snap-back wipe

**Novelty.** None. **Weak.**
**Inventive step.**
1. *Material.* ref-02 [0024] already offers a "spring steel lip seal" as an alternative seat. US9611937B2 (superelastic Ni–Ti metal seal, claim 1) and US8100410B2 (superelastic ring seal, claim 1) show superelastic metal seals. Argument: obvious material substitution for better recovery and fatigue. Rated **moderate**: both are static seals in other fields; the motivation in them is sealing force, not wiping.
2. *Wipe.* US3473561A: "The wiping motion dislodges any particles residing on the valving surface" (sealing edge 134); US4050473A: disk that performs "a wiping action to wipe away dirt particles from the valve seat". Argument: self-cleaning by wiping at the seat is known; a recovering seat will inherently rub the poppet. Rated **moderate**: both wipe with the moving member; neither ties the wipe to shape recovery of the seat. The inherency argument is the one to watch: if the specification says only that recovery "wipes", an examiner will say any recovering seat does so. The effect needs to be tied to the speed and force of superelastic recovery (§2) and to the bench observation (§8), and the bench test does not isolate the wipe (03 Q1).
3. *Af below 5 °C.* Routine selection of a transformation temperature for superelastic behaviour at use temperature (assumption A4). **Strong** as a stand-alone limitation; useful only as a dependent fallback.

#### N5 — operating window

**Inventive step.** Routine optimisation of known variables (stroke, threshold, gap width, wall thickness) once N0 is known. **Strong.** Only one value per range is built (01 (m) M8) and no criticality is shown, so the ranges will not carry inventive step at the EPO or USPTO; they are amendment fallbacks and enablement support, not distinctions.

#### N6 — prototype and process

**Inventive step.** US9611937B2 Abstract ("Superelasticity may be imparted ... through a thermal treatment process") plus CGK of laser cutting, shape-setting and electropolishing of NiTi tube (assumption A5). Rated **moderate–strong** as process; the concrete 0.20 mm convoluted ring pressed against a locating shoulder is a narrow fallback, not a distinction.

#### What the examiner will lead with

1. ref-02 alone against any N0 claim that does not separate the purge gap from the main passage and the over-travel from the normal stroke.
2. ref-02 + US5918628A (extended stroke to clear particles) against N0 that does separate them.
3. ref-02 [0024] + US9611937B2 + US3473561A against N4.
4. Routine optimisation against N5 and the process steps of N6.

## Rationale

ref-02 leads because it is the only reference that has a seat deflected by the poppet and recovering to seal, in the tyre valve field. It is fictional, but it stands in for the kind of art a professional search is likely to find, and drafting against it is cheap insurance.

I rated the N0 novelty objection moderate, not weak, because its failure depends entirely on claim wording: the two structural distinctions (second threshold, separate secondary path) must be recited, and the purge gap must be defined, otherwise ref-02 reads on. That is a drafting instruction more than a verdict.

US5918628A was ranked above US4044791A as a secondary reference for N0 because it supplies a contamination-clearing motivation for extended travel; US4044791A's motivation is flow rate, which does not lead to cleaning.

Wiping was rated moderate rather than weak because the inherency argument (any recovering seat rubs) is cheap for an examiner and the bench data does not isolate the wipe.

## Assumptions

A1. Common general knowledge (not tied to a charted reference): blowing air past a fouled seat helps clean it. An examiner is likely to assert this. Not blocking.
A2. Common general knowledge (not tied to a charted reference): a corrugated or convoluted tube expands radially under axial compression. Not blocking.
A3. Common general knowledge (not tied to a charted reference): compression springs with two rates (variable pitch or nested springs) are standard components. Not blocking.
A4. Common general knowledge (not tied to a charted reference): the austenite finish temperature of NiTi is selected below the use temperature to obtain superelastic behaviour. Not blocking.
A5. Common general knowledge (not tied to a charted reference): laser cutting, shape-setting in a fixture and electropolishing of NiTi tube are standard processes. Not blocking.
A6. The two requester-supplied references are fictional; they are treated as published prior art. Not blocking.
