## Rationale

F1 is drafted at the level of the nucleus N0 of `P1/03` because `P2/08` found that a one-piece limitation (N1) excludes the two-piece seat without improving the position over ref-02. The genus "radially deflectable element" is the one `CONFIRMED.md` already uses.

F3 keeps the radially deflectable element. A function-level method without it would reach the rigid-part routes DA-10 and DA-14, but `CONFIRMED.md` says those cannot be closed from the dossier: every disclosed embodiment deforms an element, and whether the air purge alone gives the effect is unanswered. Drafting it would claim something the inventor has not been shown to have conceived. It is a disclosure request and an open question, not a claim.

F5 is kept because the value map ranks it third and the disclosure describes the seat ring fully. Its exposure is real: the claim cannot lean on the over-travel distinction, and the search did not cover convoluted tubular elements. I preferred a functional tie to the purge gap over the §4 dimensions, which `P2/08` rates as routine optimisation; the dimensions are fallbacks.

F4 is kept narrow and tied to F1. `P2/09` rates the process as easy to avoid and INDEX #18 shows the NiTi tube process chain, so the family is justified only as a claim whose novelty comes from the product it makes.

F2 and F6 are not separate families: each adds a defendant or a fallback but no feature that would justify another independent claim in the same category.

Kit, composition, software, product-by-process and the thermal variant were rejected as volume or as unsupported.
