## Rationale

**Why these three.** Claims 1 and 2 of `P5/22.r1.md` define the second travel range by pressure. PF-6, R1, R2 and R9 each show that the pressure threshold is the cheapest limitation to step outside or to make unprovable. Each formulation here removes it and replaces it with a different kind of limitation: parts and spacing (F1), relationships between travel range, load path and flow path (F2), and acts (F3). That is what makes them different scopes and not re-orderings of claim 1.

**F1.** A structural claim has to name structure, so it takes the one-piece seat ring, the purge shoulder and the outer-surface location, which `P5/22.r1.md` deliberately kept out of claim 1. I did not recite the convoluted wall: DA-3 shows it is avoided cheaply, and the locked functional description of the seat ring already gives the wall by what it does. The bore is claimed as "a bore surrounding the seat ring" with both disclosed boundaries named, following PF-3 and R4(b). I considered reciting the fit band and the free portion, which the registry carries as 35 and 37; they rest on the relieved core body bore, which is a candidate requiring the inventor's confirmation, so they are ladder material at most.

**F2.** I kept "radially deflectable element" from `P5/22.r1.md` and did not adopt the genus wording that 19 uses ("radially compliant seat element"): the wiper ring of the two-piece seat is not a seat, so the 19 wording fits it badly. "Spaced from" states positively what `P2/09` row 3 states with "only" (lesson L1). "Located in the core body" is dropped, which leaves room for the element formed in the core body or bounded by the valve stem (R4) without asserting either.

**F2 and ref-02.** Removing the pressure thresholds weakens the first of the two pillars of PF-1. I have recorded that in the comparison and not softened it. PF-6 takes the view that the structural definition keeps the distinction; I did not read ref-02 for this task and cannot confirm that its poppet does not travel before it meets the lip. If it does, F2 stands on the separate flow path and on the load path alone, and the pressure threshold becomes the first fallback, not an optional one.

**F3.** Claim 2 of `P5/22.r1.md` is already a method; F3 differs by being travel-based. "Air purge" is not used in step (c) because its locked functional description and the 19 definition carry a result (the sealant film carried into the tyre chamber), which the bench notes do not establish.

**Declined.** A method without the deflectable element (no support; PF-7 asks for it to be held as a candidate, and I hold it as a disclosure request because 19's definition of the purge gap excludes it). The inversion of R3 and the rigid floating seat of R5 (not conceived on the record). "Closed or restricted" for R7 (needs a definition in the description first). A system claim with the pump head (no disclosure beyond the pump head applying the pressure differential).

**Recommendation.** F1 lies wholly inside F2, so at the EPO and in the UK it is better used as three rungs than as a second independent claim. Re-casting claims 1 and 2 keeps their numbers, which the revision rule requires; the old wording becomes the first dependent in each case, so nothing drafted in `P5/22.r1.md` is lost.
