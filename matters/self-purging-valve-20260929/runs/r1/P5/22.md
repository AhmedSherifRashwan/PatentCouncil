1. A valve core for a tyre valve that admits air into a tyre chamber, the valve core comprising:
a core body;
a sealing edge located in the core body;
a poppet having a sealing face that seats on the sealing edge when the poppet is in a closed position, the poppet being movable from the closed position through a normal stroke when a pressure differential across the poppet exceeds a crack pressure, thereby opening a main passage past the sealing edge, and being movable through an over-travel beyond the normal stroke when the pressure differential exceeds an over-travel differential that is greater than the crack pressure; and
a radially deflectable element located in the core body and arranged to receive an axial load from movement of the poppet through the over-travel, the radially deflectable element converting the axial load into radial expansion of the radially deflectable element;
wherein the radial expansion opens a purge gap, the purge gap being a secondary flow path that is separate from the main passage and through which air flows across a surface of the radially deflectable element towards the tyre chamber;
wherein the purge gap is closed when the poppet is in the closed position and throughout the normal stroke; and
wherein the radially deflectable element undergoes shape recovery that closes the purge gap when the axial load is removed.

2. A method of removing sealant film from a valve core of a tyre valve during an inflation cycle, the valve core having a poppet, a sealing edge on which a sealing face of the poppet seats when the poppet is in a closed position, and a radially deflectable element, the method comprising:
applying across the poppet a pressure differential that exceeds a crack pressure, so that the poppet moves from the closed position through a normal stroke and air flows through a main passage past the sealing edge into a tyre chamber;
raising the pressure differential above an over-travel differential that is greater than the crack pressure, so that the poppet moves through an over-travel beyond the normal stroke and movement of the poppet through the over-travel applies an axial load to the radially deflectable element, the radially deflectable element converting the axial load into radial expansion that opens a purge gap, the purge gap being a secondary flow path separate from the main passage;
passing air through the purge gap as an air purge across a surface of the radially deflectable element and into the tyre chamber; and
removing the pressure differential, so that the poppet returns to the closed position and the purge gap closes.

3. A seat ring for a valve core of a tyre valve, the seat ring comprising a tube having:
a sealing edge at one end of the tube, for seating a sealing face of a poppet of the valve core;
a convoluted wall having at least one convolution, the convoluted wall converting an axial load applied to the seat ring by the poppet into radial expansion of the seat ring; and
an outer surface that, when the seat ring is retained in a core body bore of the valve core, closes a purge gap between the outer surface and the core body bore when the seat ring is unloaded and opens the purge gap on the radial expansion;
wherein the seat ring undergoes shape recovery that closes the purge gap when the axial load is removed.

4. A method of manufacturing the seat ring of claim 3, the tube being of a NiTi alloy, the method comprising shape-setting the tube in a fixture that holds the convoluted wall.

## Rationale

**Removed from claim 1 as not needed to distinguish the charted art.** The two-rate characteristic and the return spring (DA-1, DA-6: the threshold can come from the seat ring or from two springs); the purge shoulder and its position (DA-7); the convoluted wall (DA-3); the annular location of the purge gap and the core body bore (DA-5, and the unresolved fit conflict); the one-piece seat ring (N1, which `P2/08` says excludes the two-piece seat for no gain); superelastic material, NiTi alloy and the austenite finish temperature (obvious-substitution attack, and Alt-A is polymer); the snap-back wipe (inherency attack; the bench test does not isolate it); sealant in the preamble; any valve pattern. Each goes to the ladder.

**Kept although not a distinction.** Shape recovery in claims 1 and 3: `P1/03` classes it essential to the effect, and an independent claim without it invites an essential-features objection at the EPO. I took it out of claim 2, where "the purge gap closes" is enough for a method and lets the claim reach closure by positive return.

**"Only" avoided.** `P2/09` proposes "only when" and "only during". Lesson L1 warns against limitations that say what a part does not do. I state the same separation positively: the purge gap is closed in the closed position and throughout the normal stroke. I first considered "the poppet is spaced from the element throughout the normal stroke"; it is wrong for the one-piece seat ring, which the sealing face touches at rest.

**Load path.** "Arranged to receive an axial load from movement of the poppet" rather than "the poppet bears on": the second would let a collar or a spring seat washer escape (DA-7). The purge shoulder is the only load path the inventor disclosed; the wider wording is a relationship the disclosed species supports, not a statement that other load paths were conceived.

**Claim 2, result language removed.** A first wording had the sealant film "carried into the tyre chamber". The bench notes record cured film remaining on the outer surface in the purge gap region at cycle 40 (FM-4), so a result limitation there would be hard to prove and open to attack. The purpose stays in the preamble.

**INDEX #15.** US5924444A was added after the `07` charts and is the nearest real reference to the radial opening of a ring in a tyre-mounted valve. I read its claim 1 and the description of its first embodiment. Its ring is deformed by a jet of fluid, the gap it forms is the one flow path of the valve made larger, and flow is out of the tyre. Claim 1 here differs on the axial load from poppet travel, on the separate secondary flow path and on the second threshold. It should be charted before Prompt 32.

**Claim 3.** Tied to the purge function rather than to dimensions. It remains the most exposed claim.

**Claim 4.** Re-anchored on the seat ring (see `21.r1.md`). Laser cutting, quenching, electropolishing and press-fitting are dependent steps.

**Not drafted.** A claim with no second threshold (DA-2); a method with no deflectable element (DA-10, DA-14); over-travel by mechanical force (DA-15); non-radial deformation (DA-9). Each needs the inventor's confirmation and is a disclosure request.
