## Rationale

Categories were drawn from the four groups the prompt names (failure modes, manufacturing variability, environmental conditions, user errors) plus abuse cases, and anchored wherever possible to the inventor's own named unknowns (`01` (m) M2, M3, M6, M8, M10–M12, M16, M17) and to the one directly observed partial failure in the bench data (cured film in the purge-gap region at cycle 40). Rows without a dossier anchor (the positive stop, spring pre-set, purge-gap surface treatment, alignment chamfer, anti-rotation key, batch verification) are still concrete structures or process steps consistent with the disclosed materials and geometry, not results the dossier lacks; each is marked `candidate - needs confirmation` rather than asserted. Rows where an existing disclosed alternative (Alt-D, Alt-E) already answers the failure mode are marked accordingly rather than inventing a new embodiment. Rows with no technical mitigation available in the record (cross-threading, dust ingress, storage-only degradation, untested sealant chemistries) are marked `not worth disclosing` and carried instead as open questions, consistent with the instruction not to pad the register.
