## Rationale

Every ladder nests three things and only three things: the disclosed broad range (§4), the disclosed single prototype value, and this analysis's proposed intermediate/preferred subdivisions of that same disclosed range, built to bracket the prototype value symmetrically or near-symmetrically so the preferred band always contains the tested point. No rung uses a number that does not appear in the disclosure or is not a linear subdivision of numbers that do. Where §4 gives no range at all (normal stroke) or only a one-sided bound (austenite finish temperature), no subdivision is proposed, because there is nothing disclosed to subdivide and no stated physics from which to derive a second bound; inventing one would be exactly the fabrication the operating contract forbids.

The cycle-count and leak-down rows are performance values rather than design inputs, but the prompt's list of parameter types includes "performance values," and a fallback claim limitation of the "at least 40 inflation cycles" kind is a live possibility for Claims Architect, so both are laddered as far as the data supports: a proven lower bound and no proven upper bound, consistent with 01 (m) M10.

The valve stem outer diameter is the only row marked "confirmation needed: no," because the disclosure presents it as a conventional Presta-pattern dimension, not as part of the inventive combination; every other row either rests on the inventor's unmeasured belief range or on a single untested value, so every other row needs confirmation before it is asserted as a claim boundary.

The thermal variant (Alt-C) row is included because it is the only place in the record with an independent range (35–40 °C) for a second embodiment, but it is laddered no further than the disclosed endpoints, since 01 (m) M14 / 03 Q-equivalent leaves open whether Alt-C is kept at all.
