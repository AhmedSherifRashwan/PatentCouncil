### F-32-1 [high] [enablement] Principal embodiment: how the purge gap opens, and how air reaches it, is not disclosed and the stated geometry contradicts itself

- **Ground:** enablement / sufficiency; clarity
- **Claims:** 1, 2, 3, 16, 17, 18, 19, 22, 24, 45, 58, 65
- **Paragraphs and figures:** [0081], [0112], [0113], [0124], [0134], [0170], [0172], [0187], [0194], [0202], FIG. 2, FIG. 4A
- **Text attacked:**
  - claim 16: "wherein the purge gap is bounded by an outer surface of the radially deflectable element and by a bore surrounding the radially deflectable element"
  - [0081]: "means an outward movement of the wall of the seat ring"
  - [0112]: "The seat ring 30 is pressed into the core body 20 with a light interference fit"
  - [0113]: "an axial load applied to the seat ring 30, for example at its upper edge"
  - FIG. 2 instruction: "the purge shoulder (44) touching the seat ring (30) at the fit-band end of the convoluted wall (34)"
  - [0134]: "the convolution 36 flattens under the axial load"
  - [0172]: "The bow increases under axial load in the manner of a pre-buckled column."
  - [0194]: "The purge flow leaves the purge gap 60 into the tyre chamber 80."
  - [0202]: "They are not stated to be the construction of the valve cores tested"
- **Attack:** Five gaps, each on the face of the text. (1) Direction. The purge gap lies between the outer surface of the ring and the bore around it, and is said to open when the wall moves outward. An outer surface that moves outward moves towards the bore that bounds the gap. The text never says which contact closes the gap at rest, or why an outward movement breaks that contact instead of tightening it. (2) Load path. The description does not say which surface of the seat ring the purge shoulder bears on, in which axial direction the load acts, or what reacts it. [0113] puts the load at the upper edge, which is the sealing edge that the sealing face has just left; the figure instruction puts the purge shoulder at the fit-band end, which is the end pressed into the bore and seated on the locating shoulder, where a load passes into the core body without crossing the convoluted wall. (3) Sign of the deformation. [0134], [0170] and claim 22 have the axial load flatten the convolution; [0172] says that a single outward bow, which it equates with one convolution, increases under axial load. Claim 24 claims the single outward bow in dependence on claim 22, so one claim requires the bow to flatten while the description says it grows. (4) Inlet. Only the exit of the purge flow is described. With the outer surface of the ring pressed into the bore, no route is identified by which air at pump pressure enters the gap from upstream without passing the sealing edge. (5) As built. [0202] states that the fit band, the free portion and the core body bore relief are not the tested construction, so the construction that did run for forty cycles is not described in the one respect that matters. An examiner puts this as lack of enablement of the only tested embodiment and of claims 16 to 19, 45, 58 and 65; an opponent uses the contradiction as the verifiable fact that raises serious doubt.
- **Least narrowing action:** Add disclosure taken from the inventor's prototype and drawings: the surface of the seat ring on which the purge shoulder bears and the direction of the load; the support that reacts it; the contact that closes the purge gap at rest and how the movement of the wall opens it; the entry and the exit of the purge flow; and the retention actually used in the tested valve cores. Reconcile [0134], [0170] and [0172], and claim 24 with claim 22. No claim is narrowed. If the inventor's answer is that part of the wall moves inward to open the gap, claims 1 to 3 need the genus wording 'radial deflection', which [0014] already uses.
- **New disclosure required:** yes · **Suggested target:** P4

### F-32-3 [high] [novelty] Inventive step: the application's own inspection contradicts the effect attributed to the purge gap

- **Ground:** inventive step / non-obviousness
- **Claims:** 1, 2, 3, 38, 57
- **Paragraphs and figures:** [0008], [0009], [0010], [0011], [0013], [0140], [0282], [0287], [0289]
- **Text attacked:**
  - [0010]: "The technical problem addressed is the removal, on each inflation cycle and without a tool, of a sealant film that has already formed on surfaces of the valve core."
  - [0287]: "Cured sealant film was present on the outer surface of the seat ring 30 in the region of the purge gap 60."
  - [0287]: "The sealing edge 32 and the sealing face 42 of the poppet 40 were clean."
  - [0289]: "No valve core having the air purge alone or the snap-back wipe alone was tested"
  - US4044791A: "permitting fluid to flow past the protruding portion of the poppet and through secondary passageways 54"
  - US5924444A: "As the flow develops, the elastomeric ring 303 deforms radially outward thereby increasing the gap 314 available for fluid flow."
  - US3570510A: "is supplied to the valve seat of the valve device from a circular space having an annular slit so as to keep the valve seat clean"
- **Attack:** Closest art: the conventional tyre valve core of [0003], a spring-biased poppet on a seat in a threaded core body. Claim 1 differs by the over-travel in which a surface moved by the poppet deforms an element to open a second flow path. The effect relied on, at [0010] and [0013], is that air through the purge gap removes sealant film from the surfaces it sweeps. The inspection at [0287] found cured film on exactly those surfaces after forty cycles. The surfaces found clean, the sealing edge and the sealing face, bound the main passage, where by [0011] the ordinary stream of air already flows. The comparison cannot attribute the longer life to the purge gap either: the conventional valve cores are unidentified ([0282]), their crack pressure, seat material and seat geometry are not given, and [0289] concedes that the two cleaning actions were never separated. With no effect shown for the distinguishing feature, the problem falls to providing a second flow path that opens at a higher pressure differential. Staged opening by further poppet travel is acknowledged at [0008] and shown in US4044791A; a ring that deforms radially outward to open a gap and returns when the valve closes is acknowledged at [0009] and shown in US5924444A, a valve for mounting on a tyre valve stem; cleaning a valve seat with air through an annular slit is in US3570510A. No embedded reference deforms the ring by a surface moved by the poppet; the examiner will call that a mechanical alternative to deformation by flow. The objection is credible against claims 1, 2 and 3 and against claims 38 and 57, which recite the air purge itself.
- **Least narrowing action:** Add what the inventor actually recorded: the state of the same surfaces on the conventional valve cores at failure, the identity and crack pressure of those valve cores, and any observation that air passed through the purge gap. If the inventor confirms it, restate [0010] and [0013] so that the effect asserted is the one the inspection supports, namely that the surfaces on which the poppet seals stay free of sealant film. Add, without touching claim 1, an independent fallback to the tested combination (claim 1 with claims 20 and 27), so that one claim rests on the example as run. No narrowing of claim 1 is proposed.
- **New disclosure required:** yes · **Suggested target:** P4
