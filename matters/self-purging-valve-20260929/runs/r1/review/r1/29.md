## Findings

### Amendment Paths

Assumed rejection: claims 1, 2 and 3 fall for lack of novelty or inventive step against a document in which further poppet travel opens a further flow path (the kind described at [0008]) or in which an element deforms radially to open a gap (the kind described at [0009]). Each path below uses only features expressly disclosed in the package. Risk is stated for EPO added-matter and intermediate-generalisation practice (Art. 123(2) EPC) and applies equally to US written description on amendment (35 U.S.C. § 112(a)); the UK follows the EPO on added matter.

**Path 1. One part seals and opens the purge gap, by a convoluted wall.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the radially deflectable element is a seat ring retained in the core body, the sealing edge being an edge of the seat ring, and the seat ring has a convoluted wall having at least one convolution, the convoluted wall converting the axial load into the radial expansion".
Combination support: statements 1.1 → 1.6 → 1.7 → 1.8 ([0298], [0303]–[0305]); [0017], [0113], [0114]; FIGS. 1, 2, 4A, 4B.
Risk: none. The chain of statements is express. Adding claim 22's "flattens" would import the inconsistency of F-28-1.
Specification addition: none needed for basis; F-28-1's load-path paragraph is needed for the path to survive a sufficiency challenge.

**Path 2. The purge gap is an annular clearance between the seat ring and the core body bore.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the purge gap is an annular clearance bounded by an outer surface of the radially deflectable element and by a core body bore of the core body".
Combination support: statements 1.10, 1.11, 1.13 ([0307], [0308], [0310]); [0024], [0065], [0124].
Risk: none with the seat ring. Possible if the radially deflectable element is left generic: [0124] describes the annular clearance for the seat ring 30 only, and nothing describes it for the slotted sleeve, split seat ring, tapered seat ring or wiper ring (F-28-3, F-28-4).
Specification addition: one sentence that the annular purge gap bounded by the core body bore applies to each form of the radially deflectable element that has an outer surface facing that bore (F-29-1).

**Path 3. Two-rate return bias with a purge shoulder on the poppet.**
Amended claim 1 adds claims 5, 6 and 13: crack pressure and over-travel differential, "a return bias ... that has a two-rate characteristic ... a first spring stage ... and a second spring stage", and "the load-applying surface is a purge shoulder formed on the poppet".
Combination support: statements 1.3, 1.4, 1.16 ([0300], [0301], [0313]); [0023], [0026], [0119]–[0122], [0142]; FIG. 5.
Risk: none. [0142] applies every return-bias construction to the purge shoulder arrangement, and statement 1.16 depends on any of statements 1.1 to 1.15.
Specification addition: none for basis. The detent of [0148] is a step in force, not a rate change, so it falls outside "two-rate characteristic"; if the path is to cover it, "return bias having a two-rate characteristic or a detent" needs a sentence stating that the detent is an alternative to the two-rate characteristic in every combination in which the two-rate characteristic is described (F-29-2).

**Path 4. Superelastic NiTi seat ring with austenite finish below 5 °C.**
Amended claim 1 adds claims 20, 27, 28 and 29.
Combination support: statements 1.6, 1.17–1.19 ([0303], [0314]–[0316]); [0028], [0203].
Risk: none. [0028] and [0203] give NiTi, superelastic and Af below 5 °C together.
Specification addition: none.

**Path 5. Fit band and free portion expanding into a core body bore relief.**
Amended claim 1 adds claims 20, 25 and 26: interference fit on a fit band next to the locating shoulder, the free portion expanding into a core body bore relief.
Combination support: [0186]–[0188]; FIGS. 4A, 4B, 12A. No numbered statement covers it.
Risk: possible. [0186] ties the fit band to the interference fit and to the locating shoulder ("a short band of the outer surface next to the locating shoulder 24"); taking the relief without the fit band, or the fit band without the locating shoulder, is an intermediate generalisation. The FIG. 8 instructions say the fit band distinction is not drawn for the polymer seat ring, so the path with the polymer seat ring (claims 30, 31) has no combination support. FIG. 12A is captioned as a candidate (F-28-2).
Specification addition: a numbered statement combining the seat ring, the interference fit on a fit band, the free portion and the core body bore relief, and a sentence stating whether the fit band and free portion apply to the polymer seat ring 130 (F-29-3).

**Path 6. Purge flow through wall slots.**
Amended claim 1 adds claim 44: "a seat ring having a convoluted wall, and the purge gap comprises wall slots through the convoluted wall, the wall slots being closed at rest and open on the radial expansion".
Combination support: [0025], [0190], [0191], statement 1.14 ([0311]); FIG. 12B.
Risk: possible. [0190] places the slots "in the free portion 37, below the sealing edge 32" and gives two specific closing mechanisms ("either because they lie against the bore ... or because their edges abut"); claim 44 omits both. An examiner may hold the unlocated slot an intermediate generalisation. Statement 1.14 ("a flow path through a wall of the seat ring") is broad enough to support the claim as worded, which reduces the risk.
Specification addition: a sentence stating that the wall slots may be anywhere in the convoluted wall below the sealing edge and closed at rest by any means, if the inventor so conceived (F-29-4).

**Path 7. Numerical ranges of the working geometry.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the over-travel has an over-travel distance of from 0.4 mm to 0.9 mm and the purge gap has a purge gap width of from 0.10 mm to 0.25 mm" (or a narrower fallback from [0245], [0246]).
Combination support: [0236], [0237], [0245], [0246], [0249], statement 1.23 ([0320]).
Risk: likely without the seat ring; none with it. Statement 1.23 depends only from statements 1.6 onwards (seat ring), and [0236], [0237] define both ranges for the seat ring 30 and the convolution 36 (F-28-7). Taking the ranges into generic claim 1 is an intermediate generalisation.
Specification addition: the sentence recommended at F-28-7; no separate finding here.

**Path 8. Over-travel distance set by a separate collar.**
Amended claim 1 adds claim 15 and: "the position of the collar on the poppet setting the over-travel distance".
Combination support: [0103], [0153].
Risk: none. [0153] states the collar, its fixing to the poppet below the sealing face, and that its position sets the over-travel distance, in one passage. Pressing, crimping or threading ([0153]) need not be added.
Specification addition: none.

**Path 9. Method, pump-driven, in place, with sealant.**
Amended claim 2 adds claims 51, 52, 57 and 59: poppet moved by a pressure differential above the crack pressure and then above the over-travel differential, applied by a pump head coupled to the tyre valve, the tyre chamber containing sealant and the air purge flowing across a surface on which sealant film is present, carried out with the valve core in place in the valve stem.
Combination support: statements 3.1, 3.2, 3.4 ([0329], [0330], [0332]); [0021], [0270]–[0273]; FIG. 6.
Risk: none. Adding "floor pump" (statement 3.5, [0333]) is also supported. Adding the pressure ranges of claim 53 is supported through statement 3.1's reference to statement 1.23 only with the seat ring: possible otherwise (as Path 7).
Specification addition: none beyond F-28-7.

**Path 10. Seat ring claim restricted to the tested seat ring.**
Amended claim 3 adds claims 62, 65, 70, 71 and 72: a convoluted wall having at least one convolution, the purge gap bounded by the outer surface of the seat ring and a surrounding bore, of superelastic NiTi alloy with Af below 5 °C; optionally the prototype dimensions of claim 69.
Combination support: statements 4.1–4.3 and 4.5 ([0334]–[0336], [0338]); [0203], [0231]–[0234], [0280].
Risk: none for the convoluted wall, bore boundary and material. Possible for the prototype dimensions: Example 1 ([0279], [0280]) does not state the number of convolutions of the tested seat ring, nor the construction of its two-rate return spring, nor whether the tested purge gap was bounded by the core body bore or a relief. A fallback to "the seat ring as tested" cannot be combined with one convolution, two convolutions, a variable-pitch spring or a plain core body bore without an intermediate generalisation, because the description does not say which of these the tested cores had.
Specification addition: state in Example 1 the number of convolutions, the return-spring construction and the purge-gap boundary of prototypes P-1 to P-3 (F-29-5).

Unused basis. Statements 2.4 (Presta pattern), 2.5–2.6 (tyre chamber containing sealant; inner tube or tubeless cavity), 4.6 (replacement seat ring), 5.6 (seat ring obtainable by the method) and 6.1 (replacement valve core) are disclosed as numbered combinations but not claimed. They are basis for later claims or divisionals. Statement 7.1 (two-piece seat) is a separate independent combination that does not recite a purge gap; it is basis for a divisional directed to the two-piece seat, subject to F-28-4.

### F-29-1 [low] [support] The annular purge gap bounded by the core body bore is described for the seat ring only

Claims 16, 17 and 18 (Path 2): "the purge gap is bounded by an outer surface of the radially deflectable element and by a bore surrounding the radially deflectable element"; "the bore is a core body bore (22)"; "the purge gap is annular".

Attack. [0124] and [0185] describe the annular clearance for the seat ring 30. Statements 1.10–1.13 depend from statement 1.1 (generic), which gives literal basis, but no passage describes the clearance for the slotted sleeve, the split seat ring, the tapered seat ring or the wiper ring. An examiner testing Path 2 on a generic element will ask where the combination is shown.

Least narrowing action. Add one sentence that, for each form of the radially deflectable element whose outer surface faces the core body bore, the purge gap may be an annular clearance between that outer surface and the core body bore. Requires new disclosure (inventor to confirm); target P4.

### F-29-2 [medium] [support] The detent is not disclosed in combination with the other features as an alternative to the two-rate characteristic

Claim 6: "a return bias ... that has a two-rate characteristic"; [0148]: "the crack pressure 110 and the over-travel differential 112 are separated by a step in force in place of a change of rate".

Attack. [0148] is the only passage on the detent. Every numbered statement and every combination under "Combinations" uses the two-rate characteristic. A competitor using a spring-loaded ball or magnet detent is outside claim 6 and all its dependants; an amendment of claim 1 to "a return bias having a two-rate characteristic or a detent" would draw an intermediate-generalisation objection, because the detent is disclosed only once and not with the pressure ranges, the purge shoulder or the seat ring.

Least narrowing action. Add a numbered statement: the valve core of statement 1.3, further comprising a detent that holds the poppet at the end of the normal stroke until the over-travel differential is exceeded, and a sentence that the detent may replace the two-rate characteristic in each combination and with the ranges of [0238] and [0239]. Requires new disclosure (restatement of [0148]); target P4.

### F-29-3 [low] [support] The fit band and relief have no numbered combination, and none with the polymer seat ring

Claims 25, 26 and 66 (Path 5): "the interference fit acts on a fit band (35) of the seat ring, and the seat ring has a free portion (37) which, on the radial expansion, expands into a core body bore relief (202)".

Attack. [0186] ties the fit band to the interference fit and the locating shoulder, and FIG. 12A is the only drawing of the relief. No statement combines them with the seat ring, and the FIG. 8 instructions exclude the fit band distinction from the polymer seat ring. An amendment taking the relief alone, or combining it with claims 30–31, lacks basis.

Least narrowing action. Add a numbered statement combining the seat ring, the interference fit on a fit band next to the locating shoulder, the free portion and the core body bore relief, and a sentence stating whether this applies to the polymer seat ring 130. Requires new disclosure; target P4.

### F-29-4 [low] [support] Wall slots: location and closing mechanism narrower in [0190] than in the claims

Claims 44 and 67 (Path 6): "wall slots (204) through the convoluted wall, the wall slots being closed at rest and open on the radial expansion".

Attack. [0190] places the slots "in the free portion 37, below the sealing edge 32" and closes them "because they lie against the bore surrounding the seat ring 30 or because their edges abut". Statement 1.14 gives general basis; the only drawing (FIG. 12B) shows one arrangement and is captioned as a candidate (F-28-2). An examiner may treat the unlocated slot as an intermediate generalisation of [0190].

Least narrowing action. Add a sentence that the wall slots may be at any position in the convoluted wall that lies outside the main passage, and closed at rest by any of the means described, if the inventor so conceived. Requires new disclosure; target P4.

### F-29-5 [medium] [support] Example 1 does not state the convolution count, return-spring construction or purge-gap boundary of the tested cores

[0279]: "The return spring 50 was of stainless music wire and had a two-rate characteristic. The seat ring 30 ... had a convoluted wall 34."

Attack. The tested embodiment is the strongest fallback because it carries the data of [0285]–[0288]. But Example 1 does not say whether the seat rings had one or two convolutions, how the two-rate characteristic was produced (variable pitch, [0143], is only "by way of example" in FIGS. 1 and 2), or whether the purge gap was bounded by a plain core body bore or a relief ([0202] says only that the relief is not stated to be the tested construction). Claims 12, 34 and 69 recite prototype values, and a fallback to "the valve core as tested" cannot be combined with any of these features without an intermediate generalisation. An examiner will also ask whether the data support one or two convolutions.

Least narrowing action. Add to Example 1 the number of convolutions, the return-spring construction and the purge-gap boundary of P-1 to P-3, from the inventor's records. Requires new disclosure; target P4.
