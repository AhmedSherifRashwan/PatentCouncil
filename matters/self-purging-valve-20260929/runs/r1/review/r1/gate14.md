### F-gate14-1 [medium] [formal] The package has no abstract

The package lists description, claims, figures and registry only; `README.md` of the package names no abstract, and no abstract text appears in any file. An abstract is a filing requirement in all three target offices (37 CFR 1.72(b); Art. 78(1)(e) and Rule 47 EPC; section 14(2)(d) Patents Act 1977), and question 12 cannot be answered on an absent text. The attack is procedural, not substantive: the EPO and UKIPO will issue a formalities invitation, and the USPTO a notice to file missing parts; a filing date is not lost, but the abstract then drafted outside the review loop is unreviewed text. Least narrowing action: draft an abstract of not more than 150 words summarising claims 1, 2 and 3 in the locked terms, with a reference sign after each principal feature and no "means for" language, and run it through this gate's question 12 before Gate 3. No new disclosure is required.

### F-gate14-2 [medium] [formal] No drawing sheets exist; only figure instructions

`figures.md` contains a figure plan and figure instructions ("Figure Instructions … line drawings in patent-drawing style"), not drawings. The description relies on FIGS. 1–13B ([0036]–[0049]) throughout. Drawings filed later than the description risk being treated as late-filed missing parts (Rule 56 EPC; section 15(5) Patents Act 1977), and in the US drawings added after filing are new matter if they show anything the text does not. Drawing formalities (Rule 46 EPC; 37 CFR 1.84; rule 14, Patents Rules 2007) cannot be checked. Least narrowing action: render drawing sheets from the figure instructions, check every numeral against `registry.md`, and file them with the description; strip from the captions the words that describe the constructions as candidates, unconfirmed or "shown for disclosure of the genus only" once the inventor has decided on them (see F-32-4). No new disclosure is required.
