### F-40a-1 [medium] [formal] Multiple dependent claims refer to antecedents conjunctively instead of in the alternative

**Claim language attacked:** claim 13 (representative), "The valve core of any one of claims 1 and 5 to 12, ..."; the identical pattern recurs in 30 further claims listed in the table above.

**Attack.** 37 CFR 1.75(c) and MPEP 608.01(n) require a multiple dependent claim to refer to the claims it incorporates in the alternative only. Joining a single claim number to a following range with "and" states the reference conjunctively on its face, which an examiner may object to as an improper multiple dependent claim requiring correction, or may misconstrue as intending dependency on both the single claim and the whole range simultaneously. This is a mechanical formatting defect, not a substantive one (the substance is fully covered under Prompt 37's forensic audit, which this reply cross-references rather than repeating, to avoid duplicating 31 identical correction rows).

**Least narrowing action.** Replace "and" with a comma throughout, e.g. "any one of claims 1, 5 to 12"; no scope change. The complete per-claim correction list is given in `37.md`.

**Category / severity.** `formal`; `medium` — recurs across roughly a third of the claim set and would draw a first-action objection if unaddressed.

**Requires new disclosure:** No. **Suggested target:** `in-place`.

### F-40a-2 [medium] [formal] Brief description of the drawings omits FIG. 13A, which is registered in the numeral registry

**Paragraph attacked:** [0049], the last entry in the Brief Description of the Drawings, jumps from FIGS. 12A/12B to "FIG. 13B" with no entry for FIG. 13A.

**Attack.** 37 CFR 1.74 and MPEP 608.01(f) require a brief description of every figure of the drawings. The reference-numeral registry supplied with this package registers numeral 220 as first appearing in FIG. 13A, and the figure-instructions document describes FIG. 13A in detail as a Schrader-pattern candidate core body; neither the figure nor the numeral appears anywhere in the Brief Description or the Detailed Description. This is the same underlying inconsistency identified in the Prompt 38 specification-consistency audit (`38.md`, F-38-3), and is raised here again because it is also, independently, a formal drawing-description-completeness defect under the requirement this prompt specifically checks.

**Least narrowing action.** Add a Brief Description sentence for FIG. 13A and a short corresponding Detailed Description passage, or remove FIG. 13A and numeral 220 from the figure plan and registry if the Schrader-pattern candidate is not to be filed. No claim is affected either way, since numeral 220 does not appear in any claim.

**Category / severity.** `formal`; `medium`.

**Requires new disclosure:** Only if FIG. 13A is retained. **Suggested target:** `P4`.
