### F-30-1 [high] [enablement] Sufficiency in all three jurisdictions: the text does not say how the tested valve core opens its purge gap

- **Claims:** 1, 2, 3, 16, 17, 18, 19, 22, 24, 45, 58, 65
- **Paragraphs and figures:** [0081], [0112], [0113], [0124], [0134], [0172], [0194], [0202], FIG. 2, FIG. 4A
- **Text attacked:**
  - claim 16: "wherein the purge gap is bounded by an outer surface of the radially deflectable element and by a bore surrounding the radially deflectable element"
  - [0081]: "means an outward movement of the wall of the seat ring"
  - [0113]: "an axial load applied to the seat ring 30, for example at its upper edge"
  - FIG. 2 instruction: "the purge shoulder (44) touching the seat ring (30) at the fit-band end of the convoluted wall (34)"
  - [0134]: "the convolution 36 flattens under the axial load"
  - [0172]: "The bow increases under axial load in the manner of a pre-buckled column."
  - [0202]: "They are not stated to be the construction of the valve cores tested"
- **Attack:** Issue for counsel under 35 U.S.C. 112(a), Art. 83 EPC and s.14(3) of the UK Act. The gap lies between the outer surface of the ring and the bore around it and is said to open when the wall moves outward, that is, towards that bore. The description does not say which surface of the ring the purge shoulder bears on, in which direction the load acts or what reacts it, and the two places that touch the point disagree: [0113] names the upper edge, the figure instruction names the fit-band end. [0134] has the load flatten the convolution; [0172] has the equivalent bow increase under load; claim 24 depends on claim 22. At the EPO and in the UK an opponent needs verifiable facts to raise serious doubt, and the contradictions supply them. In the US the full scope of claims 16 to 19 rests on the one embodiment the text leaves unexplained.
- **Least narrowing action:** Add the inventor's own account of the prototype before filing, when new matter is not yet a constraint: where the purge shoulder bears, the direction of the load and its reaction, the contact that closes the gap at rest, how the wall's movement opens it, where the purge flow enters and leaves, and how the tested rings were retained. ## Assumptions

A1. The package is the whole of the text to be filed. The figure plan and the figure instructions are instructions to the draftsperson; the captions they prescribe would appear on the drawings.
