### F-29-1 [high] [support] The two-piece seat cannot be used as a fallback because its purge gap is not described

Paragraphs cited: [0181]–[0183], [0293]–[0295], Statement 7.1 at [0347]; path 6.

Attack. An amendment of claim 1 to the rigid seat and wiper ring would have to state that the deflection of the wiper ring opens the purge gap. The description says only that the wiper ring is deflected outward and wipes. An EPO examiner would refuse the amendment under Article 123(2) as a combination not directly and unambiguously disclosed; in the United States the combination would lack written description. The two-piece seat is also the cheapest construction for a competitor who wishes to keep a conventional rigid seat, so the fallback that is missing is the one most needed. This has the same root as F-28-3.

Least narrowing action. Add the purge gap 146 to the description of the two-piece seat and to Statement 7.1: its boundaries, its closed state at rest, its relation to the main passage and the path of the air purge.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-29-2 [medium] [support] The fit band and free portion are disclosed only with the relief, and the tested construction is not identified

Paragraphs cited: [0186], [0202], [0229]; claim 66, "a fit band (35), for retention of the seat ring by an interference fit, and a free portion (37) that undergoes the radial expansion"; paths 1 and 4.

Attack. [0186] introduces the fit band, the free portion and the relief in one paragraph, and [0229] combines the first two with the relief or with clamping or staking. A claim to the fit band and free portion against a plain bore is an intermediate generalisation. [0202] disclaims the relief for the tested valve cores without saying what they had.

Least narrowing action. State whether the fit band and free portion are used with a plain core body bore, and state the construction of the tested valve cores.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-29-3 [medium] [support] The wall-slot and valve-stem-bore arrangements are each too thinly described to carry an amendment

Paragraphs cited: [0189], [0190], [0191], [0229]; claims 44, 45 and 47; paths 7 and 8.

Attack. The wall slots are placed in a free portion in one paragraph and combined with a fully retained outer surface in the next. The valve-stem-bore arrangement is one sentence, without a figure, and the tyre-valve claim excludes it.

Least narrowing action. Describe the two wall-slot arrangements separately; add a figure and paragraph for the valve-stem-bore arrangement and extend the dependency of claim 47 to claim 45.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-29-4 [medium] [support] Parameters with a single value give no room to amend

Paragraphs cited: [0235] (normal stroke, "no range is stated"), [0250] (interference fit, composition, shape-setting temperature and time), [0203] (austenite finish temperature); claims 29, 34, 73, 81, 85; paths 5 and 10.

Attack. If a point-value claim is anticipated or found obvious, there is no range to fall back to, and if a range claim is too broad there is no relation between parameters to narrow by. [0237] and [0234] imply a fixed relation between purge gap width and convolution amplitude that is never stated.

Least narrowing action. Ask the inventor for the ranges believed workable for the normal stroke, the interference fit and the shape-setting conditions, for the method of determining the austenite finish temperature, and for any relation between dimensions relied on; add them as candidates.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-29-5 [high] [design-around] Disclosed alternatives that are outside every claim, one of them by the description's own statement

Paragraphs cited: [0148], [0151], [0155], [0179], [0180], [0216]; claim 1, "a radially deflectable element" and "a radial expansion".

Attack. [0179] describes a disc spring whose rim lifts from a face to open a purge gap and says "its deflection is not a radial expansion". That is a working instruction for avoiding claim 1, written by the applicant. The detent and the spring-loaded stop are outside claim 6. None has a claim or a claim-support statement. In the United States, subject matter disclosed and not claimed is dedicated to the public and cannot be recovered by equivalents.

Least narrowing action. Add an independent or generic claim to a deflectable element whose deflection opens the purge gap, with claim-support statements and a figure for [0179]–[0180], and the generic term of path 2. This broadens; nothing is given up.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P5.

### F-29-6 [medium] [support] The combination clause is closed

Paragraphs cited: [0035], [0217], [0349].

Attack. "in the combinations set out under 'Combinations'" limits every later amendment to the listed combinations. The slotted sleeve, split seat ring, tapered seat ring, collar, detent and wall slots are not among the forms listed in [0217].

Least narrowing action. Replace with an open statement and extend [0217] to every form of the radially deflectable element, load-applying surface and return bias.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.
