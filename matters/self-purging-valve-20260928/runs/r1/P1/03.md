### Inventive Nucleus

This is a drafting and analysis aid prepared by AI. It is not legal advice or a filing decision, and it makes no patentability judgement; qualified patent counsel signs off before anything is filed. The nucleus and every classification below are `candidate` until Human Gate 1. No prior art has been considered here, so "minimum combination" means the smallest combination the disclosure says produces the effect. It does not mean the smallest novel one; that is Phase 2's question.

**N0 — nucleus.** A valve for an inflatable body containing sealant, in which:

- a valve member makes a normal stroke away from a valve seat when a pressure differential from an inflation device exceeds the opening pressure;
- the valve member moves beyond that normal stroke (over-travel) when the pressure differential exceeds a higher over-travel threshold;
- the over-travel deflects a deflectable element, opening a purge gap that is closed at rest; purge flow of inflation gas then passes through the purge gap across the sealant-accumulation surfaces and into the inflatable body;
- when the pressure differential falls, the deflectable element recovers and the purge gap closes.

Sources: §2 para 1–2; §3 items 3–6; §6 alternatives A and B; 01 (b), (d) S1–S5.

Why this is the minimum. The inventor names two cleaning mechanisms (§2 para 2). Both depend on the same underlying action: pressure-driven over-travel deflecting a separate, resilient element. Purge flow is the mechanism the inventor states first and names the device after ("self-purging"). §6 shows that neither the NiTi material (alternative A) nor the one-piece seat (alternative B) is needed to produce it. Removing any clause of N0 removes the mechanism, so each clause is essential.

Alternative nucleus to keep open (N0-W). The recovery wipe could equally be the minimum: over-travel deflects a deflectable element, and on recovery the element slides across the sealing face. This candidate has no purge flow. The bench data cannot tell N0 from N0-W. The only evidence is that film remained in the purge-gap region while the sealing edge and poppet face were clean (§8). Which nucleus is right, or whether both are needed together, is Q1. Until it is answered, the ladder below carries the wipe as the first narrowing rung, so that either answer leaves a supported fallback.

**Ladder of progressively narrower versions** (each rung adds to the one above):

- **N1** = N0 + recovery wipe: on recovery, the deflectable element slides across the sealing face of the valve member, shearing off partially cured sealant film (§2 para 2).
- **N2** = N1 + the over-travel threshold is set by a biasing member with a two-rate characteristic: a first stage gives the opening pressure and a stiffer second stage must be overcome to reach over-travel (§3 item 5; §4).
- **N3** = N2 + a purge shoulder on the valve member, below the sealing face, contacts the deflectable element only during over-travel (§3 item 4).
- **N4** = N3 + the deflectable element is also the valve seat: a seat ring whose upper edge receives the sealing face. It is held in the core bore by an interference fit and located against a locating shoulder (§3 items 3–4; §7).
  - **N4-B** (parallel branch, alternative B): instead of N4, a rigid valve seat plus a separate wiper ring below it, which the purge shoulder deflects outward (§6 para 2). Not built.
- **N5** = N4 + the seat ring is tubular with a convoluted wall (one or two shallow convolutions) that converts axial load on its upper edge into radial expansion (§3 item 3).
- **N6** = N5 + the seat ring is a superelastic alloy, specifically NiTi, with Af below 5 °C (§2 para 2; §4; §5).
  - **N6-A** (parallel branch, alternative A): instead of N6, a polymer (PEEK) seat ring with the same convoluted wall (§6 para 1). Not built; weaker recovery wipe stated.
- **N7** = N6 + a valve core that screws into a valve stem, with core body, valve member, biasing member and seat ring as one replaceable unit (§3 items 1–2; 01 A4).
- **N8** = N7 + prototype dimensions and pressures within the §4 ranges: seat ring inner diameter 2.0–2.6 mm; wall thickness 0.15–0.30 mm; free length 2.5–4.0 mm; convolution amplitude 0.08–0.20 mm; over-travel 0.4–0.9 mm; purge gap 0.10–0.25 mm; opening pressure 0.8–1.5 bar; over-travel threshold 2.5–5.0 bar. These are estimates, not tested limits (01 A5).
- **N9** = N8 + manufacturing route: laser cutting from NiTi tube, shape-setting at 500 °C for 10 min in a fixture, water quench, electropolishing, press fit ≈0.03 mm (§7).
- **N10** = N9 + prototype materials and details: NiTi 55.8 wt% Ni; brass CW614N or stainless 303 core body; brass valve member; lock nut; Presta-pattern valve stem of 6 mm OD (§3, §5).

Rung order reflects fallback value, not the order of assembly. If Gate 1 shows the purge flow alone gives the effect, N1 can move lower. If it shows the wipe alone does, N0-W replaces N0 and purge flow becomes a narrowing rung.

Enablement flag on N0 itself: the disclosure states that the seat ring "flexes radially outward and opens a secondary annular gap between the ring and the [core/stem] bore" (§2; §3 item 6). It also states that the ring is pressed into that bore with an interference fit (§3 item 3; §7). As written, outward expansion against a surrounding bore closes a gap rather than opening one. Until the inventor explains the geometry (01 Q1, Q2), every rung that relies on the purge gap carries an enablement risk.

## Rationale

I set the nucleus at the mechanism level: over-travel deflecting a separate resilient element to open a closed purge gap. I did not set it at the NiTi seat ring. The inventor's own alternatives (§6 A and B) show that both the material and the one-piece construction can be changed, so tying the nucleus to either would make it narrower than the conception.

The hardest call is purge flow versus the recovery wipe. The inventor presents both, the device's name points to purge flow, and the inspection evidence (film left in the purge-gap region, clean sealing surfaces) is at least as consistent with the wipe. I kept purge flow in N0, stated N0-W as a live alternative, and placed the wipe as the first rung. That way the ladder survives either answer at Gate 1.

The two-rate characteristic is classified provisionally advantageous rather than essential. The concept needs some over-travel threshold distinct from the opening pressure, but the disclosure does not say that a two-rate biasing member is the only way to set one. The enablement risk is flagged instead.

The interference fit is classified conventional but flagged. Physically it sits right at the unexplained purge-gap mechanism, and it may turn out to be part of how the purge gap is sealed at rest.

## Assumptions

- A1 N0 includes purge flow rather than only the recovery wipe. Rests on §2 para 1 (stated first) and the title term "self-purging". Blocking: yes, for P3 and P5, until Q1 is answered.
- A2 A distinct over-travel threshold, above the opening pressure, is needed for the effect; a two-rate biasing member is one way to provide it. Rests on §3 item 5 and §4. Blocking: no; revisit after 01 Q6.
- A3 The purge shoulder is one means of deflection among possibly several; alternative B also uses it. Rests on §3 item 4 and §6 para 2. Not blocking.
- A4 The rung order (wipe first) is chosen for fallback value. It does not state the inventor's view of importance. Not blocking.
- A5 The seat ring's interference fit is conventional retention and not part of the purge mechanism. Rests on §3 item 3 ("light interference fit"). Blocking: yes, for P4, until 01 Q1 and Q2 are answered.

## Open Questions

- Q1 Which cleaning mechanism does the job: purge flow, the recovery wipe, or both together? Can the inventor say from experience, or run a test that separates them (for example, blocking the purge gap)? Decides between N0 and N0-W. Blocks P3, P5.
- Q2 Is the interference fit, or any contact between the seat ring's outer surface and the core bore, what keeps the purge gap closed at rest? Same geometry question as 01 Q1. Blocks P3, P4, P5.
- Q3 Can the over-travel threshold be set by anything other than a two-rate biasing member (for example, the stiffness of the deflectable element itself)? The inventor should say only what they have conceived. Blocks P3, P5.
- Q4 Does the effect need the deflection to be radial, or would any deflection that opens the purge gap work? Blocks P3, P5.
- Q5 Is electropolishing functionally important (sealing, wipe, fatigue)? Blocks P4.
- Q6 Does the inventor regard the thermal variant as part of this invention, or as a separate idea? Blocks P3, P4.
