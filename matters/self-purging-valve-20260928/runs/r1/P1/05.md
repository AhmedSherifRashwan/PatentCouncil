### Inventor Interview Questionnaire

This questionnaire is a drafting and analysis aid prepared by AI. It is not legal advice or a filing decision; qualified patent counsel signs off before anything is filed. Nothing in it is confirmed: it asks, and the inventor answers at Human Gate 1 in `gate-1-answers.md`.

How to answer. Answer only from what you built, measured or have actually thought of. "Don't know" and "not conceived" are useful answers and are recorded as such. Where a question lists options, the options are prompts to help you answer, not proposals: pick one only if it matches what you built or conceived. A sketch answers several questions at once (see Q56).

Key. Each question ends with why it matters and, in brackets, the phases it blocks until answered (P2 prior art, P3 embodiments, P4 description, P5 claims, PACKAGE filing package). Sources: `intake/disclosure.md` (§1–§9), `intake/data/bench-test.md` ("bench data"), and the Phase 1 outputs 01, 02, 03 and 04. Terms are the preferred terms of the 04 table. The questions are ordered within each theme, and the themes overall, by how much the answer could change claim scope or fallback positions. The ten that matter most are also in `top10-questions.md` as a short multiple-choice form.

#### A. How the purge gap opens and closes

- **Q1** §2 and §3 say the seat ring flexes radially outward and opens the purge gap against a bore. §3 item 3 and §7 say the seat ring is pressed into the core bore with an interference fit. As written, outward movement against a surrounding bore would close a gap, not open one. Please explain which portion of the seat ring is held by the interference fit, which portion is free to move, and which two surfaces move apart to form the purge gap. — Closes the main enablement gap on the 03 nucleus (N0); resolves 01 M1. [P3, P4, P5]
- **Q2** Is the purge gap formed against the core bore of the core body (§3 item 6) or against the bore of the valve stem (§2)? — Resolves 01 M1 and 04 Q2; decides the "core bore" row of the terminology table. [P4, P5]
- **Q3** Trace the purge flow: where does it enter the purge gap, where does it leave, and does it pass over the valve seat, go round it, or run beside the main flow passage? Which surfaces does it sweep? — Closes an enablement gap and defines "sealant-accumulation surfaces"; resolves 01 M2. [P3, P4, P5]
- **Q4** In the rest state, what keeps the purge gap gas-tight: contact between the seat ring's outer surface and the core bore, the interference fit itself, the valve seat alone, or something else? — Decides whether the interference fit is conventional retention (03 A5) or part of the mechanism; resolves 01 M10 and 03 Q2. [P3, P4, P5]
- **Q5** Must the deflection be radial? Have you conceived a deflectable element whose deflection opens the purge gap in another way (for example by moving axially, tilting, or lifting from the locating shoulder)? — Broadens scope beyond radial expansion; resolves 03 Q4. [P3, P5]
- **Q6** Was the 0.15 mm fully-open purge gap width measured or calculated? Does the purge gap open progressively with over-travel, or all at once at a point in the over-travel? — Closes an enablement gap and supports the purge gap width range. [P4]

#### B. Which cleaning mechanism does the work

- **Q7** Which of the two mechanisms keeps the valve working: purge flow, the recovery wipe, or both together? Answer from experience, or say whether a test could separate them (for example a unit with the purge gap blocked, or one in which the deflectable element cannot slide across the sealing face). — Decides between the 03 nucleus N0 (purge flow) and N0-W (recovery wipe); settles whether purge flow stays "essential (provisional)" and the recovery wipe "advantageous (provisional)"; resolves 01 M8 and 03 Q1. [P3, P5]
- **Q8** In the recovery wipe, which surface of the deflectable element touches which surface of the valve member, in which direction does it slide, and does it happen on every inflation cycle? — Closes an enablement gap for the recovery wipe; resolves 01 M7. [P3, P4, P5]
- **Q9** The bench notes record cured film on the seat ring's outer surface in the purge-gap region at cycle 40. Do you expect that film to keep growing, and could it eventually stop the purge gap closing or opening? — Identifies a failure mode and bounds the technical-effect statement. [P4]
- **Q10** The bench notes record an audible snap on pump-head removal. Is that sound the recovery wipe? Do you see it as useful feedback to the rider that a purge has happened? — May provide a fallback feature (an unexpected advantage not yet stated). [P4]

#### C. Over-travel, thresholds and the biasing member

- **Q11** When does over-travel happen during one inflation: only when the pump head is engaged, on every pump stroke, throughout inflation, or only at the end? — Defines the sequence of operation; resolves 01 M5 and 01 A3. [P4, P5]
- **Q12** Across which two pressures is the pressure differential measured: pump-side pressure against inflatable-body pressure, or pump-side pressure against atmosphere? As the inflatable body approaches 6 bar, does the pressure differential still reach the over-travel threshold? — Needed to define the over-travel threshold; resolves 01 M5 and 04 Q3 / 04 A2. [P4, P5]
- **Q13** How is the two-rate characteristic produced on the prototype: two springs, one spring with varying pitch, one spring plus a stop, the deflectable element's own stiffness acting as the second stage, or something else? — Closes an enablement gap; resolves 01 M6. [P3, P4, P5]
- **Q14** Have you conceived any way other than a two-rate biasing member to set the over-travel threshold apart from the opening pressure (for example the stiffness of the deflectable element alone, a detent, or a second spring that acts only during over-travel)? — Tests the "advantageous (provisional)" classification of the two-rate characteristic; could broaden scope; resolves 03 Q3. [P3, P5]
- **Q15** What limits over-travel: a stop, the deflectable element, the biasing member closing up, or nothing? What happens when the pressure differential is far above the over-travel threshold (for example a compressor)? — Identifies an edge case and a possible fallback feature. [P4]
- **Q16** §4 gives the normal stroke as 0.6 mm with no range. What range would work? — Provides a fallback range. [P4]
- **Q17** Does manual deflation through the valve (pressing the valve member) cause over-travel or purge flow? If it does, is that useful as a manual purge? — Resolves 01 M11; may add an embodiment you have conceived. [P4]
- **Q18** Does closing the lock nut prevent over-travel? — Resolves 01 M22. [P4]
- **Q19** Which inflation devices reach the over-travel threshold: floor pumps, hand or mini pumps, compressors, CO2 cartridge inflators? What is the basis for the statement in §2 about floor pumps? — Tests an unevidenced generalisation (01 A6); sets the scope of the inflation device. [P2, P4]

#### D. The deflectable element: form, materials and alternatives

- **Q20** Is the seat ring's wall continuous or slotted? What does the laser cutting produce (the outline, slots, or the wave itself)? Is the convolution formed by shape-setting on the mandrel? — Closes an enablement gap for making the seat ring; resolves 01 M3. [P3, P4, P5]
- **Q21** Does the prototype seat ring have one convolution or two? Do the convolutions bulge inward or outward? Would three or more work? Have you conceived any other wall profile (for example tapered or stepped) that turns axial load into radial expansion? — Broadens the convoluted wall and tests its "advantageous (provisional)" classification; resolves 01 M4. [P4]
- **Q22** Is alternative B (rigid valve seat plus a separate wiper ring) a real product option you want covered? If so: the wiper ring's shape, thickness and material, how it is held, where the purge gap forms, how purge flow passes it, and which surface it wipes. — Provides design-around cover for the one-piece seat ring; resolves 01 M13 and 02 Q2. [P3, P4]
- **Q23** Have you conceived materials for the deflectable element other than NiTi and PEEK (for example other superelastic alloys, spring steels, other polymers)? For each, why would it work or not? — Broadens scope beyond NiTi; resolves 01 M13. [P3, P4]
- **Q24** For alternative A (polymer seat ring), would purge flow still occur as with NiTi? How much weaker do you expect the recovery wipe to be? Any polymer other than PEEK? — Supports the polymer fallback. [P4]
- **Q25** Is the 55.8 wt% Ni composition critical? Are other Ni contents or ternary NiTi alloys contemplated? — Provides a fallback composition range. [P4]
- **Q26** What Af and martensite start temperature did the prototype rings actually have? What riding-temperature range (lowest and highest) must the valve work over? — Supports the Af limit; resolves 01 M20. [P4]
- **Q27** Is the thermal purge variant part of this invention, a separate idea, or to be dropped? If kept: how does warming open the purge gap without over-travel, and what is its structure? — Decides whether it is described; resolves 01 M14, 02 Q3 and 03 Q6. [P3, P4]
- **Q28** Have you conceived more than one deflectable element in a valve, or a deflectable element carried on the valve member rather than in the core body? Answer only if you have. — May broaden the arrangement. [P3, P4]

#### E. Valve seat, sealing face and valve member

- **Q29** Is sealing at the valve seat metal-to-metal (the sealing face on the NiTi upper edge)? Is there any elastomer anywhere in the valve core? — Affects how the valve seat is described and distinguished; resolves 01 M21. [P4, P5]

Have you conceived any other part of the valve member bearing on the deflectable element? — Supports the purge shoulder dependent and tests 03 A3. [P4]
- **Q33** How is the valve member guided in the core body, and does sealant film affect the guide? — Closes an enablement gap on guidance. [P4]

## Rationale

Every unresolved item in 01 (m) (M1–M24) appears here, as does every open question from 02, 03 and 04. Each provisional classification from 03 has a question that would settle it: purge gap and purge flow in Q1–Q4 and Q7; recovery wipe in Q7–Q8; two-rate characteristic in Q13–Q14; convoluted wall in Q21; electropolishing in Q42. The interference fit's conventional status is tested in Q4.

The themes are ordered by scope impact, not by component. Theme A comes first because, until the purge gap mechanism is explained, the nucleus is not enabled as written. Theme B comes next because it decides between N0 and N0-W. Where a question offers examples (Q5, Q14, Q21, Q23, Q28, Q43), they are there so the inventor can recognise something they have already thought of; each question says to answer only from their own conception, and no example is to be carried into the application unless the inventor adopts it.

Q54's grace-period note is general and stated only so the inventor understands why it is asked; counsel decides.
