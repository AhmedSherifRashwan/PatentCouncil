## Rationale

I ranked by what a competitor must copy to get the outcome, not by technical interest. For that reason the NiTi seat ring, the part the inventor emphasises, sits at ranks 7–8: the inventor's own alternative B shows the effect can in principle be had without it. The broadest defensible subject is the over-travel-driven opening of a closed purge gap, and that is where the independent protection should sit, subject to Phase 2.

I did not assume a sales channel, price or licensing plan; none is disclosed. Where a ranking rests on a market guess (OEM fitting, retrofit sales), it is an assumption. The method subject matter is kept but ranked below the apparatus subject matter because its direct infringers are riders. Indirect-infringement treatment differs across US, EP and GB, and that is counsel's call.

The ranking for rank 1 does not claim novelty. No prior art has been analysed here; that is Phase 2.
