## Rationale

**What triggered a revision.** No finding and no correction was supplied; the only trigger is disclosure that `description.v2.md` newly contains. Two of the three new paragraphs state something a claim can rest on: [0034a] (the two surfaces of the recovery wipe in the first embodiment) and [0094a] (the rigid valve seat carried by the core body). [0132a] reports a measurement and its limits; no claim wording rests on it.

**Why rungs and not amendments to existing claims.** Claims 5, 35 and 52 claim the recovery wipe for every deflectable element ([0055], [0117]); writing the upper edge into them would exclude the wiper ring, for which [0034a] names no contacting portion. Claims 27 and 45 cover a rigid valve seat wherever it is carried; writing the core body into them would give up the valve-stem position for nothing. So each new fact is a rung, and the claims above it stay broad.

**Why claims 57 and 59 hang on claims 20 and 42.** [0034a] opens with the words "In the first embodiment", and [0090] defines the first embodiment as the seat ring of superelastic alloy with the convoluted wall. The reasoning of [0034a] (the upper edge is the surface that provides the valve seat and contacts the sealing face) would hold for any seat ring, and [0093] says the second embodiment differs only in the material of the seat ring. But reading [0034a] onto the polymer seat ring needs that argument, and a limitation that needs my argument to look supported is not supported. The wider dependency (claims 17 and 41) is therefore a disclosure request, not a claim.

**Why no "recovery wipe" in claims 57 and 59.** Claim 20 can depend, through claim 12, on claim 5, which already introduces "a recovery wipe"; a second introduction in claim 57 would raise the question whether two wipes are meant. The new claims recite the sliding itself, which [0055] defines as the recovery wipe.

**Concessions.** The drafter refused six requests as needing the inventor (CL-1, CL-3, CL-5, CL-7, CL-8, CL-9). I concede all six. Each refusal quotes a confirmed entry that rests on nothing the inventor has said, and the standing instruction is that nobody fills a factual gap about the invention on the inventor's behalf. I cannot point to a passage of the Description that contains the missing fact, so there is nothing to push back with. Those requests now lie with the inventor, and they are kept in `disclosure_requests` marked as such so that the human sees them if the loop is capped.

**Where I do disagree.** Two points, both on the text. First, the drafter's record that the two-rate characteristic is the only disclosed means is right, but it does not follow that claims 1, 2 and 4 must recite it: the Description states the requirement generally in five places and says in terms that the concept requires the threshold "however it is provided" ([0016]). Whether one disclosed means carries that breadth in each office is counsel's call; I have left the claims broad and recorded the narrowing for the human. Second, [0034a], [0094a] and [0132a] each contain sentences saying what the specification does not state or did not measure. They support no limitation, and [0132a] in particular says on the face of the specification that the evidence for a feature of every independent claim is indirect. My request asked whether the closure is gas-tight; the honest answer ("not measured separately") belongs in the dossier, and whether it also belongs in the filed text is a decision for counsel.

**Appending rather than inserting.** The prompt allows renumbering when claims are added; my standing rule is that unchanged claims keep their numbers. Appending satisfies both and keeps every citation in `25`, `26`, `27` and the pre-drafting response valid. The cost is that claims 48, 49 and 54 do not reach the new rungs; adding them to those lists would make a claim refer to a later claim, which is a formal defect. [0038] covers the gap as amendment basis.

**Declined.** A rung reciting that the purge shoulder bears on the upper edge ([0111] step 706, [0034a]) was not added: the passage was already in `description.md`, so nothing new triggers it, and claim 17 already recites the axial load on the upper edge. A rung reciting that both parts are in motion during the sliding ([0034a] second sentence) was not added: it excludes nothing a competitor would build and adds a fact to prove.
