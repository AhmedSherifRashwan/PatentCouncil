## Rationale

The blueprint is arranged so that the reservoir is wide but honest. The inventors' disclosure is thin in exactly the places the claims need most (the purge gap geometry, the threshold measuring condition, the wipe geometry, alternative B), and Gate 2 confirmed, without a human, a large body of AI-generated alternatives. I resolved that tension by splitting the Description into two kinds of content with a fixed home for each: S9 holds only what the inventors conceived; S13 holds the genus statements the disclosure supports, followed by flagged sub-sections for every AI-proposed species. The compose step can then include or omit the flagged species one by one, and the response table records the disposition. That is the least narrowing way to keep the amendment basis those species would give without letting them masquerade as the inventors' work.

The purge gap is described by function throughout (S7.4, S8.4). I considered adopting E10.1 as "the prototype's likely construction" because it is the only topology consistent with every §3 and §7 statement, and rejected it: it would state an invented fact in the filed text and the inventor has not been asked. The §2 "stem bore" wording is not reproduced as a location; if the inventor confirms it, it becomes a second embodiment (E10.4) rather than an inconsistency.

The two cleaning actions get separate effect statements and neither is called sufficient. The N0-W family (F5) is opened in the blueprint so that either answer to 03 Q1 leaves a supported independent claim; Claims Architect decides whether to draft it as a second independent claim or an alternative formulation.

Claim families follow the value map and PF-10 rather than a Claims Architect draft, which does not exist in the quick profile at this point. Sealant appears as a positive limitation only in F3. Kit, composition and software families are listed with "none" so that the absence is a recorded decision, not an oversight.

The Background cites publication numbers, never titles, because two saved titles contain the avoid-list phrase "self-cleaning valve"; the terminology check would fail and, more to the point, the phrase overstates what forty inflation cycles show.

I did not write the P4 response table (`P4/pre-drafting-response-P4.md`) in this task: its "where" column needs paragraph numbers and figure numbers that do not exist until Prompt 20 and the compose step, and the task names only this reply path. The blueprint's PF tags make the table mechanical to fill at compose time. The Orchestrator should route that file with the compose task.
