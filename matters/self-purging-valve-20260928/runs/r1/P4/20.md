## Rationale

The plan keeps the blueprint's FIG. 1 to FIG. 6 and adds two figures the playbook asks for and the blueprint does not name: a use-sequence flow chart (FIG. 7), because the purging method is a claim family of its own and a flow chart in claim order is the cheapest support for it, and a system overview (FIG. 8), because the valve, valve core and inflatable-body claim levels need one figure that shows all three together with the inflation device. FIG. 8 comes last rather than first so that the inventors' two sketches keep the numbers FIG. 1 and FIG. 2 that the blueprint fixed.

The purge gap is the only region I refused to draw. The disclosure says the seat ring expands outward and opens an annular gap against the bore that holds it by an interference fit, and nobody has explained how; a sheet that resolves that would state an invented fact in the filed drawings, which cannot be repaired after filing. Every figure that shows the purge gap therefore marks it schematically, and the instruction to match the inventors' sketches when supplied goes into the Prompt 20a drawing instructions, not into the filed text.

FIG. 4 is split into 4A and 4B because the disclosure says "one or two" convolutions and does not say which the prototype has; two part-views disclose both without asserting either as built. The convolution is drawn outward on the sheet because the disclosure speaks of radial expansion, but the direction is left open in the text (19 D26). Dimensions are given as reference letters so that the §4 values live in the description, where each endpoint can be named individually, rather than on a sheet where a single number would be read as the invention.

FIG. 5 carries the three-band behaviour that PF-2 says must anchor "over-travel". It is a diagram, so it makes no statement about the measuring condition the dossier lacks (PF-3); the two stages are straight segments only because the disclosure says "soft" and "stiffer" and nothing more.
