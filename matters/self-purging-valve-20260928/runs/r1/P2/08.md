### Novelty and Inventive-Step Objections

This is a preliminary, diagnostic attack prepared by AI as a drafting and analysis aid. It is not a patentability opinion, not legal advice and not a filing decision; qualified patent counsel signs off before anything is filed. The final, text-level examiner attack is Prompt 32. Everything here is `candidate`.

**Basis.** The only art used is the ten references charted in `P2/07` (eight saved real references and two fictional requester summaries). Chart rows are cited as `07 <reference> R<n>`. There are no drafted claims, so the "proposed independent claim concepts" are 03's nucleus N0, the alternative nucleus N0-W, each ladder rung, and the purging method ranked fourth in the value map (`P1/02`). `intake/answers.md` does not exist, so no inventor answer narrows or widens any concept here.

**Coverage warning.** The reference set is thin (07 Q1). An objection rated weak below is weak against these ten documents only. A professional search could produce a single reference that makes the N0 novelty objection strong.

**Ratings.** *Strong*: an examiner would likely maintain it, and the concept needs amendment or evidence to survive. *Moderate*: arguable either way; the outcome depends on claim wording, the definitions and the evidence of effect. *Weak*: the art lacks a clause the concept needs, and the gap is not bridged by any supplied reference.

**Jurisdictions.** Novelty is tested one reference at a time in all three offices. EPO and UKIPO require direct and unambiguous disclosure, so the `ambiguous` rows in 07 are weaker as novelty attacks there than under US inherency, which asks whether the feature is necessarily present. Inventive step is framed as problem-solution at the EPO, Windsurfing/Pozzoli in the UK, and Graham/KSR in the US (where "predictable use of prior-art elements according to their established functions" is the examiner's usual line). The ratings below apply to all three unless a difference is stated.

#### Objections leading the attack (what a real examiner would raise first)

**Objection 1: novelty of N0 over US11179979B2 (closest single reference).** A tyre valve for sealant-filled tubeless wheels (07 US11179979B2 R1). The valve member of its valve core (core rod 16 and venting element 11) is moved until it deflects a deflectable element (sealing lips 6) and opens a gap (slit 7) that is closed in the rest state ([0101]; R4 ambiguous). The lips recover and close the slit ([0016]; R7 explicit). The examiner's reading: "The core rod's travel through the first valve is travel beyond the stroke of an ordinary Presta-type valve core, and the slit carries gas across the lips, which are exposed to sealant." **What it lacks:** no over-travel above a pressure threshold; the core rod is moved by hand after unlocking the core nut ([0101]; R3 ambiguous). The slit is in series in the only flow path, so it is not a purge gap separate from the main flow passage (R5 absent). No flow is directed across sealant-accumulation surfaces to clean them; the first valve keeps sealant out ([0030]; R6 absent). **Rating: weak**, provided the claim requires (i) over-travel caused by the pressure differential exceeding an over-travel threshold, and (ii) a purge gap separate from the main flow passage. If either is left out or left to interpretation, the rating becomes **moderate**. The lips and slit would then read on "deflectable element" and "purge gap".

**Objection 2: inventive step of N0 over ref-02 or US11338632B2 in view of US20080188352A1 and US3570510A (strongest combination).**
- *Closest art (EPO).* Ref-02 is a tyre valve whose valve member deflects an elastomer seat lip that recovers on removal of the inflation device (07 ref-02 R2, R4 partial, R7 partial, R11). It is fictional; on the real references, US11338632B2 plays the same role. It is a tubeless valve that names sealant fouling as the problem ([0004]; R1 explicit).
- *Difference.* Over-travel above a higher threshold (R3); a separate purge gap closed in the rest state and opened by the deflection (R5); purge flow across the sealant-accumulation surfaces (R6).
- *Objective technical problem.* Keeping the valve's sealing surfaces free of sealant film without a tool.
- *Examiner's case.* US3570510A teaches exactly that aim for a valve handling "sticky dust particles" ([3], [7]). Its solution is to blow gas through an annular slit at the seat across the sealing surfaces, strongest as the valve closes ([12], [26]). US20080188352A1 teaches opening a second, normally closed flow path only above a second, higher pressure ([26]; R5 partial). US3570510A [28] even contemplates matching the cleaning fluid to "the kind of fluid passing through the valve". The skilled person would supply the purge from the inflation gas already present, gate it with the two-stage opening of US20080188352A1, and form the gate from the deflectable seat lip already in ref-02 (claim 4) or US3417778A (claim 1). Motivation: ref-02 [0008], US11338632B2 [0004] and US11179979B2 [0030] all identify contamination of tyre-valve sealing surfaces as the known problem.
- *Where the attack is thin.* It needs three references from three fields: tyre valves, transmission oil and industrial process valves. None of them opens a flow path by deflecting a resilient element through over-travel. US3570510A's purge uses a separate gas store (container 12, [22]) and piston valves interlocked with its "valve body 3" ([23]); taking it into a 6 mm valve core means discarding its whole mechanism. US20080188352A1 opens its second path by lifting a second rigid valve member, not by deflection. Assembling the claimed mechanism from these pieces needs the claim as a guide.
- **Rating: moderate** (EPO and UK: moderate to weak, because problem-solution and Pozzoli penalise a three-document mosaic across fields; US: moderate, because KSR tolerates combinations of known elements when the examiner can articulate a reason). The rating rises to **strong** if a professional search finds a single tyre or check valve in which a higher-pressure stage deflects a resilient seat to open a secondary path.

**Objection 3: common general knowledge (labelled assumptions, not tied to a supplied reference).** An examiner may assert without a document that (a) two-stage spring-loaded valves and (b) flushing a valve seat with the working fluid are CGK in valve design (A2, A3). Against N0 as a whole, such an assertion carries little weight at the EPO without documentary evidence for the combination (Guidelines practice requires evidence when CGK is contested). In the US it can be pressed as "official notice" and must be traversed. **Rating: weak** against N0; **strong** against the individual increments noted below (N2, N4 retention, N6 Af, N7, N8, N10).

#### What the attack shows

1. The whole case rests on three N0 clauses that no single supplied reference discloses: R3 (pressure-driven over-travel above a higher threshold), R5 (a purge gap separate from the main flow passage, closed in the rest state, opened by deflection) and R6 (purge flow across the sealant-accumulation surfaces). Each is found in part somewhere in the set; none is found together with the others.
2. The attacks on novelty are weak only if the claim language makes R3 and R5 unambiguous. The US11179979B2 core-rod passage ([0101]) and the US20080188352A1 ball-in-inner-member arrangement ([26], [33]) are the two readings a hostile examiner will use to erode "over-travel". Both are answered by anchoring over-travel to the valve seat and to a pressure-differential threshold.
3. The rungs N2, N4, N6-A, N7, N8 and N10 add almost nothing against this art. Their value is as fallbacks against later art, not as distinctions from the art in hand. N3, N5 and the recovery wipe in N1 are the rungs with no supplied reference against them.
4. N0-W (wipe without purge) is materially more exposed than N0 (ref-02 [0020] + US4236547A [6]). If Gate 1 answers show the wipe alone gives the effect, the nucleus choice should be revisited with that in mind.
5. The evidence of technical effect (40 cycles against 12–18, §8) supports the combination as built. It does not separate purge flow from the recovery wipe, or NiTi from any other material. An examiner arguing "no technical effect attributable to the distinguishing feature" against N6 would find the data silent.

## Rationale

The attack is led the way an examiner writes an action: the closest single reference for novelty, then the best combination, then CGK. US11179979B2 leads rather than US11338632B2 because it is the only reference where movement of a tyre-valve valve member deflects a resilient element to open a normally closed gap. US11338632B2 shares the problem but has no deflectable element at all.

For the combination, ref-02 is used as closest art because it has the most N0 clauses in one document. It is fictional, so the same attack is restated on US11338632B2, which a real examiner could use. The rating is moderate rather than strong because the three pieces come from unrelated fields and none opens a gap by deflection; it is moderate rather than weak because each piece is a known answer to a known problem, which is what KSR rewards.

The rungs are rated on their increment over the rung above, not re-attacked from scratch. The per-rung novelty column repeats "as N1" where no reference adds anything.

CGK assertions that no supplied reference supports are recorded as assumptions (A2–A7) and not as findings.

- A8 Ratings assume the claim defines over-travel as travel of the valve member relative to its valve seat caused by the pressure differential exceeding the over-travel threshold (07 Q4). Blocking for P5 claim definitions only.

- Q3 Is there any evidence that the superelastic alloy, rather than the geometry, gives the recovery wipe its effect (for example a PEEK or elastomer ring tested in the same core)? Without it, N6 is exposed to a material-substitution objection with no shown effect. Blocks P5.
- Q4 Is there any evidence of criticality inside the §4 ranges, in particular the over-travel threshold (2.5–5.0 bar), which overlaps US20080188352A1 [14]? Without it, N8 falls to routine optimisation. Blocks P5.
- Q5 01 Q1 remains open: the purge-gap geometry. The R5 distinction on which the N0 rating depends assumes §3 item 6 works as described. Blocks P3, P4, P5.
