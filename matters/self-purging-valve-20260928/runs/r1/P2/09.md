## Rationale

D1 and D2 lead because together they answer the novelty objection over US11179979B2 and remove two of the three limbs of the combination attack. D3 and D4 each answer one remaining limb of that attack. D5 answers the aggregation argument against N1. D6–D11 are rung distinctions that 08 rated weak increments or fallbacks.

Every row states a structural or sequential relationship (where the gap is, what opens it, what triggers the travel, which part slides on which) and the effect it produces, rather than the result "keeps the valve clean". Result language appears only in the effect column.

D1 carries the highest design-around risk despite leading the matrix. It is the strongest distinction on the art and the weakest on enablement, because the disclosure does not explain the purge-gap geometry. That is the most important single answer the inventor can give.

D8 is kept, though exposed, because the inventor's alternative A statement supports an effect tied to the material. The support is an assertion, and the row says so.
