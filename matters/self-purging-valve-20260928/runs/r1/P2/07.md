## Rationale

The rows follow 03's nucleus and ladder, with N0 split into seven clauses. N0 is a combination, and the art hits individual clauses; a single row for N0 would have hidden which clause each reference meets.

Status is given for the row as defined, and partial disclosures go in the Note with their passage. The alternative, marking a row `ambiguous` whenever part of it is disclosed, would overstate what the references teach. `ambiguous` is kept for cases where the reading genuinely depends on interpretation. There are four: US11179979B2 R3/R4 (core rod travel opening the lips), US20080188352A1 R3/R9 (ball travelling with the inner member; two springs), US3417778A R4/R16, and ref-01 R6.

US11179979B2 is placed first because it is the only reference in which movement of a valve member of a sealant-exposed tyre valve deflects a resilient element to open a gap closed in the rest state ([0101]). The Researcher's triage rated it on the lips opening by pressure; the core-rod passage makes it closer than that.

US20080188352A1 R3 is marked ambiguous rather than absent. A hostile examiner will read "travel beyond the normal stroke" relative to the housing; the definition of over-travel should fix the reference frame (relative to the valve seat and the core body). That is a drafting point for Phase 5, recorded as an open question.

The two fictional references are charted fully so the prompts are exercised. Their "reviewer's note" sections are not part of the reference text and were not used as disclosure.
