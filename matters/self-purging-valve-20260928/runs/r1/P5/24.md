## Rationale

The prompt asks for three materially different approaches, not three re-orderings. The material differences are these. Formulation 1 removes the pressure thresholds and the purge flow from the claim text and puts the geometry of the lost motion in their place: the purge shoulder clear during the normal stroke, bearing only during over-travel, the undeflected shape closing the purge gap and the load opening it. That is 09 D4 promoted from a rung to the defining relation, and it is the one formulation that a competitor cannot leave by changing what the pressure does. Formulation 2 removes every part that transmits the over-travel and defines the valve by its map of behaviour against pressure differential, which [0009] states in exactly three ranges; its value over 22 claim 1 is that the conventional-valve middle range and the parallel topology are claim words, so the two hostile readings in 08 fail on the text. Formulation 3 defines the same nucleus as the interactions of one inflation and carries the sequence through to the recovery wipe, which is the one action that reaches the sealing face and the one that 09 D5 and 08 N1 identify as needing four references to attack.

I tested the alternative 22 A3 left for this prompt, anchoring over-travel "relative to the valve seat" (09 A2, 08 A8). It is not needed in any formulation: Formulation 1 does it structurally (the same valve member carries the sealing face and the purge shoulder), Formulation 2 does it by "the same valve member ... produced by the pressure differential", and the anchor would give up a seat that moves with the valve member. I kept "produced by the pressure differential acting on the valve member against the biasing member" in Formulation 2 for the reason 22 gives; in Formulation 1 the same exclusion is carried by the definition of over-travel ([0043]), and the comparison says so rather than hiding it.

The recovery wipe was the hardest call. Putting it in the method independent claim narrows the broadest supported method, so I recommend it as the first dependent, with a stated trigger for promoting it: if the inventor answers 22 Q4 that both actions are needed, the wipe belongs in the independent claim because the only evidence of effect ([0131]) is for a valve in which both occur, and a purge-only claim would then invite the objection that the effect is not credible across its scope. That is a judgement for counsel, and the comparison row gives both routes.

Formulation 1 could have recited that the normal stroke ends at the position at which the purge shoulder first bears on the deflectable element ([0041]); I left it out because [0041] states it for "the embodiments described" and 19 A3 records that whether the second stage of the biasing member begins at that position is unconfirmed (19 Q3). It is an available dependent with [0041] as anchor. I also considered "the main flow passage being the only path for inflation gas through the valve while the purge gap is closed" ([0045]) for Formulation 2 and rejected it: a third, permanently open path would take a competitor outside it for no technical cost, which is the pattern lessons.md L1 warns against.

None of the three uses a negative limitation: "remains undeflected" and "remains closed" are states the description names ([0009], [0031]). Antecedent and format checks were run on the three claims as a preview of Prompt 25: no flag. The support map is a Prompt 25 deliverable and is not written here.
