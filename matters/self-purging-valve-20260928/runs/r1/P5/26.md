Summary of the audit. No limitation of the Final Claim Set is drafted as a means-plus-function limitation, and none uses "means". The one term with real US placeholder exposure is "deflectable element"; its two disclosed architectures and three materials are all that the disclosure contains, so a §112(f) construction would cost scope over undisclosed forms only, and the species ladder already exists. The two exposures that matter are not clarity but sufficiency: the mechanism by which the purge gap opens (rows for the purge gap and for claims 16 and 40), and the determination condition of the two pressures (rows for the three ranges and for claims 32 to 34 and 47). Both are repeated from `25` as disclosure requests, because the audit confirms that no claim wording cures them. Two functions rest on a single disclosed way and are therefore disclosure requests rather than narrowings: the provision of the over-travel threshold (one principle, the two-rate biasing member, in four constructions) and the reception of the valve core in the valve stem (threaded only).

## Rationale

The audit treats each limitation from three seats. As US examiner, the question is whether the word is a placeholder; "element" is the classic one and "member" the next, and the answer for each is the structural relations the claim itself recites plus [0088]. As EPO examiner, the question is whether the function can be verified and whether the description enables it across the breadth; that is why the purge gap and the pressure thresholds are the rows that carry the exposure, since each is verifiable but each rests on a description that states the result and not the mechanism or the measurement. As litigator, the question is whether the fallback exists in the claim set already; in every case where a structural rung would be needed it does (claims 6, 7, 8, 14, 16, 27, 46), so no reword is proposed for the independent claims.

I did not propose narrowing the independent claims to the located purge gap or to the two-rate biasing member, because the description discloses the wiper-ring purge gap and states the over-travel threshold is a property of the valve whatever provides it; narrowing would surrender the design-around cover that 09 D1 and D2 rely on, and the prompt directs that a single disclosed way is a disclosure request, not a narrowing. The "spring" alternative for the biasing member is recorded only as a US fallback; it is not proposed for adoption, since [0121] discloses only springs and the term "biasing member" costs nothing at the EPO.

The construction rungs of [0120] and [0119] are named as amendment basis and not as proposed dependent claims, because `25` A5 records that those paragraphs entered the description from AI-proposed embodiments confirmed only mechanically at Gate 2, and a rung resting on them would claim what the inventor has not confirmed conceiving.

Statutory provisions are named for orientation only; counsel applies them.
