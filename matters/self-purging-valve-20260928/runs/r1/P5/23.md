### Dependent Claim Ladders

These ladders are a drafting and analysis aid prepared by AI. They are not legal advice, a patentability opinion or a filing decision; qualified patent counsel signs off before anything is filed. Every claim is `candidate`. The description (`description.md` with `registry.json`) is the only source of support relied on; every rung carries its paragraph anchors, and no rung without one has been drafted. Reference numerals are those of the registry and, as [0091] states, are for ease of reference only.

Independent claims 1 to 4 are those of `22` (valve; valve core; inflatable body fitted with the valve of claim 1; method of purging) and are not repeated. The dependent claims are numbered continuously from 5. Each claim is followed by one note: *Added* (the limitation) | *Effect* (the technical effect) | *Support* (paragraphs) | *Answers* (the prior-art or design-around risk the rung answers, citing the rows of the differentiation matrix `09 D1` to `09 D11`, the chart rows `07 R1` to `07 R20` and the per-concept ratings of `08`). Prior-art statements are measured against the ten charted references only (07 Q1).

Rungs are ordered by fallback value within each ladder: first the rungs with no charted reference against them (recovery wipe, purge shoulder, convoluted wall), then the two-rate characteristic and the material, then the seat ring branch and the wiper ring branch as alternatives, then the numeric rungs. Multiple dependencies are written in EPO form ("any one of claims"); counsel converts them for the US. Constructions that entered the description as AI-proposed alternatives and were confirmed only mechanically at Gate 2 (the forms of the purge shoulder in [0119], the constructions of the two-rate characteristic in [0120], the spring forms of [0121] and the alternative retentions of [0122]) are not drafted as rungs; they remain amendment basis, as A4 records.

## Rationale

The ladders are built by fallback value, not by order of assembly. The three rungs no charted reference reaches (recovery wipe, purge shoulder, convoluted wall) sit highest in their branches; the rungs 08 rates as strong increments (two-rate characteristic, interference fit, austenite finish temperature, the ranges) are kept because their value is against art not yet found, and each note says so, so that nobody reads a numeric rung as a distinction from the ten references in hand.

Two branch points are deliberate. Claim 11 (valve seat provided by the deflectable element) and claim 28 (rigid valve seat with a wiper ring) are alternatives, and neither is required by any rung above it, so the wiper ring remains inside every combination of claims 1 and 5 to 10. Claim 12 introduces the core body once, and the seat ring rung hangs from it, because [0051] defines the seat ring as received in the core bore of the core body; the seat ring cannot be claimed without the core body without contradicting the definition. The alloy is claimed twice at different levels (claim 10 on the deflectable element; claim 21 on the convoluted-wall seat ring) so that the alloy is available with and without the convoluted wall; the austenite finish temperature and the alloy system then depend on either.

Numeric rungs are drafted from the stated ranges only, with the endpoints [0125] to [0127] disclose individually. Claim 35 is the one sub-range: it is formed from the stated lower endpoint and the prototype value, exactly as [0085] and [0130] permit, and it exists to take the over-travel threshold below the second pressure of US20080188352A1 [14]. The seat ring dimensions hang from the alloy seat ring because [0124] and [0125] say the values are those of that seat ring and are not stated for the polymer seat ring. The pressure, over-travel and purge gap width ranges hang from claim 1 because [0016], [0040], [0042], [0043] and [0066] state them for the valve generally; A3 records the tension with [0124].

Rungs considered and declined. The forms of the purge shoulder in [0119] (separate collar, lugs), the constructions of the two-rate characteristic in [0120], the spring forms of [0121] and the retentions of [0122] entered the description from AI-proposed embodiments (12 E5.1 to E5.5, E6.3, E6.4, E14.2 to E14.4) that Gate 2 confirmed mechanically with no inventor review; a dependent claim to a construction the inventor has not conceived is not a fallback but a liability, so they stay as amendment basis. The conical sealing face and the hard-anodised aluminium sealing face ([0029], [0050], [0095]) add nothing a competitor could not omit at no cost. The lock nut is conventional ([0036]). The cartridge inflator is disclosed but its behaviour with the alloy is undetermined ([0113]), so claim 56 names the floor pump only. The statement that the recovery wipe shears partially cured sealant film ([0010], [0034]) is a result attributed by the inventors, not a structure, and is not claimed. The thermal purge variant is not claimed ([0096]). A rung widening the last step of claim 4 to the pressure differential falling without removal of the inflation device is a widening, not a narrowing, and belongs to Prompt 25 with [0039] as basis. The kit is not claimed (21 F10).

The valve core ladder is compact by design: twelve rungs that decide election and enforcement, with the transplantable rungs named in the section heading, rather than a second copy of the valve ladder. Claim 49 duplicates claim 13 in effect; it is kept for the election case and flagged for Prompt 25.

Multiple dependencies are EPO-style and are limited so that every "any one of" range contains the antecedents the rung uses; the wiper ring rung therefore depends on claims 6 and 7 only, and the alloy seat ring rung on claims 18 to 20 only. A preview run of the antecedent and claims-format checks over claims 1 to 57 (independents from `22.r1.md`) is reported in the task comment; the support map and those checks are Prompt 25 deliverables.

Statutory provisions are named for orientation only; counsel applies them.
