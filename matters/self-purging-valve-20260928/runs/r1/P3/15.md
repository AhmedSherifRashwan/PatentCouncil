## Rationale

The rows follow the mechanism, the materials, the manufacture and the user, in that order, because that is the order in which a competitor's product engineer and an opponent's expert would attack the device. The bench data and the inventor's own §9 list gave the first three rows (film in the purge-gap region, fatigue, cold shock); the rest are inferred from the mechanism. The recurring answer, an over-travel stop, was kept as one candidate cross-referenced from six rows rather than repeated, so that Gate 2 decides it once.

Mitigations were limited to structures, steps and use notes that a valve-core designer could plausibly have conceived from the disclosure; nothing presumes a test the dossier does not contain. Where a row's mitigation is only a use note (FM-20) or a question (FM-7) it is marked not worth disclosing, so the table does not pad the description. Rows on temperature (FM-8 to FM-10) state the material behaviour as general knowledge and leave every number to the inventor.

Prompt 12 and 13 replies were not available, so no equivalent architecture or ladder rung is cited; the parameter references are the §4 estimates only.
