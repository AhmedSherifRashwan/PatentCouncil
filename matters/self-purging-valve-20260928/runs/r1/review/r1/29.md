## Findings

### Amendment Paths

Premise: claims 1, 2 and 4 have been rejected, for example for lack of inventive step over a combination of the saved references on tubular resilient seats, two-stage opening and seat purging. Each path uses only features expressly disclosed in the package, and each is written for claim 1. The same path applies to claim 2 through [0038], and to claim 4 as the matching method step. Amendments must also be read with the findings of Prompt 28: a path that imports the seat ring geometry or the wiper ring also imports F-28-1 to F-28-3 until those are fixed.

**Path 1 — add the recovery wipe.**
Wording: claim 1 + "and wherein, as the deflectable element recovers when the pressure differential falls, the deflectable element slides across the sealing face of the valve member in a recovery wipe."
Combination support: [0010], [0011], [0016] ("The following optional features apply to every aspect. The deflectable element ... may slide across the sealing face"), [0034], [0055], [0117]; claim 5 as filed.
Risk: **none**. [0016] states the feature for every aspect, and [0117] states it for all three deflectable elements.
Specification addition: none needed for basis. See F-29-1 for the direction of the sliding.

**Path 2 — add the purge shoulder.**
Wording: claim 1 + "the valve member having a purge shoulder which is clear of the deflectable element throughout the normal stroke and bears on the deflectable element only during the over-travel, so that the over-travel of the valve member is applied to the deflectable element as a load which deflects it."
Combination support: [0016], [0029], [0054], [0119], [0111] step 706; claim 6 as filed.
Risk: **none** for this wording. It is **possible** if "annular step ... below the sealing face" (claim 7) is added, because that position is the one F-28-1 finds incoherent.
Specification addition: the actual position of the purge shoulder relative to the sealing face and the valve seat (F-29-2).

**Path 3 — add the two-rate characteristic.**
Wording: claim 1 + "the biasing member having a two-rate characteristic with a first stage which sets the opening pressure and a stiffer second stage which sets the over-travel threshold."
Combination support: [0016], [0033], [0063], [0112], [0120] (four constructions named); claim 8 as filed.
Risk: **none**. It would be **possible** only if one of the constructions of [0120] (variable pitch, springs in series, nested springs, preloaded second stage) were claimed with the seat ring features. [0124] makes those alternatives combinable with each embodiment, so the risk is low.
Specification addition: none needed.

**Path 4 — the deflectable element provides the valve seat as a seat ring in the core bore.**
Wording: claim 1 + "wherein the deflectable element is a seat ring whose upper edge provides the valve seat, the seat ring being received in a core bore of a core body of the valve and located against a locating shoulder of the core body."
Combination support: [0016], [0028], [0030], [0049], [0051]; claims 11, 12 and 14 as filed.
Risk: **none** for basis. **Likely** Art. 83 exposure unless F-28-1 is fixed, because the stated relation between the upper edge, the sealing face and the purge shoulder is not coherent.
Specification addition: F-29-2.

**Path 5 — add the convoluted wall and the purge gap on the outer surface of the seat ring.**
Wording: Path 4 + "the seat ring having a convoluted wall with at least one convolution, the convoluted wall converting an axial load applied to the upper edge during the over-travel into radial expansion of the seat ring, the radial expansion opening the purge gap, the purge gap being formed between an outer surface of the seat ring and the core bore."
Combination support: [0030], [0031], [0064], [0095] (seat ring with the convoluted wall "disclosed as an article irrespective of its material"), [0111] step 706; claims 16 and 17 as filed.
Risk: **possible**, and serious on clarity and enablement rather than added matter. The words have basis, but [0035] retains the ring by an interference fit in the same bore, and outward expansion cannot open a gap on the outer surface (F-28-2). Adding the interference fit (claim 15) makes the conflict explicit.
Specification addition: which surfaces separate, where the interference fit acts, and the outlet past the locating shoulder (F-29-3).

**Path 6 — the deflectable element of a superelastic alloy with a stated austenite finish temperature.**
Wording: claim 1 + "wherein the deflectable element is of a superelastic alloy having an austenite finish temperature below 5 °C."
Combination support: [0016] ("The seat ring may be of a superelastic alloy having an austenite finish temperature below 5 °C"), [0048], [0074], [0118] (stated for "the deflectable element" generally); claim 38 as filed.
Risk: **possible** at the EPO. [0118] states the material for the deflectable element generally, but "below 5 °C" appears there only as an example of "below the lowest temperature at which the valve 92 is intended to be used". The only superelastic element described with structure is the seat ring. An examiner may say that superelastic alloy with Af below 5 °C is disclosed only for the seat ring with the convoluted wall ([0092]). [0016] and [0134] answer that objection, but only as general statements.
Specification addition: none that needs new matter. The basis is adequate if [0118] is kept unchanged. Adding "nickel–titanium" (claim 22) to this path is **possible** risk, because [0073] states NiTi for the first embodiment only.

**Path 7 — add the numerical rungs for pressure and travel.**
Wording: claim 1 + "wherein the opening pressure is from 0.8 bar to 1.5 bar and the over-travel threshold is from 2.5 bar to 3.5 bar, each being a value of the pressure differential, and the over-travel is from 0.4 mm to 0.9 mm beyond the end of the normal stroke."
Combination support: [0040], [0042], [0043], [0085] (endpoints disclosed individually, prototype values usable as endpoints), [0127], [0130]; claims 31, 32 and 34 as filed.
Risk: **likely** intermediate generalisation. [0124] states that "the dimensions and pressures of the section on ranges are those of the first embodiment and are not stated for the alternatives", and [0125] ties the values to the seat ring of superelastic alloy. An examiner would say that the ranges are disclosed only together with that seat ring. The definitions in [0040], [0042] and [0043] ("in some embodiments") point the other way, and the application contradicts itself (F-28-4).
Specification addition: F-29-4.

**Path 8 — the prototype as a whole (last-resort fallback).**
Wording: claim 2 + "wherein the deflectable element is a seat ring of a nickel–titanium superelastic alloy having an austenite finish temperature below 5 °C, whose upper edge provides the valve seat, having a convoluted wall with one or two convolutions and a convolution amplitude of from 0.08 mm to 0.20 mm, a seat ring inner diameter of from 2.0 mm to 2.6 mm, a wall thickness of from 0.15 mm to 0.30 mm and a free length of from 2.5 mm to 4.0 mm, the seat ring being received in a core bore of the core body against a locating shoulder, the opening pressure being from 0.8 bar to 1.5 bar and the over-travel threshold from 2.5 bar to 5.0 bar."
Combination support: [0092] (the first embodiment as a whole), [0073], [0074], [0125], [0127]; claims 42 and 43 as filed.
Risk: **none** on added matter; everything is in [0092] with [0125] to [0127]. Omitting the core body material, the interference fit and the valve member material from [0092] is a **possible** intermediate generalisation, but [0095] and [0122] disclose alternatives for each, which answers it. The path carries F-28-1 and F-28-2 in full.
Specification addition: F-29-2 and F-29-3.

**Path 9 — rigid valve seat and separate wiper ring.**
Wording: Path 2 + "wherein the valve seat is a rigid valve seat which is not deflected during the over-travel, and the deflectable element is a wiper ring separate from the rigid valve seat, positioned below the rigid valve seat and deflected outward by the purge shoulder during the over-travel."
Combination support: [0049], [0053], [0094], [0094a], [0107]; claims 27 and 28 as filed.
Risk: **likely** added matter in combination with claim 1. [0094] and [0094a] leave the position of the purge gap unstated, and a wiper ring below the seat cannot give a purge gap in parallel with the main flow passage as [0046] defines it (F-28-3). The combination "wiper ring + parallel purge gap" is not directly and unambiguously derivable.
Specification addition: F-29-5.

**Path 10 — the method with the recovery wipe, the purge shoulder and a floor pump, over repeated cycles.**
Wording: claim 4 + "as the deflectable element recovers, the deflectable element sliding across the sealing face in a recovery wipe; the over-travel deflecting the deflectable element by a purge shoulder of the valve member which is clear of it throughout the normal stroke; the inflation device being a floor pump; and the method being repeated over a plurality of inflation cycles, purge flow passing through the purge gap at each inflation cycle in which the pressure differential exceeds the over-travel threshold."
Combination support: [0015], [0017], [0054], [0099], [0111], [0113]; claims 52, 53, 55 and 56 as filed.
Risk: **none** for basis. **Possible** challenge to the credibility of the floor pump rung. The only floor pump evidence is an audible snap on removal ([0102], [0115]), and when over-travel occurs within an inflation is expressly not stated ([0111]). An amendment reciting over-travel "during a pump stroke" or "before removal of the inflation device" would have no basis.
Specification addition: F-29-6.

### F-29-1 [low] [support] The direction of the recovery wipe is unstated, so no amendment can use it

[0034a]: "The direction of that sliding relative to the axis of the valve 92 is not stated herein." [0055]: the term "does not specify the direction".

Attack. If a reference shows a wipe in one direction (the saved references on seat-cleaning discs and cutting edges wipe as the valve closes), no disclosed feature can be added to Path 1 to distinguish by direction or by the surface that wipes. The fallback stops at "slides across".

Least narrowing action. If the inventors know the direction of the sliding in the prototype, or which part of the upper edge 32 contacts the sealing face 42 in the wipe, add it to [0034a] as a stated feature of the first embodiment. Nothing is to be added that they have not observed. New disclosure is required.

### F-29-2 [high] [enablement] Paths 2, 4, 5 and 8 have no coherent geometry to amend into

Claim 7: "an annular step of the valve member (40) below the sealing face (42)"; claim 14: "a seat ring (30) whose upper edge (32) provides the valve seat"; [0029], [0030], [0084], [0111] step 706.

Attack. The same defect as F-28-1. On the orientation of [0084], a purge shoulder below a sealing face that seals on the upper edge cannot bear on that upper edge by travel away from the seat under a pressure differential from the upper side. Every fallback built on the first embodiment inherits an Art. 83 and Art. 84 objection, and the objection cannot be cured after filing without new matter. Amendment basis is only worth what the embodiment behind it can carry.

Least narrowing action. Before filing, add the inventors' statement of the direction of the normal stroke, the side on which the sealing face seals, and where and in which direction the purge shoulder loads the seat ring. Align [0029], [0030], [0054], [0111] and the FIG. 1 and FIG. 2 instructions with it. New disclosure is required.

### F-29-3 [high] [enablement] Path 5 has no coherent purge gap mechanism to amend into

Claim 16: "formed between an outer surface (38) of the seat ring (30) and the core bore (22), and opens when the seat ring expands"; [0031], [0035], [0048].

Attack. The same defect as F-28-2. The seat ring is interference-fitted in the core bore, and its outward expansion cannot open a gap on its outer surface. No outlet past the locating shoulder 24 is described. Path 5 is the most distinctive structural fallback in the package, and as filed it cannot be relied on.

Least narrowing action. Before filing, add the inventors' statement of where the interference fit acts, which surfaces separate, and by what passage purge flow reaches the inflatable body. New disclosure is required.

### F-29-4 [high] [support] The pressure and travel ranges are tied to the first embodiment by [0124] and [0125], defeating Path 7

[0124]: "The dimensions and pressures of the section on ranges are those of the first embodiment and are not stated for the alternatives of this section"; [0125]: "The following values are those of the seat ring 30 of superelastic alloy of the prototype"; set against [0040], [0042], [0043] and [0066] ("in some embodiments").

Attack. The same defect as F-28-4, seen from the amendment side. If claim 1 is amended with an opening pressure and an over-travel threshold, an examiner will cite [0124] to say those values are disclosed only with the superelastic seat ring of the first embodiment. The amendment is then an intermediate generalisation (Art. 123(2)), unless the seat ring features are added too, which collapses Path 7 into Path 8.

Least narrowing action. Obtain the inventors' confirmation that the opening pressure, over-travel threshold, over-travel and purge gap width ranges belong to the valve as such. If they do, restate them in [0127] and [0126] for "the valve 92 of every embodiment" and confine [0124] and [0125] to the seat ring dimensions. If they do not, record that and accept that Path 7 needs Path 8. New disclosure is required, in the form of the inventors' confirmation.

### F-29-5 [high] [support] Path 9 lacks a disclosed purge gap for the wiper ring

Claim 28: "positioned below the rigid valve seat (132), towards the inflatable body (80)"; [0094]: "the position of its purge gap 60 are not otherwise limited"; [0094a].

Attack. The same defect as F-28-3. A wiper ring below the rigid valve seat sees only gas that has passed the valve seat, so its purge gap is not the parallel path that claim 1 and [0046] require. No combination of claim 1 with the wiper ring is directly and unambiguously disclosed, so Path 9 is an added-matter amendment as the text stands.

Least narrowing action. Add the inventors' statement of the third embodiment's purge gap: where it lies, and how inflation gas reaches it without passing the rigid valve seat 132. If there is none, Path 9 is available only under the recovery-wipe aspect of [0011]. New disclosure is required.

### F-29-6 [medium] [support] No basis exists to amend the method to over-travel during a pump stroke

Claim 4: "while the purge gap is open, passing purge flow (64) of the inflation gas supplied by the inflation device"; [0111]: "The point within an inflation at which over-travel occurs is not stated herein"; [0102], [0115].

Attack. If the method is rejected over a valve that purges on closing, the natural distinction is that purge flow occurs during the pump stroke, while the device is supplying gas. The package expressly declines to state that timing, and the only observation is a snap on removal. An amendment to "during a pump stroke" would be added matter.

Least narrowing action. If the inventors have observed when over-travel occurs during pumping, add that observation to [0111] and [0102]. Nothing is to be added that they have not observed. New disclosure is required.
