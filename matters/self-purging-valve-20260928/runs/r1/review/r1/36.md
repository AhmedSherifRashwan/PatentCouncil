### F-36-1 [high] [enablement] The first embodiment's purge gap opens "when the seat ring 30 expands" into the bore that holds it by interference

Paragraph and claim language: [0031], "the purge gap 60 is annular, is formed between the outer surface 38 of the seat ring 30 and the core bore 22 of the core body 20, and opens when the seat ring 30 expands"; [0035], "the seat ring 30 is retained in the core bore 22 by an interference fit of approximately 0.03 mm on diameter"; [0111] step 706; claims 16 and 40, "is formed between an outer surface (38) of the seat ring (30) and the core bore (22), and opens when the seat ring expands".

Attack (examiner and opponent): the seat ring is pressed into the core bore with interference, and the purge gap lies between its outer surface and that bore. Radial expansion of the seat ring ([0030], [0048]: "outward radial deflection") drives the outer surface 38 into the core bore 22; the text does not explain how that opens a gap between them, where along the seat ring the interference fit acts and where the gap forms, or how inflation gas enters and leaves the gap. [0026] and [0046] say the position and topology are "not limited to what is shown", which does not supply what is missing. The only built and tested embodiment's defining mechanism is therefore not taught; claims 16 and 40 recite it directly, and claims 1 and 2 depend on it for their only working example. An opponent reads this as insufficiency (EPC Art. 83; UK s.14(3)) and lack of enablement (US §112(a)); an examiner may raise it as an internal inconsistency.

Least narrowing action: add, from the inventors, a description of the first embodiment's purge-gap geometry: the axial region of the seat ring held by the interference fit and the region that forms the purge gap, which surface moves away from which during over-travel, and the path by which inflation gas enters the purge gap and passes into the inflatable body, with the sketch that shows it. No claim change if the added text bears out claims 16 and 40; if it shows the gap opening elsewhere, claims 16 and 40 are corrected to match.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-36-2 [high] [support] No drawings exist; the purge gap is drawn "schematically" with no inlet or outlet

Paragraph: `figures.md`, preamble, "The inventors' two sketches, `FIG-1.png` and `FIG-2.png`, exist by name only; no image has been seen"; FIG. 2 instructions, "Draw the gap as a plain annular space of uniform width along the length shown; draw no inlet or outlet feature"; description [0026], "The purge gap is shown schematically in FIG. 1, FIG. 2 and FIG. 3".

Attack (examiner and opponent): the package has a figure plan and draftsperson's instructions, not drawings. The drawings a mechanical valve application needs to show the purge gap opening do not yet exist, and the planned FIG. 2 would draw a gap between the outer surface and the bore without showing how it opens or connects (see F-36-1). Drawings cannot be added after filing without added-matter exposure (EPC Art. 123(2); US new-matter bar; UK s.76), so any later amendment on the purge gap's geometry would have no drawing basis. `figures.md` also contains internal working references and draftsperson's notes that are not filing text.

Least narrowing action: obtain the inventors' sketches, prepare formal drawings of FIG. 1 to FIG. 8 from them, with FIG. 1 and FIG. 2 showing the actual purge-gap region and flow path in the rest and over-travel states, and file drawings plus a brief description, not the figure plan.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-36-3 [medium] [support] Only the Presta pattern is disclosed; no Schrader or other valve-standard embodiment

Paragraph: [0027], "In the prototype the valve stem 10 is a conventional brass valve stem of Presta pattern"; [0070], "The term does not itself import any thread form or valve standard"; [0079], "including a bicycle inner tube and a tubeless tyre set-up"; [0123].

Attack (competitor and opponent): claims 1 and 2 are not limited to Presta, but every embodiment is; a competitor launching a sealant-purging Schrader core for mountain bikes or motor vehicles will argue that the functional claims extend beyond what is disclosed, and a later dependent claim to a Schrader core would lack basis.

Least narrowing action: add a paragraph stating that the valve core may be of Schrader pattern or another valve standard, with any structural adaptation the inventors have in mind (for example where the seat ring sits in a Schrader core and how the depressor pin interacts with the valve member), and naming other inflatable bodies with sealant they contemplate.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-36-4 [medium] [support] No range is stated for the normal stroke

Paragraph: [0126], "The normal stroke is 0.6 mm in the prototype; no range is stated for it"; claim 31, "the over-travel is from 0.4 mm to 0.9 mm beyond the end of the normal stroke".

Attack (opponent): every other travel, dimension and pressure has a range; the normal stroke alone has only the prototype value. An opponent argues that the three-range behaviour is disclosed only for a 0.6 mm stroke, and the applicant has no fallback on stroke or on the ratio of over-travel to normal stroke.

Least narrowing action: add the range of normal stroke the inventors believe would work, with endpoints disclosed individually as for the other ranges ([0085]).

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-36-5 [medium] [enablement] Behaviour on manual depression of the valve member and with the lock nut is not stated

Paragraph: [0114], "No statement is made herein as to whether manual depression of the valve member 40, for example to deflate the inflatable body 80, produces over-travel or purge flow 64, or as to whether a closed lock nut 70 prevents over-travel".

Attack (examiner and competitor): pressing the valve member of a Presta core is how the inflatable body is deflated, and each inflation cycle of Example 1 ended with full deflation through the valve ([0099]). Whether that pressing forces the valve member into over-travel, and so deflects and cycles the seat ring with no gas flowing to purge, bears on fatigue, on the rest-state seal, and on whether a competitor's "press to purge" core practises the invention. The description is silent.

Least narrowing action: state what the inventors observed when deflating the prototype by pressing the valve member, and whether the lock nut limits the valve member's travel.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-36-6 [medium] [enablement] No dimensions for the polymer seat ring or the wiper ring

Paragraph: [0093], "The dimensions given in the section on ranges … are not stated for the polymer seat ring"; [0094a], "The thickness of the wiper ring 130, the manner in which the wiper ring 130 is mounted, and the portion of the wiper ring 130 that contacts the sealing face 42 … are not stated herein"; claims 25, 26, 27 to 29, 44, 45, 58 and 60.

Attack (opponent): the second and third embodiments are prophetic and carry no dimension, mounting or wall form beyond the material. A PEEK ring of the NiTi dimensions will not behave as the NiTi ring (the text itself says its recovery is slower and weaker, [0093]); the skilled person is left to design both from scratch, which an opponent will argue is undue experimentation for the claims directed to them.

Least narrowing action: add the dimensions or dimension ranges the inventors contemplate for the polymer seat ring and for the wiper ring, and the mounting of the wiper ring in the core body.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-36-7 [medium] [enablement] The geometry of the recovery wipe is not stated

Paragraph: [0034a], "The direction of that sliding relative to the axis of the valve 92 is not stated herein"; [0055], "The term does not specify the direction of the sliding"; claims 5, 35, 52, 57 and 59.

Attack (opponent): the recovery wipe is one of the two cleaning actions ([0010]) and would be the basis of an independent claim (F-33-1). The text says the upper edge 32 slides across the conical sealing face 42 while both parts move, but not which way, over what distance, or why the edge stays in contact while the valve member returns and the ring contracts. An opponent argues the wipe is asserted rather than taught, and a defendant argues no sliding contact occurs in its product.

Least narrowing action: add the inventors' account of the relative motion in the recovery wipe (direction relative to the axis, the sequence of seat-ring contraction and valve-member return, and the contact path on the sealing face), and for the wiper ring the portion that contacts the sealing face.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-36-8 [low] [enablement] Spring rates and the effective area of the valve member are not stated

Paragraph: [0033], [0063], "a soft first stage … and a stiffer second stage"; [0112], opening pressure 1.0 bar and over-travel threshold 3.5 bar; no spring rate, preload or effective area is stated anywhere in the package.

Attack: the skilled person can derive a spring from the pressures only with the valve member's effective area, which is not given; the gap is small because the pressures and travels are stated, but it leaves no basis for a fallback claim on the spring itself.

Least narrowing action: add the prototype's first- and second-stage spring rates or loads, and the effective area on which the pressure differential acts.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-36-9 [low] [enablement] The thermal purge variant is disclosed without a mechanism

Paragraph: [0096], "warming the valve, for example by hand, opens the purge gap without an inflation device … The thermal purge variant has not been built or tested and no data exist for it; the inventors have not decided whether to pursue it"; [0077].

Attack: the variant states a result (warming opens the purge gap) without how a two-way shape-memory seat ring deflects to open it; it supports no claim, and as published text it is available to competitors as a teaching point.

Least narrowing action: either add the inventors' account of how warming deflects the seat ring and opens the purge gap, if they intend to keep the variant, or leave the paragraph as a recorded variant with counsel's agreement. Deletion is a choice for the human, not proposed here.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

## Assumptions

A1. Common general knowledge (mechanics): a thin tube pressed with interference into a bore and expanded radially outward bears harder on that bore; it does not move away from it unless another region of the tube moves inward or the bore is locally relieved. Neither is stated in the package.

A2. Common general knowledge (patent practice): drawings and technical content cannot be added to an application after filing without added-matter or new-matter exposure (EPC Art. 123(2); UK s.76; US 35 U.S.C. §132); a US continuation-in-part can add matter only with a later effective date for it.

A3. `figures.md` is read as the drawings' current state because no drawing file is in the package.

A4. The saved reference US3417778A is cited only for its claim 1 text.
