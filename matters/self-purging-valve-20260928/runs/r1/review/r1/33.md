Dependent claims affecting enforcement: the recovery-wipe claims 5, 35, 52, 57 and 59 ("slides across the sealing face (42)") are hard to prove on a commercial product (relative sliding of two moving parts, direction not stated, [0034a], [0055]); they are fallback positions for validity, not enforcement claims. The numerical claims 30 to 34, 43 and 47 are provable by bench test because every pressure is a value of the defined pressure differential ([0039]); claim 24 (product-by-process) is effectively unprovable against an imported seat ring without discovery.

### F-33-1 [high] [design-around] Purge gap limited to a second parallel path; enlarging the seat gap on over-travel avoids claims 1, 2 and 4

Claim language: claims 1 and 2, "a purge gap (60), which is a second path for inflation gas … in parallel with the main flow passage"; claim 4, "a purge gap (60) of the valve, separate from the main flow passage". [0046] fixes the meaning: gas through the purge gap "does not pass between the sealing face and the valve seat".

Attack (competitor's engineer): build the same core, but let over-travel of the valve member flex the seat element so that the annulus *between the sealing face and the valve seat* widens and the higher flow scours the seat and sealing face, and let the element snap back and wipe the sealing face on recovery. There is no second path, so no purge gap as defined; every independent claim is avoided while the product keeps both cleaning actions [0010] attributes to the invention. The recovery wipe alone is claimed only in dependent claims (5, 35, 52, 57, 59) that hang on the purge gap.

Least narrowing action: add an independent claim (valve and valve core) to the aspect already written in [0011]: a valve seat, valve member with sealing face, biasing member and deflectable element; the normal stroke above the opening pressure; over-travel above a higher over-travel threshold that deflects the deflectable element; and, as the pressure differential falls, the deflectable element recovering and sliding across the sealing face in the recovery wipe, with no purge gap limitation. Support: [0011] ("This aspect is founded on the recovery wipe; it may be combined with the purge gap"), [0034], [0034a], [0055], [0117]. This is a new claim, not a narrowing. Counsel should weigh that the recovery-wipe claim sits closer to the wiping art saved in the reference set (US4050473A [13], US4236547A [6]), in which the wiping part is carried on the valve head; the distinguishing feature is the over-travel-driven deflection with recovery on pressure fall.

New disclosure required: no. Suggested target: P5.

### F-33-2 [high] [design-around] "Undeflected" in ranges (a) and (b) is an absolute limitation avoided by a light preload

Claim language: claims 1 and 2, "(a) … the deflectable element is undeflected and the purge gap is closed" and "(b) … the deflectable element remains undeflected and the purge gap remains closed"; claim 4, "while the deflectable element remains undeflected and a purge gap (60) … remains closed".

Attack (competitor's engineer and litigation counsel): set the purge shoulder in light contact with the deflectable element at rest, or let it touch it part-way through the stroke, so the element carries a small deflection from the start while its purge gap stays shut until the high threshold. The product behaves as the invention; it is literally outside all three independent claims, and the patentee must prove a negative ("undeflected") against micron-level strain evidence. In the US, equivalents may be argued but are exposed to estoppel if the word is relied on in prosecution; in the UK and at the EPO the Actavis/Art. 69 Protocol analysis is available but uncertain. The functional point of ranges (a) and (b) is that no purge flow passes, and that is what the purge-gap clause already says.

Least narrowing action: delete "the deflectable element is undeflected and" from (a), "the deflectable element remains undeflected and" from (b) in claims 1 and 2, and "the deflectable element remains undeflected and" from claim 4, keeping "the purge gap is closed / remains closed". Support: [0044] and [0046] state purge-gap closure in the rest state and throughout the normal stroke; [0134] ("where features are described together they are also disclosed separately"). EPO intermediate-generalisation risk is moderate, since [0009] and [0041] state both features together; the "undeflected" wording can stay in a dependent claim as a fallback. If counsel prefers to keep the independent claims as they are, the dependent fallback is the reverse: add a dependent claim reciting that the deflectable element may carry a deflection during the normal stroke that does not open the purge gap, but that needs new disclosure and is not proposed here.

New disclosure required: no. Suggested target: P5.

### F-33-3 [medium] [design-around] Method claim 4 is performed by the rider, so it reaches a competitor only through indirect infringement

Claim language: claim 4, "the method comprising, in one inflation of the inflatable body through the valve by an inflation device (84): applying, by the inflation device, a pressure differential across the valve"; and dependent claims 52 to 56.

Attack (litigation counsel): the competitor makes and sells valve cores; it performs no step of claim 4. The direct infringer is each rider, whose pump may or may not exceed the over-travel threshold ([0078], [0113]: "no statement is made herein that every inflation device reaches the over-travel threshold"). Against the competitor the claim needs inducement or contributory infringement (US), or supply of means relating to an essential element with knowledge (UK s.60(2) and national EPC-state equivalents), each dependent on knowledge and intent and on proof of a user's inflation above threshold. The claim has almost no independent enforcement value; it is not a defect in validity.

Least narrowing action: none to claim 4. Keep the apparatus claims 1, 2, 3 and 48 as the enforcement claims, and treat the method as a supplementary claim for counsel's weighing (for example against a pump-and-valve kit seller whose instructions direct inflation above the threshold). No reformulation is proposed.

New disclosure required: no. Suggested target: P5.

### F-33-4 [medium] [design-around] Deflection only by the valve member's over-travel; deflection by gas pressure or by the pump head avoids the claims

Claim language: claims 1 and 2, "(c) … the over-travel of the valve member deflects the deflectable element"; claim 4, "the over-travel of the valve member deflecting the deflectable element".

Attack (competitor's engineer): open the purge path by a resilient element that yields directly to the gas pressure above a threshold, or by a pin in the pump head, with the valve member never bearing on it. The product purges on high-pressure inflation and is outside the claims. The package discloses only valve-member-driven deflection ([0043], [0048], [0116]), and the thermal purge variant ([0096]) is the only other opening mechanism named. Broadening the claim towards gas-pressure-driven deflection would move it towards US3417778A claim 1, whose seat is "radially expandable under fluid pressure", so the gap cannot be closed by reformulation without cost to validity.

Least narrowing action: none within the present text. If the inventors have conceived a pressure-driven or pump-actuated opening of the purge gap, it would have to be disclosed as a further embodiment and assessed against US3417778A before any claim is drafted to it; otherwise counsel should record this as accepted residual scope. Narrowing is not proposed.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P3.

### F-33-5 [medium] [design-around] Purge flow and purge-gap closure are provable only by a bench test the description does not describe

Claim language: claims 1 and 2, "purge flow (64) of the inflation gas passes through the purge gap" and "the deflectable element recovers and the purge gap closes"; claim 4, the corresponding steps. [0046] defines "Closed" as "does not admit purge flow"; [0132a] states that "no measurement herein isolates the purge gap".

Attack (litigation counsel): the defendant's expert tests a sectioned or instrumented sample and says either that no measurable flow passes the purge gap (so no purge flow) or that some flow passes it at rest (so it is never "closed" and never "recovers"). The patentee has no test method in the specification to point to, and the court construes "closed" and "purge flow" on the evidence each side brings. Detectability from a commercial product is low: the behaviour exists only during inflation above the threshold.

Least narrowing action: add to the description a test by which purge flow through the purge gap and purge-gap closure at rest are identified on a valve core (for example, a leak or flow measurement with the valve member held at a stated position, or a tracer through the purge gap region), with the acceptance criterion the inventors used or would use. That is added disclosure, not a claim change, and it must come from the inventors; nothing in the package states such a test.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P3.

### F-33-6 [low] [design-around] Recovery-wipe dependent claims are hard to prove on a commercial product

Claim language: claims 5, 35 and 52, "the deflectable element slides across the sealing face (42) of the valve member (40) in a recovery wipe"; claims 57 and 59, "the upper edge (32) of the seat ring slides across the sealing face (42)". [0034a] and [0055]: the direction of sliding is not stated and both parts are moving.

Attack: proving relative sliding contact between two parts that are both moving, inside a closed core, needs high-speed imaging or wear-mark analysis; a defendant will argue the parts separate before any sliding. These claims are validity fallbacks, not enforcement claims.

Least narrowing action: none to the claims; if F-33-1 is taken up, state in the description the observable evidence of the recovery wipe the inventors rely on (for example the audible snap on removal of the inflation device already reported in [0102] and [0115], or wear or witness marks on the sealing face), so that a court has a disclosed proxy. Only the snap is in the package now.

New disclosure required: no. Suggested target: P5.
