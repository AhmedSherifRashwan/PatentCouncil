### F-40a-1 [medium] [formal] No drawing sheets exist, though the description relies on eight figures

**Paragraphs cited.** [0018] to [0025] describe FIG. 1 to FIG. 8. [0026] says "The figures are schematic". The package's `figures.md` and `registry.md` are a figure plan and drawing instructions. They direct that the purge gap region be "matched to the inventors' sketch `FIG-1.png` once supplied".

**Attack.** In the US, drawings are required where necessary to understand the invention (35 U.S.C. § 113). The description relies on them throughout, for example [0027] "Referring to FIG. 1". At the EPO, drawings referred to but missing are handled under Rule 56: either the reference is deleted, or the application is re-dated to the date the drawings are filed. In the UK, a missing drawing is dealt with under s.15(5) with the same re-dating consequence.

This is a formal gap only. It must be closed before the filing date is fixed.

**Least narrowing action.** Prepare FIG. 1 to FIG. 8 from the registry.md instructions, reconciled with the inventors' sketches FIG-1.png and FIG-2.png. File them with the description on the same date. No new disclosure is needed beyond the sketches. Target: P4.

### F-40a-2 [medium] [formal] The claim set is not usable in the US as drafted

**Claim language.** Claim 8 "The valve of any one of claims 1 to 7" refers to claim 6 ("The valve of claim 1 or claim 5"), which is itself multiple dependent. The same pattern runs through, for example:
- claim 17 ("any one of claims 14 to 16");
- claim 30 ("any one of claims 1 to 29");
- claim 48 ("any one of claims 2 and 35 to 47");
- claim 54 ("any one of claims 4, 52 and 53").

**Attack.** Under 35 U.S.C. § 112(e) and 37 CFR 1.75(c), a multiple dependent claim may not serve as a basis for another multiple dependent claim. Such claims are objected to and not examined on the merits. Each multiple dependent claim also attracts a fee and counts, for fee purposes, as the number of claims it refers to.

The EPO and the UK accept the structure.

**Least narrowing action.** File a US-specific claim set. Keep the EPO/UK set unchanged. In the US set, give each dependent claim a single parent, chosen by counsel for commercial value, and add further single-dependent claims only where a combination matters. Claim 8 is shown in Correction 2 as an example. No new disclosure is needed. Target: P5.

### F-40a-3 [low] [formal] No abstract

**Location.** The package contains no `abstract.md`.

**Attack.** An abstract is required in all three offices (37 CFR 1.72(b); EPC Art. 78(1)(e) and Rule 47; UK s.14(2)(c)). In the US it must be at most 150 words, on a separate sheet. The missing abstract does not affect the filing date at the EPO or the UK IPO, but it will draw a formalities objection.

**Least narrowing action.** Write the abstract before filing. Ideally it summarises claim 1 with the reference signs of FIG. 2, which the EPO commonly publishes with the abstract. No new disclosure is needed. Target: P4.

### F-40a-4 [low] [formal] Lettered paragraph numbers

**Paragraphs cited.** "[0034a]", "[0094a]", "[0132a]".

**Attack.** US 37 CFR 1.52(b)(6) calls for paragraphs numbered consecutively with four Arabic numerals in square brackets. Lettered inserts are a sign of revision and should not appear in a filed specification. The EPO and the UK do not mandate numbering, so consecutive renumbering is harmless there.

**Least narrowing action.** Renumber [0001] to [0137] consecutively. Update every internal cross-reference to a paragraph. The description refers to paragraphs by section name, not by number, so none is expected. See Corrections 4 to 6. No new disclosure is needed. In place.

### F-40a-5 [low] [formal] FIG. 4A and FIG. 4B each combine two different views

**Paragraph cited.** [0021]: "FIG. 4A shows, in axial section and in end view, the seat ring of the first embodiment alone ...; FIG. 4B shows in the same way". The registry.md instruction reads: "for each, two views side by side on one sheet".

**Attack.** 37 CFR 1.84(u)(1) requires different views to be numbered as separate figures. A capital-letter suffix is reserved for partial views that together form one complete view. EPC Rule 46(2)(h) likewise numbers each figure separately. An axial section and an end view are different views.

**Least narrowing action.** Drop the end views. They add only the diameter D, which the axial section already shows. See Corrections 7 to 9. The alternative is to renumber the views as FIG. 4A to FIG. 4D and update [0021], [0093] and [0095]. No new disclosure is needed. In place.

### F-40a-6 [low] [formal] Claims 57 to 60 are out of their groups

**Claim language.**
- "57. The valve of claim 20,"
- "58. The valve of any one of claims 27 to 29,"
- "59. The valve core of claim 42,"
- "60. The valve core of claim 45,"

**Attack.** EPC Rule 43(4) requires dependent claims to be grouped together as far as possible. Claims 57 to 60 follow the inflatable-body and method claims, away from the valve and valve-core groups they belong to. This draws a formal objection at the EPO and makes the set harder to read everywhere.

**Least narrowing action.** Move each claim next to its parent and renumber:
- claim 57 after claim 24;
- claim 58 after claim 29;
- claim 59 after claim 43;
- claim 60 after claim 45.

Then update every dependency reference. See Correction 10. No new disclosure is needed. In place.

### F-40a-7 [low] [formal] "Inflation gas" carries no reference sign in the claims

**Claim language.** Claim 1: "along which inflation gas passes the valve seat". Claim 2: the same. Claim 4: "and inflation gas from the inflation device passing the valve seat".

**Attack.** Under EPC Rule 43(7), features shown in the drawings should carry their reference signs in the claims. Inflation gas is numbered 66 in FIG. 2 and in the registry. Every other claimed component with a numeral carries it, so this is an inconsistency.

**Least narrowing action.** Add "(66)" at the first mention in each independent claim. See Corrections 11 to 13. No new disclosure is needed. In place.

### F-40a-8 [low] [formal] Two claimed features are not shown in any figure

**Claim language.** Claim 51: "the inflatable body (80) is a bicycle inner tube or a tubeless tyre set-up". Claim 28: "is deflected outward by the purge shoulder (44)". Claim 45 has the same wording as claim 28.

**Drawing instructions.** FIG. 8 draws only a bicycle inner tube. FIG. 3 draws the over-travel position of the valve member 40 in broken line, but the wiper ring 130 only undeflected.

**Attack.** 37 CFR 1.83(a) requires every claimed feature to be shown. Conventional features may be shown as a labelled representation. Without that, the US examiner objects and requires a drawing correction.

**Least narrowing action.**
- In FIG. 3, add the wiper ring 130 deflected outward, in broken line, with the over-travel position.
- In FIG. 8, add a labelled rectangular box for the tubeless tyre set-up as the alternative inflatable body 80.

Both are within [0094] and [0113]. See Corrections 14 and 15. No new disclosure is needed. In place.

### F-40a-9 [low] [formal] The claims have no heading

**Claim language.** claims.md begins "1. A valve (92) for an inflatable body (80)".

**Attack.** The US requires the claims to begin on a separate sheet (37 CFR 1.75(h)). A heading or introductory phrase is customary (MPEP 608.01(m)). The EPO and UK expect a "Claims" heading on a separate page.

**Least narrowing action.** Add the heading "CLAIMS" above claim 1. See Correction 16. No new disclosure is needed. In place.
