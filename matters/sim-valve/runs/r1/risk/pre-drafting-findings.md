**Counts:** 20 findings: 3 critical, 11 high, 4 medium, 2 low. Owners: P4 5, P5 2, P4+P5 13.

**The five that matter most**

1. **PF-1 (critical, novelty).** ref-02 anticipates N0 unless the independent claim recites both pillars: a travel of the poppet beyond the normal stroke, and a purge gap that is a flow path separate from the main passage.
2. **PF-2 (critical, novelty/inventive step).** INDEX #15, a tyre-mounted valve, already has a ring pushed radially outward to open a gap that exists only above an excess pressure. A purely functional "flow path opened by deformation" claim reads onto it. The independent claim must recite that the poppet itself bears on the element in over-travel.
3. **PF-3 (critical, enablement/support).** The main distinction, the purge gap, is not enabled as disclosed. The interference fit and the purge gap sit on the same outer surface, and the disclosure names two different bores (§2 "stem bore" and §3 core body bore). It also never says where the purge air leaves.
4. **PF-4 (high, enablement/credibility).** A 0.15 mm radial opening on a 1.35 mm outer radius implies about 11 % hoop strain (12.r1 credibility check). A hostile expert will call that not credible for the seat ring as described unless the description explains the bending mechanism, or the figure turns out to be diametral.
5. **PF-5 (high, inventive step).** The strongest combination is ref-02 plus INDEX #8, #2 or #15. The only answer is a technical effect the specification proves: the purge gap sweeps surfaces the main passage does not. That rests on the bench note about where the cured film remained and on the C-1..C-3 versus P-1..P-3 table.

The drafters must answer every critical and high finding in a response table.

## Findings

### PF-1 — ref-02 anticipates any nucleus claim that omits either pillar

- **Category:** novelty · **Severity:** critical · **Owner:** P4+P5
- **Concerns:** independent claims of every category; the description's definitions of normal stroke, over-travel, main passage and purge gap.
- **Evidence:** 08 N0: ref-02 claim 1 (a ring that seals and deflects), [0019] (lip "deflects outward to enlarge the flow passage"), claim 4 ("deflectable by the poppet during opening"), [0020] (returns under its own elasticity). 09 rows 1–2 and Rationale: without the separate purge gap [0019] reads on; without the second travel range claim 4 reads on.
- **Risk:** a claim reciting only "an element deflected by the poppet to open a gap and recovering to close it" is anticipated by a single reference in the same field.
- **Drafting response required:**
  - P5: the independent claim recites (a) a normal stroke and a further over-travel of the same poppet, with the radially deflectable element loaded only in the over-travel; and (b) a purge gap that is a secondary flow path, distinct from the main passage past the sealing edge, closed at rest.
  - P4: give express definitions of "normal stroke", "over-travel", "main passage" and "purge gap". All four are marked "needs definition" in the lock. The definitions must make the purge gap distinct from the main passage by where it runs and which surfaces it sweeps, not by its size. A drafted definition must not let the main passage enlarged by deflection count as a purge gap.
  - Least narrowing: neither pillar needs the convoluted wall, NiTi alloy, the two-rate characteristic or any value.

### PF-2 — INDEX #15 reads on a functional "deformation opens a secondary gap above a threshold" claim

- **Category:** novelty · **Severity:** critical · **Owner:** P4+P5
- **Concerns:** the independent claim if it follows 09 row 1's suggestion to claim the purge gap purely functionally ("a secondary flow path opened by radial expansion of the seat ring"), and the load-path wording of 09 row 3.
- **Evidence:** INDEX #15 (Litigator, D1a) is a tyre-mounted relief valve, with inflation through the valve in some embodiments. Its claim 2 has an "elastomeric ring being elastically deformed by flow of fluid issuing thereagainst from said radial nozzle". Its gap-type claim recites a gap "between said external sealing surface and a portion of said ring-shaped seal" with means "for deforming said seal to promote widening of said gap". In its bypass embodiment, poppet 144 has a bypass passage 180 that opens only when "excess pressure will force poppet 144 upwardly", and springs 221, 221′, 221″ give selectable loadings. 08 was written before this reference was saved and does not rate it.
- **Risk:** INDEX #15 supplies, in one tyre-valve reference, three things: a ring deformed radially outward, a gap that is closed at rest, and an opening only above a higher pressure. Combined with ref-02's poppet-deflected seat, the examiner has most of N0 without hindsight. INDEX #15 differs in four ways. Its ring is deformed by a fluid jet, not by an axial load from the poppet. Its gap lies between the poppet and the ring, not between the ring and a surrounding bore. Its flow runs in the relief (outward) direction. It has no cleaning purpose. Only the first two of those differences are structural.
- **Drafting response required:**
  - P5: the independent claim recites that the poppet, or a load-applying surface moved by it, bears on the radially deflectable element during over-travel, and that this axial load produces the radial expansion that opens the purge gap. Keep the purge shoulder "below the sealing face" and the inflow direction ("air at pump pressure into the tyre chamber") for dependents. The load path is enough to distinguish, and reciting inflow in the independent claim gives up DA-18 for no gain.
  - P4: state expressly that the purge gap is opened by the mechanical load of the poppet in over-travel, and that it opens regardless of how fast the pump delivers air (09 row 3 effect). Describe the purge gap as bounded by the seat ring outer surface and a surrounding bore, in contrast to a gap at the sealing face.
  - Tension to record: this wording gives up DA-10 and DA-14 (no deformable element) in the apparatus claim. See PF-7.

### PF-3 — The purge gap is not enabled as disclosed

- **Category:** enablement · **Severity:** critical · **Owner:** P4
- **Concerns:** the purge gap passages, the FIG-2 description, and every claim reciting the purge gap or its boundary.
- **Evidence:** 03 Q2 and A5; 09 A1, Q2 and disclosure_requests ("Row 1 … is not enabled as described"); 01 M2. The seat ring is press-fitted with about 0.03 mm diametral interference (§7), yet §3 item 6 opens the purge gap between that same outer surface and the core body bore. §2 says "stem bore". CONFIRMED (12.r1 purge gap items 1–3, locating shoulder item 1) offers a relieved core body bore, a gap bounded by the valve stem bore, purge flow through the wall, and axial clamping. Each carries "requires inventor confirmation", and gate-2.md records that the gate did not resolve this.
- **Risk:** the lead distinction (09 row 1) fails for enablement or sufficiency. That is EPO Art 83, US §112(a) and UK s14(3). Two different boundary bores also create a clarity and added-matter trap when the claims are later amended.
- **Drafting response required:**
  - P4: describe the purge gap at the level the record supports: "a bore surrounding the seat ring", with the core body bore as the preferred boundary and the valve stem bore as disclosed in §2.
  - Describe how the fit band and the free portion coexist only in the candidate language CONFIRMED uses: the relieved core body bore, axial clamping against the locating shoulder, and flow through the wall. Mark each as an embodiment, not as the inventor's tested construction.
  - Describe the exit of the purge air into the tyre chamber only as far as §2 and §3 item 6 state it.
  - Raise a `disclosure_requests` entry for the inventor's actual construction. Do not invent one. Until it arrives, P5 claims the purge gap boundary as "a bore surrounding the seat ring", not as the core body bore.

### PF-4 — The disclosed opening implies a hoop strain a hostile expert will contest

- **Category:** enablement · **Severity:** high · **Owner:** P4+P5
- **Concerns:** the §4 purge gap width 0.15 mm (range 0.10–0.25 mm, "radial"), the seat ring dimensions, and every range claim on purge gap width.
- **Evidence:** CONFIRMED, 12.r1 purge gap, "Credibility check": 0.15 mm radial at an outer radius of 1.35 mm is about 11 % hoop strain if produced by hoop expansion alone, above the recoverable strain usually cited for superelastic NiTi alloy. The same check notes that bending-dominated geometries avoid this. The lock defines purge gap width as "Radial size of the purge gap when fully open".
- **Risk:** an examiner or opponent argues that the prototype as described cannot work, which attacks enablement of N4–N6. The figure is also internally ambiguous: radial or diametral.
- **Drafting response required:**
  - P4: describe the radial expansion as produced by flattening of the convolution under axial load (§3 item 3: axial load "becomes radial expansion"), which is a bending mechanism, and not as uniform hoop stretching. State the purge gap width with the measurement basis left as the inventor states it. Do not convert it to diametral.
  - Raise a `disclosure_requests` entry asking the inventor to confirm the basis.
  - P5: no range claim on purge gap width in any claim above N5 (PF-11).

### PF-6 — The second-threshold pillar invites the "purge on every opening" design-around

- **Category:** design-around · **Severity:** high · **Owner:** P4+P5
- **Concerns:** the over-travel limitation in the independent claim; the over-travel differential; DA-2 and DA-15.
- **Evidence:** CONFIRMED DA-2: the purge shoulder touches the seat ring at the end of the normal stroke, so the purge opens on every opening ("Needs inventor confirmation (03 Q6; 01 M7)"). DA-15: a plunger in the pump head drives the poppet into over-travel mechanically. 09 row 2 rates design-around risk as high. The same limitation is one of the PF-1 pillars.
- **Risk:** the limitation that gives novelty is also the one a competitor removes first. If the independent claim recites a pressure threshold ("over-travel differential greater than the crack pressure"), mechanical actuation escapes it too.
- **Drafting response required:**
  - P5, least narrowing: define over-travel structurally in the independent claim, as a travel range beyond the normal stroke in which, and only in which, the load-applying surface bears on the radially deflectable element. Put "over-travel differential greater than the crack pressure" in the first dependent claim. That keeps the PF-1 distinction over ref-02, because in ref-02 the seat is deflected during ordinary opening, and still reaches DA-15 if the inventor confirms mechanical actuation.
  - P4: describe the normal stroke and over-travel as two travel ranges and the pressure trigger as the preferred actuation. Describe mechanical actuation and a purge threshold equal to the crack pressure only if the inventor confirms them.
  - Scope given up if not confirmed: DA-2 stays outside, and the report says so.

### PF-9 — The convoluted wall is weaker as a distinction than 08 rated

- **Category:** novelty (inventive step) · **Severity:** high · **Owner:** P4+P5
- **Concerns:** N2; 09 row 4; 10 item 3 (seat ring as a component).
- **Evidence:** INDEX #17 (Litigator, D1a) describes a poppet check valve whose seat is a resilient metal element with an axially extending sidewall and a "wavy or undulating shape … which facilitates the resiliency" (description, resilient metal seal 405), engaged by a poppet. - **Drafting response required:**
  - P5: keep the convoluted wall out of the independent claim. Claim the seat ring component only with the functional relationship (axial load at the sealing edge produces radial expansion that opens the purge gap), never on geometry alone.
  - P4: describe a "load-converting geometry" genus, with the convoluted wall as the preferred species and pre-bowed wall, slotted sleeve and strut pattern as candidate species. Distinguish INDEX #17 by effect: its undulation preloads a seal and does not open a flow path.
