Both requester files are marked FICTIONAL test references; they are charted as supplied and are not real publications. Google Patents text carries no paragraph numbers for the fetched references, so locations are given as abstract, claim number, or the element numeral in the description. This is a drafting and analysis aid; qualified patent counsel signs off on anything filed.

Row key (from `03`):
- **N0.1** poppet has a normal stroke above the crack pressure and a further over-travel reached only above a higher over-travel differential.
- **N0.2** in over-travel the poppet bears on a radially deflectable element that converts axial load into radial deflection.
- **N0.3** that deflection opens a purge gap, a secondary flow path beside the main passage, through which air at pump pressure sweeps the surfaces where sealant collects (air purge).
- **N0.4** on collapse of the differential the element shows shape recovery and the purge gap closes, so the tyre valve seals at rest.
- **N1** the deflectable element is the seat ring itself, the sealing face seating on its sealing edge.
- **N2** the seat ring is a short tube with a convoluted wall (one or two convolutions); the purge gap lies between the seat ring outer surface and the core body bore.
- **N3** purge shoulder on the poppet below the sealing face contacts the seat ring only in over-travel; return spring with a two-rate characteristic.
- **N4** superelastic NiTi alloy seat ring, austenite finish temperature below 5 °C; snap-back wipe across the sealing face.
- **N5** the §4 operating window (ranges).
- **N6** prototype: laser cutting of NiTi tube, shape-setting, quenching, electropolishing, press-fitting against the locating shoulder with an interference fit.
- **F** field: valve core of a tyre valve fouled by sealant film.

## Rationale

The rows follow `03` exactly so that `08` can cite a row per rung. N0 was split into four rows because the references hit its parts separately: US4044791A hits the second threshold and the secondary path, ref-02 hits deflection and recovery, and no reference hits the combination.

The two fictional requester references were charted with the same care as the fetched ones because they are the closest on this set; ref-02 in particular is the strongest single-reference threat to an N0 claim that does not distinguish the purge gap from the main passage. The reviewer notes inside the fictional files were read, but every status is based on the quoted summary text, not on those notes.

For ref-02, N0.2 is split "explicit (pressure) / ambiguous (poppet)" because claim 4 says the lip is deflectable by the poppet during opening: a hostile reading equates that with N0.2. Leaving it as absent would understate the risk.

US4044791A N0.1 is marked explicit only as "two pressure regimes"; the fetched text quoted does not settle whether one poppet travels beyond a normal stroke, so that part is ambiguous. I did not overstate the mechanism.

References that hit only field or material were charted compactly, with absent rows grouped, rather than repeated row by row; the grouping is explicit so no row is silently skipped.

## Open Questions

Q1. Coverage is thin: no search of CN, TW, DE or JP utility models, of Espacenet or USPTO full text, or of product literature for sealant-resistant valve cores. A professional search is warranted before filing. Blocks P4, P5 (claim scope rests on this set).
Q2. Can the inventor confirm that over-travel happens only above an over-travel differential higher than the crack pressure (03 Q6)? If over-travel occurs on every opening, ref-02 claim 4 comes much closer to N0. Blocks P2, P5.
Q3. Does the purge gap open a flow path distinct from the main passage past the sealing edge, and where does the purge air exit (03 Q2)? This is the main distinction over ref-02 [0019]. Blocks P2, P4, P5.
Q4. Does the effect need the snap-back wipe, or does the air purge alone suffice (03 Q1)? This decides how much weight the wipe distinction over US3473561A and US4050473A must carry. Blocks P3, P5.
