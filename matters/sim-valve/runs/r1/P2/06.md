### Search Concept Decomposition

This is a search strategy, a drafting and analysis aid only; it is not a search opinion, and qualified patent counsel decides whether the resulting picture is adequate for filing. The strings below were composed from generic vocabulary; the ones marked "run" in `prior-art/search-log.md` have been executed in the RES pass, and the results are in `prior-art/INDEX.md`.

**Concept list (the yardstick for every track).** Terms follow P1/01 (the terminology lock does not yet exist).
- C1 — over-travel: the poppet travels beyond its normal stroke when the pressure differential exceeds a second threshold set by a two-rate return spring (P1/01 (b) feature 1; disclosure §3 item 5).
- C2 — a seat ring that converts axial load from the purge shoulder into radial expansion, via a convoluted (wave) wall (P1/01 (b) feature 2; §3 item 3).
- C3 — the purge gap: a secondary annular flow path, closed at rest, opened by that expansion, through which inflation air flushes sealant into the tube (§2, §3 item 6).
- C4 — elastic recovery of the seat ring on pump removal that wipes the sealing face of the poppet (P1/01 (b) feature 3; §2).
- C5 — superelastic NiTi as the seat ring material, Af below riding temperature (§4, §5).
- C6 — field and problem: tyre inflation valve core fouled by liquid latex sealant (§1).
- C7 — manufacture: laser-cut NiTi tube, shape-set in a fixture, quench, electropolish, interference press fit against a shoulder (§7).

#### Track 1 — Core architecture

**Concept statement.** A tyre valve core in which a pressure-staged second travel range of the poppet deforms the seat element to open a secondary flow path that flushes the seat region, and the seat element recovers elastically on closing (C1 + C2 + C3, with C4).

**Coverage note.** In the RES pass (2026-09-29) nothing was found that reads on C1 + C2 + C3 together; the closest art sits in Track 3 (industrial self-cleaning and floating-seat check valves) and Track 6 (superelastic seals). Chinese, Taiwanese, German and Japanese utility models and non-patent product literature were not reached. Coverage is thin, and a professional search on Tracks 1 and 3 is warranted before filing.

## Rationale

Tracks were cut along the three features P1/01 (b) names as carrying the concept, with the problem (C6) and the material (C5) kept separate so that an examiner's likely combination — a tyre-valve problem reference, a self-cleaning industrial valve, and a superelastic seal — can be searched leg by leg. Functional equivalents include the inventor's own Alternatives A and B, because a competitor's design-around will most likely use a polymer seat or a separate wiper ring. The CPC codes marked uncertain were chosen from memory of the scheme and were not verified against the current CPC text in this pass. The machine-readable queries avoid the disclosure's own phrasing ("purge gap", "over-travel" combined with sealant) in favour of generic vocabulary, to keep the searches from narrating the invention to a third party.
