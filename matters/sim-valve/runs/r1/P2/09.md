## Rationale

The first two rows answer the lead objection in `08` (ref-02 alone against N0) and must both be in the independent claim: without the separate purge gap, ref-02 [0019] reads on; without the second threshold, ref-02 claim 4 reads on. Row 3 answers the pressure- and heat-driven expansion references and ties the other two together structurally.

The bench observation that cured film remained only on the ring outer surface in the purge-gap region, with a clean sealing edge and poppet face, is the only evidence that the purge gap reaches a place the main passage does not. It is cited for row 1 and must be carried into the description.

The superelastic material and the snap-back wipe are kept as separate rows because `08` rated them only moderate and the evidence does not isolate the wipe. Folding them into the independent claim would narrow it past Alt-A and still leave an obvious-substitution attack.

The effect row (tool-free cleaning over 40 cycles) is placed low because it is result language; its value is as the technical effect in the EPO problem-solution approach and as the reason the combination is not a mere aggregation.
