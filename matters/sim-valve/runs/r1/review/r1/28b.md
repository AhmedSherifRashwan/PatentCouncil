### F-28-1 [high] [enablement] Where and in which direction the axial load is applied, and how it becomes an outward movement, is not disclosed and is stated inconsistently

Claim language: claim 1, "applies an axial load that produces a radial expansion of the radially deflectable element"; claim 3, "produces through the load-converting geometry a radial expansion of the wall"; claim 22, "the axial load flattens the at least one convolution".

Attack. The description states the result and never the mechanics. (a) No paragraph says on which surface of the seat ring 30 the purge shoulder 44 bears, in which axial direction the load acts, or what reacts it. [0113] puts the load "for example at its upper edge", which is the sealing edge 32, but in the over-travel the poppet has left the sealing edge by the whole normal stroke ([0116], [0137]). The only statement of the contact location is the FIG. 2 instruction ("touching the seat ring (30) at the fit-band end"), which is not in the description and which places the load at the end seated against the locating shoulder 24. (b) [0134] and [0170] say the convolution "flattens" under the axial load and the wall "moves outward"; [0172] says a single outward bow "increases under axial load in the manner of a pre-buckled column" and that this bow "corresponds to the convoluted wall 34 with one convolution 36". The same geometry is said to flatten and to deepen under the same load. (c) [0237] states a fully open purge gap width of 0.15 mm arising "from flattening of the convolution 36", whose amplitude is 0.12 mm ([0234]); the stated ranges keep the same relation (0.10–0.25 mm against 0.08–0.20 mm). A flattening of a wave cannot move the wall further than the height of the wave. An examiner or opponent will put this as insufficiency under 35 U.S.C. 112(a), Article 83 EPC and section 14(3) of the UK Act: the skilled person is told that three prototypes worked but not what the load path was. The gap cannot be filled after filing without adding matter.

Least narrowing action. Add disclosure, from the inventor's record of the built prototypes, of: the surface of the seat ring on which the purge shoulder bears; the direction of the load relative to the locating shoulder; whether the wall is in axial compression or tension in the over-travel; whether each convolution is inward or outward at rest; and which of "flattening" and "increase of the bow" applies to which geometry. Correct [0113] and reconcile [0134], [0170], [0172] and [0237]. No claim narrowing is needed.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-28-2 [high] [enablement] An outward movement of the wall is said to open a gap between the wall and the bore that surrounds it, while the ring is held in that bore by an interference fit

Claim language: claim 16, "the purge gap is bounded by an outer surface of the radially deflectable element and by a bore surrounding the radially deflectable element"; claim 17; claim 26, "a free portion (37) which, on the radial expansion, expands into a core body bore relief (202)"; claims 58 and 65.

Attack. [0081] defines radial expansion as "an outward movement of the wall". [0124] places the purge gap 60 between the outer surface of the seat ring 30 and the core body bore 22 and says it "is open when the seat ring 30 has undergone the radial expansion". An outward movement of a wall towards a surrounding bore reduces the clearance between them; the description does not say why here it creates one. The same ring is "pressed into the core body 20 with a light interference fit" ([0112]), that is, with no clearance at rest. The only construction that addresses this is the fit band 35, free portion 37 and core body bore relief 202 of [0186]–[0187], where the gap is closed "by line contact at one end of the core body bore relief 202" and opens when the free portion "expands into" the relief; how expansion into the relief breaks the line contact is not stated, and [0202] says this construction is "not stated to be the construction of the valve cores tested". The construction of the tested prototypes in this respect is therefore not disclosed at all. For the principal embodiment, on which every piece of data rests, the skilled person is not told how the gap is closed at rest, where air enters it, or where it leaves.

Least narrowing action. Add disclosure of the actual construction of prototypes P-1 to P-3 at the outer surface of the seat ring: the extent of the interference fit, the clearance at rest, the line or surface that closes the purge gap at rest, how the radial expansion unseats it, and the entry and exit of the purge flow. State the same for the relief construction of [0187]. If the inventor's record shows the wall moving inwards or tilting at the closing line, say so and adjust [0081]. No claim narrowing is needed.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-28-3 [high] [support] The two-piece seat has no disclosed purge gap, although claim 1 reaches it through reference signs 142 and 146

[0031], [0181]–[0183], [0293]–[0295] and Statement 7.1 ([0347]) say only that the purge shoulder 44 "deflects" the wiper ring 144 "outward" and that the wiper ring "wipes on its return". None says that the deflection opens a flow path, where that path lies, that it is closed at rest and throughout the normal stroke, or that it is distinct from the main passage. The only text is the FIG. 9 instruction ("purge gap (146) as a closed clearance between the wiper ring (144) and the core body bore (22)") and the general definition [0099]. The two-piece seat is the one described species in which the sealing edge is not on the deflected element ([0164]); it is what carries the genus "radially deflectable element" beyond a seat ring, and it is the first construction a competitor would adopt to separate sealing from purging. As drafted, claim 1 is supported for that species by a definition and a drawing instruction only, and no fallback claim to it can be written from the description.

Least narrowing action. Add to [0181]–[0183], and to Statement 7.1, that in the over-travel the deflection of the wiper ring 144 opens a purge gap 146, with its boundaries, its closed state at rest, its relation to the main passage and the path of the air purge through it; use numeral 146 in the description. Add a dependent claim to the two-piece seat once the text exists.

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-28-4 [medium] [enablement] The snap-back wipe is claimed as a movement "across the sealing face" at a time when the description places the sealing face away from the seat ring

New disclosure required: yes. Suggested target: P4.

### F-28-5 [medium] [support] For the slotted sleeve, the split seat ring and the tapered seat ring, the purge gap and its closed state at rest are not disclosed

Claim language: claim 40, "a slotted sleeve (170) having fingers (172)"; claim 42, "a split seat ring (180) having an axial split (182)"; claim 43, "a tapered seat ring (190)"; each with claim 1, "closed at rest and throughout the normal stroke".

Attack. [0176] makes the axial slots between the fingers "a route for the purge flow when the fingers 172 are spread", but the slots exist at rest and nothing closes them.
