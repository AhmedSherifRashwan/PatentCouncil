Independent claims: 1 (valve core), 2 (method of cleaning), 3 (seat ring), 4 (method of manufacturing, by reference to claim 3). ### F-33-1 [high] [design-around] No independent claim covers cleaning by deflection of the seat without a secondary flow path

Claim language attacked. Claim 1: "the radial expansion opening a purge gap (60, 146)" and "the purge gap is a secondary flow path that is distinct from the main passage". Claim 2: "passing air through the open purge gap into the tyre chamber". Claim 3: "the radial expansion opens a purge gap (60) that is distinct from a main passage (26)". The snap-back wipe appears only in dependent claims 32, 56 and 76.

Anchor. [0064]: a flow path between the sealing face and the sealing edge is part of the main passage "whether or not that flow path is enlarged by a deflection of the element on which the poppet seats". [0126]: "A main passage 26 that is made larger, whether by further travel of the poppet 40 or by deflection of the seat ring 30, remains the main passage 26 and is not a purge gap 60." [0287]: "Cured sealant film was present on the outer surface of the seat ring 30 in the region of the purge gap 60. The sealing edge 32 and the sealing face 42 of the poppet 40 were clean." [0289]: no valve core having either cleaning action alone was tested.

Attack. The only inspection result in the package reports clean sealing surfaces and a fouled purge gap surface. A competitor reads that as evidence that the movement of the seat, and not the air purge, keeps the valve core working. The competitor keeps the over-travel and a seat ring that deflects and recovers, and removes the purge gap: the outer surface of the ring is held or sealed along its whole length, or the deflection merely changes the opening between the sealing face and the sealing edge. By [0064] and [0126] that product has no purge gap. It is outside claims 1, 2 and 3, and therefore outside all 86 claims. It works commercially because it deletes the tolerance that the description itself treats as delicate, the closure of the purge gap at rest ([0264], [0289] last sentence), and it keeps the audible snap that a user notices.

Least narrowing action. Add an independent claim to a valve core in which, in the over-travel, the load-applying surface deflects the element, and in which, on removal of the load, the element undergoes shape recovery and moves across the sealing face, with no purge gap recited. Basis exists for the two-piece seat: [0182], [0294] and Statement 7.1 at [0347], which recites no purge gap. For the one-piece seat ring the package describes the snap-back wipe only together with the purge gap ([0139], Statements 1.20 and 3.3), so a generic claim needs a statement from the inventors that the wipe may be provided without an open secondary flow path. No existing claim is narrowed.

New disclosure required: yes, for the one-piece seat ring. Target P3.

### F-33-2 [high] [design-around] Axially deflecting and tilting elements are described but lie outside every claim

Claim language attacked. Claim 1: "a radially deflectable element" and "an axial load that produces a radial expansion of the radially deflectable element". Claim 2 and claim 3 recite "a radial expansion" in the same way.

Anchor. [0179]: "In these embodiments the deflection is axial and the purge gap is a gap at a face, so that the element is referred to as a deflectable element and its deflection is not a radial expansion." [0180]: the deformation "is radial, axial, tilting or a combination of these".

Attack. The description teaches a working alternative, a disc spring of conical form whose rim lifts from a face, and then states in terms that it is not what the claims recite. No claim covers it. In the United States subject matter that is disclosed and left unclaimed is treated as dedicated to the public, and equivalents cannot recover it. In the United Kingdom and before the EPC national courts the express statement in [0179] tells the reader that "radial" was meant strictly. The competitor's part is a stamped disc spring, which costs less than a seat ring of NiTi alloy and, per [0179], also provides the second spring stage.

Least narrowing action. Add an independent claim, or broaden claim 1, to recite "a deflectable element" and "a deflection of the deflectable element, the deflection opening a purge gap", and move "radially" and "radial expansion" to a dependent claim. Basis: [0179], [0180]. The genus term is used in those paragraphs; if it is adopted in the claims it should be added to the definitions. A figure of the disc spring form would strengthen the basis and is reported in 36b.

New disclosure required: no, for the claim. Target P5.

### F-33-3 [high] [design-around] Contact from lift-off avoids "spaced from" and the normal stroke

Claim language attacked. Claim 1: "wherein the load-applying surface is spaced from the radially deflectable element throughout the normal stroke" and "closed at rest and throughout the normal stroke". Claim 2: "through a normal stroke (114) ... while a purge gap (60) remains closed". Claim 3: "an over-travel (116) of the poppet beyond a normal stroke (114)".

Anchor. [0071]: the normal stroke "ends at the position of the poppet at which the load-applying surface first meets the radially deflectable element". [0072]: the over-travel is the range "in which, and only in which, the load-applying surface bears on the radially deflectable element". [0102] defines the load-applying surface by the same absence of contact.

Attack. The boundary between the two ranges is defined by first contact and by nothing else. A competitor places a collar or a washer in light contact with the element from the closed position and lets the load rise with travel. The element opens its gap once the load passes a threshold set by the stiffness of the element. There is then no range of travel in which the surface is "spaced", the normal stroke as defined has no length, and the over-travel as defined, the only range in which the surface bears, does not exist. The limitation is a negative one: the claimant must prove an absence of contact over a range. The product works commercially and is cheaper: the threshold is set by the element, so a return spring of a single rate suffices.

Least narrowing action. Add an independent formulation in which the poppet has a first range of travel, in which the purge gap is closed, and a second range, in which the axial load applied through the load-applying surface opens the purge gap, without requiring that the surface be out of contact in the first range. Partial basis: [0132] (the ring keeps its at-rest shape in the normal stroke) and [0251] (parameters defined by function). The package does not describe contact below the opening load, and [0071], [0072] and [0102] point the other way, so the inventors must state whether such contact is within their conception. The existing claims stay as they are.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P3.

### F-33-4 [medium] [design-around] An element loaded otherwise than by a surface moved by the poppet, or a rigid sliding element, is outside the claims

Claim language attacked. Claim 1: "a load-applying surface that is moved by the poppet"; "a radially deflectable element"; "undergoes shape recovery".

Anchor. [0016]: the radial deflection is produced "not by a flow of fluid acting on the radially deflectable element". [0135]: "The purge gap 60 is not opened by the flow of air acting on the seat ring 30."

Attack. Two routes. (a) The element is loaded by a separate annular piston that the pressure differential drives independently of the poppet, or by the pressure itself above a threshold. Nothing "moved by the poppet" bears on it, and [0016] and [0135] close the door on any broader reading. (b) The poppet, in its further travel, pushes a rigid sleeve axially against a spring and uncovers a second path. Nothing deflects and nothing recovers a shape. Saved references 2 (US4044791A), 8 (US5918628A) and 15 (US5924444A) show that staged opening and gaps opened by fluid are known engineering in other fields, so either route is cheap to design. The commercial value of either is less certain than for F-33-1, because the package gives no evidence that a purge flow alone cleans.

Least narrowing action. None is available inside the package. Any claim broad enough to catch route (a) or (b) moves towards saved references 2, 8 and 15. The inventors should be asked whether a sliding element or a pressure-driven piston is within their conception; if so, an embodiment and a dependent claim family in the field of a tyre chamber containing sealant can be added. Reported for the human's decision.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P3.

### F-33-5 [medium] [design-around] A valve core sold alone escapes where the bore of the valve stem bounds the purge gap

Claim language attacked. Claim 1 recites "the radial expansion opening a purge gap" with no reference to fitting. Claim 3, by contrast, recites "when the seat ring is installed in the valve core". Claim 45: "wherein the bore is a bore of a valve stem (10) in which the valve core is fitted".

Anchor. [0189]: "that boundary remains in the valve stem 10 when the valve core 5 is removed". [0161]: only where the bore is the core body bore does a valve core supplied alone contain "both boundaries of the purge gap 60".

Attack. A competitor puts the element at the end of the core body so that the gap forms against the bore of the valve stem, and sells the valve core alone. As sold, the core opens no gap against anything. The difference in wording between claim 1 and claim 3 supports the argument that claim 1 requires the gap to exist in the article claimed. The manufacturer is then at most an indirect infringer, and the person who completes the combination is a rider.

Least narrowing action. Add to claim 45, and in a new dependent claim on claim 1, the conditional wording already used in claim 3: the valve core being configured so that, when the valve core is fitted in a valve stem, the radial expansion opens the purge gap against a bore of the valve stem. Basis: [0125], [0189], [0161].

New disclosure required: no. Target P5.

### F-33-6 [medium] [design-around] Claim 2 is performed by the rider and reaches a supplier only indirectly

Claim language attacked. Claim 2: "moving the poppet through an over-travel (116) beyond the normal stroke"; "passing air through the open purge gap into the tyre chamber".

Anchor. [0273]: "The user couples the pump head 90, inflates the tyre chamber 80 and removes the pump head 90." [0274]: the method needs a pump head that applies a pressure differential exceeding the over-travel differential. [0276]: instructions for use that recommend a floor pump.

Attack. Every step is carried out by a private user. Private and non-commercial acts are exempt in the United Kingdom and in EPC states, and an action against riders in the United States is not practical. A supplier is reached only through indirect infringement, which turns on the supplier's knowledge or intent, and on proof that users do reach the over-travel, which depends on the pump. The word "air" gives a further literal argument where a CO₂ cartridge is used ([0275]).

Least narrowing action. Keep claim 2 and do not rely on it. The product claims carry enforcement. If a method claim is wanted against suppliers, [0265] supports a claim to testing each valve core "to confirm that the purge gap 60 opens at the intended over-travel differential 112", a step performed by the manufacturer.

New disclosure required: no. Target P5.

### F-33-7 [medium] [enablement] Pressures and dimensions reached only under load cannot be proved without a stated test method

Claim language attacked. Claim 11: "the crack pressure is from 0.8 bar to 1.5 bar and the over-travel differential is from 2.5 bar to 5.0 bar". Claim 19: "a purge gap width of from 0.10 mm to 0.25 mm". Claim 29: "an austenite finish temperature below 5 °C". Claims 12, 33, 34, 53 and 72 are affected in the same way.

Anchor. [0069], [0070], [0097] and [0079] define the quantities and give no method of measurement. [0237] states the purge gap width "when fully open". [0274]: "The rate at which the floor pump delivered air was not recorded."

Attack. A pressure differential across an open poppet depends on the flow. The package does not say whether the two thresholds are static or dynamic values, at what temperature they are taken, or whether the valve core is clean. A defendant whose product sits near an end point chooses the method that puts it outside, and argues in the United States that the range is indefinite. The purge gap width exists only under load inside the core body. The austenite finish temperature depends on the method of thermal analysis or mechanical test used, which is a common-general-knowledge premise (A3).

Least narrowing action. Add to the description the method by which the prototype values of 1.0 bar, 3.5 bar, 0.15 mm and the austenite finish temperature were determined. This narrows nothing. The weakness shows as a problem of proof, but its root cause is a missing method in the description, so the category is enablement.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-33-8 [medium] [design-around] Claim 3 is avoided by a part that has no sealing edge or no load-converting geometry of its own

Claim language attacked. Claim 3: "a sealing edge (32) at one end of the seat ring" and "a wall having a load-converting geometry".

Anchor. [0164]: in the two-piece seat "the rigid seat 140 carries the sealing edge 142 and the wiper ring 144 is the radially deflectable element". [0165]: the conversion is made "by the shape of the radially deflectable element, by the shape of the load-applying surface that bears on it, or by both".

Attack. Claim 3 is the only claim that reaches a supplier of the deflecting part alone. A wiper ring has no sealing edge. A plain ring or sleeve spread by a conical surface has no load-converting geometry of its own. A supplier of either part, as a component or as a replacement part ([0162]), is outside claim 3, and claim 1 reaches only the assembled valve core.

Least narrowing action. Add an independent claim to a radially deflectable element for a valve core, not limited to an element that carries the sealing edge, the axial load being converted into the radial expansion by the shape of the element, by the shape of the load-applying surface, or by both. Basis: [0099], [0164], [0165].

New disclosure required: no. Target P5.

### F-33-9 [low] [design-around] The shape-setting step of claim 4 cannot be seen in the product and is avoided by another forming route

Claim language attacked. Claim 4: "shape-setting (512) the seat ring in a fixture that holds the load-converting geometry".

Anchor. [0171]: a strut pattern "cut in the wall by laser cutting". [0254]: "In other embodiments the laser cutting step 510 cuts a pattern in the wall".

Attack. The step is performed in a factory, probably abroad, and leaves nothing in the ring that distinguishes it from another route. A maker who cuts the load-converting geometry into tube that is already superelastic uses no fixture that holds that geometry. Claim 4 also depends on claim 3, so it adds no reach that claim 3 does not already have against the maker of the ring.

Least narrowing action. Add a dependent or parallel method claim in which the load-converting geometry is formed by laser cutting a pattern in the wall. Basis: [0171], [0254]. Treat the manufacturing claims as fallback positions and not as enforcement claims.

New disclosure required: no. Target P5.

## Assumptions

A1. The package is the whole of the text to be filed. No abstract and no priority document were supplied, and none was assessed.

A2. The legal premises used are stated from general knowledge for counsel to verify: that disclosed and unclaimed subject matter is treated in the United States as dedicated to the public; that private and non-commercial acts are exempt from infringement in the United Kingdom and in EPC states; and that a supplier's liability for a method performed by users is indirect and depends on knowledge or intent.

A3. Common general knowledge: the measured austenite finish temperature of a NiTi alloy depends on the test method used, and the pressure drop across an open poppet depends on the flow through it.

A4. The competitor assumed is a maker of removable threaded valve cores for tyre chambers containing sealant, with access to stamping, turning and ordinary spring manufacture, and without access to the inventors' know-how.

A5. `figures.md` and `registry.md` are instructions for drawings and a registry. No drawing was available, so every statement about a figure rests on those instructions.

## Open Questions

Q1. Is the snap-back wipe, without any open secondary flow path, within the inventors' conception for the one-piece seat ring? F-33-1 depends on the answer.

Q2. Did any prototype have the load-applying surface in contact with the seat ring before the end of the normal stroke, and is such contact within the inventors' conception? F-33-3 depends on the answer.

Q3. Have the inventors considered a rigid sliding element or a pressure-driven piston in place of the radially deflectable element? F-33-4 depends on the answer.

Q4. By what method were the crack pressure of 1.0 bar, the over-travel differential of 3.5 bar, the purge gap width of 0.15 mm and the austenite finish temperature determined?

Q5. For counsel: is a claim to the manufacturer's test of [0265] worth its cost, given that the product claims already reach the manufacturer?
