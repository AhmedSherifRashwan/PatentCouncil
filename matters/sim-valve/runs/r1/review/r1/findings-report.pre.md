#### F-32-3 — Every independent claim requires a contact-free first range of travel

**Severity** high · **Area** design-around and detectability · **Claims** 1, 2, 3 · **Specification** [0015], [0071], [0072]

**Raised by** ip-reviewer-b (review step 32)

**The objection.** Claims 1 and 2 require a normal stroke in which the surface is spaced from the element and the purge gap is closed; claim 3 requires an over-travel beyond a normal stroke. #### F-32-5 — Inventive step: the application's own inspection contradicts the effect attributed to the purge gap
