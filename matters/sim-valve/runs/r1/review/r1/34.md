## Findings

### FTO Issue List

Patentability does not establish freedom to operate. A granted patent on this valve core gives its owner the right to exclude others; it does not give the owner the right to make or sell the product if any feature falls inside someone else's live claims. This list is an analysis aid for a professional FTO search and claim-chart review, not an FTO opinion. Qualified patent counsel signs off.

Basis. The product features are taken from the description and the claims. The only third-party documents named are those saved in the matter's prior-art set (`prior-art/INDEX.md`, `prior-art/refs/`). None is named from memory. For each saved document, legal status (grant, lapse, term adjustment, maintenance fees, family members and validations) has **not** been checked. Every "status" entry below is a note for the searcher, not a finding of status. As INDEX.md itself records, CN, TW, DE and JP utility models and commercial "sealant-resistant" valve-core products were not searched. Those are where live claims on bicycle valve cores are most likely to be found.

Jurisdictions: US, EP (with the validation states where product is sold, typically DE, FR, IT, NL, ES), UK, plus the country of manufacture (manufacture is itself an infringing act; CN or TW is likely for a turned brass valve core, but the package does not say where it will be made).
