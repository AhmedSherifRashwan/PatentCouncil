### F-38-1 [high] [support] "Seat ring" and "purge shoulder" definitions are narrower than the broader claims they must support

[0058] defines "seat ring" as, among other things, having "a wall that converts an axial load applied by the purge shoulder in the over-travel into a radial expansion that opens the purge gap." [0063] defines "purge shoulder" as a surface "that is spaced from the seat ring throughout the normal stroke, that contacts the seat ring only in the over-travel." Both definitions hard-code the axial load as being applied specifically by "the purge shoulder" to specifically "the seat ring."

Claim 15, however, recites "the load-applying surface is provided on a collar that is a separate part carried by the poppet" — a load-applying surface that is not the purge shoulder. Claim 20 introduces the seat ring as one species of "the radially deflectable element." Nothing in the claims or elsewhere in the description limits the collar embodiment of claim 15 to radially deflectable elements other than the seat ring; [0153], which discloses the collar, makes no such restriction. Read together with claim 20, a valve core having a seat ring loaded by a collar (rather than a purge shoulder) falls within the claims but is expressly excluded by the specification's own lexicographic definition of "seat ring" at [0058], which requires the load to come "from the purge shoulder." The same problem recurs for "purge shoulder" at [0063], which is defined as contacting "the seat ring," so a purge shoulder acting on the wiper ring of the two-piece seat ([0182]: "the purge shoulder 44 bears on the wiper ring 144") falls outside [0063]'s own definition of what a purge shoulder does.

Because these are express "as used herein" definitions, a court construing the claims must apply them as controlling lexicography over any broader ordinary meaning. As drafted, the specification's own definitions create a genuine internal contradiction with claim scope the drafters otherwise intended to be broad (functional "load-applying surface" and "radially deflectable element" terms), and would hand an opponent a construction argument that narrows claim 1, 15 and 20 below their apparent scope, or alternatively an indefiniteness argument that the claims and the specification cannot be reconciled.

Least narrowing action: update [0058] and [0063] to use the genus terms ("the load-applying surface," "the radially deflectable element") consistently with the rest of the definitions section (compare [0067], [0071], [0072], [0081], which already use the genus terms correctly). No disclosure is added or removed; this aligns two isolated definitions with the terminology used everywhere else.

### F-38-2 [high] [support] Figures.md's "candidate, not confirmed" status for FIGS. 11 and 12 contradicts the description's and claims' unconditional treatment of the same subject matter

figures.md states, for FIGS. 11 (slotted sleeve, split seat ring, tapered seat ring) and 12 (core body bore relief, wall slots): "candidate alternative constructions identified during design-around analysis; not built, not tested, and not stated in the inventor's disclosure. Shown in prophetic/conditional form only, pending inventor confirmation." registry.md's own inconsistency flag 4 repeats this: "FIGS. 11, 12 and 13A number candidate constructions that are not in the inventor's disclosure... whether any of them is carried into the claimed embodiments... is Open Question Q5 of Prompt 20 and remains open here."

description.md, however, incorporates the identical subject matter without qualification: [0176] "In some embodiments the radially deflectable element is a slotted sleeve 170..."; [0177] "...a split seat ring 180..."; [0178] "...a tapered seat ring 190..."; [0186] "...a core body bore relief 202..."; [0190] "...wall slots 204..." — none of these passages carries a "candidate" or "pending confirmation" qualifier, and each is presented in the same register as the built-and-tested principal embodiment. claims.md then claims these constructions outright at claims 40–44 and 67, with no hedge.

This is an unresolved internal contradiction within the package as delivered for blind review. If figures.md's characterisation is the accurate one, then claims 40–44 and 67 rest on subject matter the package's own figure plan states the inventor has not actually disclosed or confirmed — a written-description and possibly inventorship problem, not a formatting one, and contrary to the company's rule that generated subject matter is a candidate until a human gate confirms it. If the description's confident, unhedged treatment is the accurate one (i.e. Open Question Q5 of Prompt 20 was since resolved), then figures.md and registry.md are simply stale and should be updated to remove the "not stated in the inventor's disclosure" language before the package is relied on further. I cannot resolve which is correct from the review package alone.

Least narrowing action: whichever is accurate, remove the contradiction — either strike the "candidate / not stated in the inventor's disclosure / pending confirmation" language from figures.md and registry.md flag 4, or add the equivalent hedge to [0176]–[0180], [0186]–[0192] and mark claims 40–44 and 67 as pending confirmation before further prosecution work proceeds on them.

### F-38-3 [medium] [formal] Reference numeral 146 (purge gap, two-piece seat) never appears in the description text

The two-piece-seat description ([0181]–[0184]) introduces the rigid seat (140), its sealing edge (142) and the wiper ring (144) by numeral, and describes the wiper ring being deflected outward and wiping on return, but never names or numbers the gap that opens between the wiper ring and the core body bore. Numeral 146 exists only in registry.md's Reference-Numeral Registry (first figure FIG. 9) and in the FIG. 9A/9B drawing instructions in registry.md ("Draw the purge gap (146) as a closed clearance between the wiper ring (144) and the core body bore (22)"). Since claim 1 itself uses "(60, 146)," this also leaves a claim term with no textual antecedent in the specification (see the companion Prompt 37 forensic audit, F-37-3).

Least narrowing action: add "146" to [0182] where the wiper ring's deflection is described, e.g. "...deflects it outward, so that a purge gap 146, closed at rest, opens between the wiper ring 144 and the core body bore 22."

### F-38-4 [medium] [support] "Radially deflectable element" and "load-converting geometry" have no preferred-term row in the locked terminology table

The genus term "radially deflectable element" is central to the description ([0099]–[0106], [0163]–[0180]) and to claim 1 itself, and "load-converting geometry" is central to claim 3 and its descendants ([0104], [0165]). Neither appears as its own row in terminology/LOCKED.md. The closest entries are indirectly worded synonyms buried in other rows' "Broader" columns: "seat ring" row lists "seat element; radially compliant seat element; valve seat member" as broader terms (not "radially deflectable element"), and "convoluted wall" row lists "non-straight wall section; load-converting wall" (not "load-converting geometry"). [0099] itself acknowledges the mismatch: "The expression 'radially compliant seat element' has the same meaning" as "radially deflectable element" — i.e. the description concedes it is using a different form of words from the one gestured at in the lock.

LOCKED.md's own Note 1 states: "Prompt 03 had not been written when this table was prepared, so its ladder features have not been cross-checked; any 03 feature without a row must be added before the table is locked at Gate 1." Two of the claim set's most heavily used genus terms having no row of their own reads as an incomplete lock rather than an isolated drafting drift, and is reported here as a defect in the lock itself rather than as a `new_terms` declaration on the reviewer's part, per the terminology-lock discipline.

Least narrowing action: add preferred-term rows for "radially deflectable element" and "load-converting geometry" to terminology/LOCKED.md (or confirm that the existing Broader-column entries were always intended as the locked terms and correct the description/claims to match them verbatim). This is a terminology-governance action, not a text rewrite.

### F-38-5 [low] [formal] FIG. 13A / numeral 220 is registered but has no description text

registry.md lists numeral 220 ("Schrader-pattern core body," first figure FIG. 13A) and figures.md includes a full figure plan entry for FIG. 13A. description.md's "Brief description of the drawings" section, however, jumps directly from "[0048] FIGS. 12A and 12B are axial sections of embodiments having, respectively, a relieved core body bore and a purge flow through the wall of the seat ring" to "[0049] FIGS. 13B is an axial section of an embodiment in which the valve core is integral with the valve stem" — FIG. 13A is not listed, and no paragraph anywhere in the body text describes a Schrader-pattern core body or uses numeral 220. This is consistent with figures.md's own caption for FIG. 13A ("Candidate alternative embodiment... not built or tested; shown for disclosure of the genus only"), but unlike FIGS. 11 and 12 (F-38-2), the FIG. 13A subject matter was never carried into the description at all — there is no contradiction here, simply an incomplete description relative to the registered figure and numeral.

Least narrowing action: either add a "Brief description of the drawings" entry and a body paragraph for FIG. 13A (if the Schrader-pattern embodiment is to be retained as disclosure), or remove FIG. 13A and numeral 220 from figures.md and registry.md if it is not to be pursued.

### F-38-6 [low] [formal] "FIGS. 13B is" — grammatical number mismatch

[0049]: "FIGS. 13B is an axial section of an embodiment in which the valve core is integral with the valve stem." "FIGS." (plural) governs a single figure reference ("13B") and a singular verb ("is"). This is a plain grammatical slip, made more visible once FIG. 13A is added or removed per F-38-5.

Least narrowing action: correct to "FIG. 13B is an axial section..."
