### F-36-1 [high] [enablement] The route of the purge flow and the reaction of the axial load are not described

Text attacked. Claim 1: "the radial expansion opening a purge gap (60, 146)". Claim 16: "the purge gap is bounded by an outer surface of the radially deflectable element and by a bore surrounding the radially deflectable element". [0124], [0127], [0186], [0187], [0194], [0113].

Attack, in four parts.

(a) Outlet and inlet. [0127] says that "the exit of the air purge into the tyre chamber 80" is described under "Purge gap arrangements and retention of the seat ring". That section says only that "The purge flow leaves the purge gap 60 into the tyre chamber 80" ([0194]). No paragraph says where air enters the purge gap or where it leaves.

(b) A closed end. [0186] places the interference fit on a fit band 35 next to the locating shoulder 24. An interference fit around the whole circumference closes the annular clearance at that end. The clearance over the free portion 37 is then open at one end only, and the text does not explain how air flows through it into the tyre chamber.

(c) Direction of movement. [0124] puts the purge gap between the outer surface of the seat ring and the bore that surrounds it, and claim 1 opens it by radial expansion. A ring that expands moves towards the bore that surrounds it. [0187] has the free portion 37 expand "into the core body bore relief 202" and the purge gap open, after being closed "by line contact at one end of the core body bore relief 202". The text does not say which surfaces part. The instruction for FIG. 4A draws the free portion "with a small radial clearance already shown" at rest, while the instruction for FIG. 1 draws the purge gap at rest as a hairline.

(d) Reaction of the load. [0113] applies the axial load "for example at its upper edge", and [0112] makes the upper edge the sealing edge 32. The instruction for FIG. 2 has the purge shoulder 44 touch the seat ring "at the fit-band end". [0117] places the purge shoulder below the sealing face and has it approach the seat ring as the poppet moves away from the sealing edge. No paragraph states which surface of the core body reacts the load, so the reader cannot tell which length of the wall is compressed.

Why it matters. The principal embodiment is the only form built and tested ([0184]). An opponent pleads that the description does not teach how to make a purge gap that is a flow path, and uses [0287], cured sealant film in the region of the purge gap, as evidence that no flow passed. Claims 16 to 19, 26, 45, 58 and 65 depend on this geometry. Any explanation supplied after filing is added matter.

Evidence of conception. Appears within conception because three prototypes were built and a purge gap width of 0.15 mm, "stated as a radial value", is given for them ([0237], [0280]).

Least narrowing action. Add to the description, from the inventors' drawings of the prototype: the inlet and the outlet of the purge gap; the surfaces that are in contact at rest and that part in the over-travel; the surface on which the load-applying surface bears and the surface that reacts the load; and one enlarged figure of the purge gap at rest and open, with flow arrows. No claim is narrowed.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-2 [high] [enablement] The dates of Example 1 cannot hold the protocol and the cycle counts stated

Text attacked. [0283]: one inflation cycle "consisted of inflating the tyre chamber 80 to 6 bar with a floor pump, holding for a period of 24 hours at a temperature of 22 °C, and deflating fully", and "The test is recorded as carried out from 12 August to 20 August of 2026." [0286]: each prototype "completed a total of 40 inflation cycles". Table after [0285]: failures at cycles 14, 12 and 18.

Attack. A cycle with a hold of 24 hours takes at least one day. Nine calendar days hold at most nine such cycles. Forty cycles need forty days, and the comparative failures at cycles 12, 14 and 18 need twelve to eighteen days. The protocol, the dates and the counts cannot all be right. Example 1 is the only measured evidence in the package, and the Background relies on it at [0003] and [0012]. An opponent uses the conflict to have the whole example disregarded. In the United States a material misstatement of test data also raises a question of candour.

Evidence of conception. The inventors hold the test record.

Least narrowing action. The inventors state from the record what was done: the true dates, or the true hold period, or that the hold of 24 hours applied only to the leak-down measurement at certain cycles. The description is then corrected to the record. Nothing is to be inferred or supplied by the drafter.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-3 [high] [formal] The Background cites a publication that the prior-art index marks as fictional

Text attacked. [0007]: "US2019/0123456A1 describes a valve core having a bore that is enlarged to reduce capillary rise of sealant along the stem of the poppet, a sealing face coated with a fluoropolymer layer ..., a rigid seat, and a removable insert".

Attack. `prior-art/INDEX.md`, under the requester-supplied references, lists S1 as "FICTIONAL US 2019/0123456 A1" and states that it "is not a real publication and was not searched for or fetched". The description presents the same number as an existing document and attributes a disclosure to it. A statement of prior art in the Background is read as the applicant's own account of the art. Filing a citation of a document that does not exist misstates the art and, in the United States, raises a question of candour. The root cause is a citation, so the category is formal; it is raised here because this prompt is the last check of the exact text.

Least narrowing action. Remove [0007] from the description, or replace the citation with the true identity of the document once the human has confirmed it. [0013] refers to "the documents referred to above" and stands without [0007]. No claim is affected.

New disclosure required: no. Target in-place.

### F-36-4 [high] [enablement] The description does not explain how the seat ring moves across the sealing face

Text attacked. Claim 32: "on the shape recovery, the seat ring moves across the sealing face of the poppet in a snap-back wipe". Claims 56 and 76 recite the same. [0138]: "It is believed that on shape recovery the seat ring 30 moves across the sealing face 42 of the poppet 40 and shears partially cured sealant film from it."

Attack. [0137] gives the order of events: "As the poppet 40 leaves the over-travel 116 the purge shoulder 44 lifts from the seat ring 30 and the axial load is removed. The seat ring 30 undergoes shape recovery ... The poppet 40 then returns through the normal stroke 114 and the sealing face 42 seats again". At the moment of recovery the sealing face is therefore a full normal stroke, 0.6 mm in the prototype ([0235]), away from the sealing edge. The text does not say how a ring that recovers at that moment touches the sealing face, and offers a belief in place of a mechanism. A defendant argues that claims 32, 56 and 76 describe something that the description's own sequence excludes, and that infringement of them cannot be proved. The same gap undermines any broader claim to the wipe.

Evidence of conception. Appears within conception because the inventors name and define the action ([0085]), heard a snap on every inflation ([0141], [0288]) and found the sealing edge and the sealing face clean ([0287]).

Least narrowing action. Add the inventors' account of the contact: which part of the seat ring meets which part of the poppet during recovery, at what point of the return travel, and any observation that supports it. If the inventors cannot say, state the wipe as a relative movement between the sealing edge and the sealing face on re-seating, if that is what they observed. No claim is narrowed.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-5 [medium] [enablement] The conversion of axial load into radial expansion is described in two ways that do not agree

Text attacked. Claim 22: "the axial load flattens the at least one convolution". Claim 24: "the at least one convolution is a single outward bow". [0134], [0170], [0172], [0234], [0237].

Attack. [0134] and [0170] say that the convolution flattens under the axial load and that the wall moves outward. [0172] says that the pre-bowed wall "corresponds to the convoluted wall 34 with one convolution 36, written as a bow", and that "The bow increases under axial load in the manner of a pre-buckled column". A wave cannot both flatten and increase under the same load. The stated purge gap width of 0.15 mm, a radial value, is also larger than the convolution amplitude of 0.12 mm from which it is said to arise ([0234], [0237]). Claim 24 depends on claims 21 and 22 in the alternative, so claim 24 read with claim 22 claims a bow that flattens, against [0172].

Evidence of conception. Appears within conception because the prototype seat rings were shape-set in a fixture that holds the geometry ([0255]), so the inventors know the at-rest shape and the loaded shape.

Least narrowing action. Add one paragraph and, if possible, one figure stating the at-rest and the loaded profile of the prototype wall and the direction in which each part of the wall moves. Make claim 24 depend on claim 21 only, if the inventors confirm that the bow increases.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-6 [medium] [enablement] Pressures and dimensions reached only under load cannot be proved without a stated test method

Text attacked. Claim 11: "the crack pressure is from 0.8 bar to 1.5 bar and the over-travel differential is from 2.5 bar to 5.0 bar". Claim 19: "a purge gap width of from 0.10 mm to 0.25 mm". Claim 29: "an austenite finish temperature below 5 °C". [0069], [0070], [0079], [0097], [0142].

Attack. The quantities are defined and no method of measurement is given. The description gives no spring rate, no axial load on the seat ring and no effective area of the poppet, and says only that the rates "are selected" to give the stated pressures ([0142]). The end points of every claimed range are open to dispute. This is the same objection as the finding of the same title in 33b.

Evidence of conception. Appears within conception because each prototype value was measured or specified ([0280], [0279]).

Least narrowing action. Add the method by which each prototype value was determined, and the spring rates and the axial load of the prototype if the inventors have them.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-7 [medium] [support] One valve pattern, one size and one actuation support claims that are general in all three

Text attacked. Claim 1 recites no valve pattern and no actuation. [0072]: "which is the preferred actuation". [0157]: "The Presta pattern is an example of a pattern of the tyre valve 1." [0049] lists FIG. 13B only. [0231]: seat ring inner diameter "from 2.0 mm to 2.6 mm".

Attack. [0072] implies an actuation other than the pressure differential and describes none. A poppet moved by hand, or by a part of the pump head that pushes it, reaches the over-travel at a travel set by that part and not at a pressure. The figure instructions and the registry contain FIG. 13A, a Schrader-pattern core body 220, which the description never mentions; the drawings would then show a figure that the text does not describe. Every dimension in the description and in claims 68 and 69 belongs to one size of valve. A competitor's product in another pattern is within claim 1 on its words, and the defendant answers that the description supports one pattern only.

Evidence of conception. Appears within conception for other actuation because [0072] calls one actuation preferred. For the Schrader pattern the figure instructions mark FIG. 13A as a candidate "not stated in the inventor's disclosure", so conception is not shown and must be asked.

Least narrowing action. If the inventors confirm, add a paragraph on actuation of the poppet other than by the pressure differential, a description of FIG. 13A, and the principal dimensions as ratios, for example convolution amplitude to wall thickness. If they do not confirm, remove FIG. 13A from the drawings.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-8 [medium] [support] The axially deflecting and tilting forms rest on one paragraph and no figure

Text attacked. [0179]: "No figure shows this form and no test of it is reported." [0180].

Attack. A claim to a deflectable element, proposed in 33b, would rest on [0179] and [0180] alone. The text does not say where the face is, how the gap at the face is a secondary flow path distinct from the main passage, or what "tilting" means. An examiner at the EPO may treat a claim generalised from these two paragraphs as unsupported over its breadth.

Evidence of conception. Appears within conception because [0179] describes the disc spring form, its sealing at rest and its second function as a spring stage.

Least narrowing action. Add one figure of the disc spring form at rest and in the over-travel, and a paragraph that identifies the face, the route of the purge flow and one example of a tilting element.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-9 [medium] [support] The thermal variant is disclosed, unclaimed and excluded by the definitions

Text attacked. [0216]: "Warming of the thermal variant 150, for example by hand, opens the purge gap 60 without a pump". [0065]: the purge gap "is opened in the over-travel by the deflection that the load-applying surface produces". [0099]: the radially deflectable element "is deflected radially by the axial load which the load-applying surface applies in the over-travel".

Attack. The thermal variant has no over-travel and no load-applying surface, so by [0065] and [0099] it has neither a purge gap nor a radially deflectable element as those terms are defined. No claim covers it. If it is left as it is, the variant is published by the applicant's own text, is prior art against any later filing on it, and in the United States is treated as dedicated to the public. No training method, no dimension and no data are given ([0296]).

Evidence of conception. [0216] and [0296] describe it. The figure instructions record that the inventors are uncertain about pursuing it.

Least narrowing action. The human decides between three courses: remove the variant from this application and keep it for a later filing; keep it and add an independent claim with its own defined terms and an enabling description of the training; or keep it as description only and accept the consequences above.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P3.

### F-36-10 [medium] [enablement] No example isolates the air purge, and no cold or fatigue test exists

Text attacked. [0289]: "No valve core having the air purge alone or the snap-back wipe alone was tested". [0287]: cured sealant film "was present on the outer surface of the seat ring 30 in the region of the purge gap 60". [0212], [0275]: no test at low temperature.

Attack. Every independent claim turns on the purge gap. The only observation about the purge gap is that the surface it sweeps was fouled. At the EPO the technical effect relied on for inventive step must be credible from the application as filed, and an opponent says that the package makes the effect of the purge gap less credible, not more. The ranges are untested ([0230]) and service beyond 40 inflation cycles is not established ([0289]).

Evidence of conception. The inventors have the prototypes and the test rig of Example 1.

Least narrowing action. Before filing, test one valve core in which the purge gap cannot open and one in which the seat ring recovers slowly, under the protocol of Example 1, and add the results whatever they show. If no test is run, leave the text as it is; no result may be supplied by the drafter.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-11 [low] [support] The normal stroke has one value and no range

Text attacked. Claim 34: "the normal stroke is 0.6 mm and the over-travel distance is 0.5 mm". [0235]: "no range is stated for it".

Attack. Claim 34 is a single point, avoided by a normal stroke of 0.5 mm or 0.7 mm. No intermediate fallback exists between claim 33 and claim 34.

Evidence of conception. [0235] states the built value only.

Least narrowing action. Ask the inventors for the range of the normal stroke within which they consider the valve core to operate, and add it with the same caveat as [0230].

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-12 [low] [support] "Air" is not defined to cover another inflation gas

Text attacked. Claim 2: "so that air flows through a main passage (26)" and "passing air through the open purge gap". Claims 38 and 57 recite "air" and "air purge".

Attack. [0212] and [0275] contemplate discharge of a CO₂ cartridge through the tyre valve. Nothing defines "air" or "air purge" to cover carbon dioxide or nitrogen. A defendant argues that inflation with a cartridge is outside the words of claim 2.

Evidence of conception. Appears within conception because [0212] and [0275] discuss the CO₂ cartridge as a case of use.

Least narrowing action. Add a definition that "air" means the gas delivered by the pump head, including carbon dioxide and nitrogen.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

### F-36-13 [low] [support] Only prototype routes of manufacture are described

Text attacked. [0261]: the polymer seat ring 130 "is turned from rod". [0253], [0254]: the seat ring is laser cut from tube and shape-set. [0262]: the slotted sleeve is "stamped, deep-drawn or rolled".

Attack. A turned polymer ring with a wall of 0.20 mm is a prototype route. A maker in volume moulds it. No route other than laser cutting and shape-setting is given for a metal seat ring with a continuous convoluted wall. The manufacturing claims then cover only the routes a competitor is least likely to use.

Evidence of conception. Appears within conception because [0208] gives lower cost as the purpose of the polymer seat ring.

Least narrowing action. Ask the inventors which routes they contemplate for volume, and add them as alternatives.

New disclosure required: yes. Target P4.

## Assumptions

A1. The package is the exact text to be filed. No abstract, no drawings and no priority document were supplied, and none was assessed.

A2. `figures.md` and `registry.md` are instructions and a registry. Statements about what a figure shows rest on those instructions.

A3. Common general knowledge: a thin-walled tube with a wave in its wall shortens under axial compression by bending at the wave, and the pressure drop across an open poppet depends on the flow through it.

A4. The statement that S1 is a fictional test reference is taken from `prior-art/INDEX.md` as written. The requester-supplied file itself was not opened.

A5. Legal premises on candour, on dedication of unclaimed subject matter and on the credibility of a technical effect are stated from general knowledge for counsel to verify.
