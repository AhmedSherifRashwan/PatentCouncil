### F-28-1 [high] [enablement] The axial load path through the seat ring is not described consistently

Claims 1, 2, 3, 21, 22 and 62. Claim 1: "the load-applying surface bears on the radially deflectable element and applies an axial load that produces a radial expansion"; claim 22: "the axial load flattens the at least one convolution".

Attack. The whole invention turns on an axial load passing through the convoluted wall between a load point and a reaction. The package does not say where either is, and what it does say conflicts:
- [0113]: the axial load is applied "for example at its upper edge", and [0112] makes the upper edge the sealing edge 32.
- [0167], [0168]: the sealing edge and the end seated against the locating shoulder 24 are opposite ends, and the fit band 35 lies next to the locating shoulder ([0186]).
- FIG. 2 instructions: the purge shoulder touches the seat ring "at the fit-band end", which by [0186] is the locating-shoulder end, not the upper edge.
- FIG. 1 instructions: both the sealing edge and the locating shoulder are "at"/"near the tyre-chamber end".
- [0116]: the poppet travels "away from the sealing edge". The package never explains how a surface carried by a poppet that is moving away from the sealing edge comes to bear on the seat ring, which end it loads, and what surface of the core body reacts that load so that the wall between them is compressed. "Below" ([0117], claim 14) is never tied to a direction.
- [0134], [0237] and claim 22 say the convolution flattens under the axial load and that the flattening gives the radial expansion. [0172] says the opposite of a bowed wall under the same load: "The bow increases under axial load in the manner of a pre-buckled column", and [0172] also says the pre-bowed wall "corresponds to the convoluted wall 34 with one convolution 36". An examiner or opponent will say the description gives two contradictory mechanisms for the same part, and that flattening a wave shortens nothing and so cannot be what an axial compressive load does (A3).

An EPO examiner has here the verifiable internal contradiction needed to raise sufficiency (Art. 83) against the principal embodiment; in the US the same facts support a § 112(a) enablement and written-description rejection of claims 22 and 62, and a construction fight on claim 1. The prototype was built and worked ([0279]–[0288]), so the defect is in the text, not in the invention.

Least narrowing action. Add one paragraph to "Principal embodiment: structure", taken from the built prototype, stating: which end of the seat ring 30 faces the tyre chamber 80; on which end or surface the purge shoulder 44 bears; which surface of the core body 20 reacts the axial load; and how the convoluted wall 34 moves (convolution deepens or flattens) as the free portion 37 moves outward. Then conform [0113], [0134], [0237], FIG. 1 and FIG. 2 instructions and claim 22 to that paragraph. No claim narrowing is needed if the mechanism is stated. Requires new disclosure (inventor facts); target P4.

### F-28-2 [high] [support] Drawn captions call FIGS. 10, 11A–11C, 12A–12B and 13A "candidate" constructions "shown for disclosure of the genus only"

Claims 26, 40, 41, 42, 43, 44, 66 and 67 rest on FIGS. 11A–11C and 12A–12B.

"Not built or tested" is already stated, correctly, in [0183], [0184], [0202] and [0216]; the captions add only the "candidate" and "pending confirmation" language.

Least narrowing action. Remove "Candidate", "shown for disclosure of the genus only" and "pending confirmation" from the drawn captions; keep the description's plain statements that no test was made. Whether the inventor conceived these constructions is a question for the human at the gate (Q1); if any is not confirmed, the corresponding embodiment and claims (40–44 for FIG. 11; 26, 44, 66, 67 for FIG. 12) should be taken out rather than filed as candidates. No new disclosure; target in-place.

### F-28-3 [medium] [support] Slotted sleeve, split seat ring and tapered seat ring: no closed-at-rest purge gap and no seal at rest described

Claims 40, 41, 42 and 43, each through claim 1, which requires "a purge gap ... that is closed at rest and throughout the normal stroke" and a sealing face that "seats on the sealing edge".

Attack. [0176] describes the slotted sleeve 170 as a cylinder in which "axial slots running from one end form fingers 172" and says the slots "are a route for the purge flow when the fingers 172 are spread". It never says the slots are closed at rest, nor whether the sleeve carries the sealing edge. Open axial slots in a sleeve that carries the sealing edge would leak at rest; if the sleeve does not carry it, no other seat is described. [0177] deals with the seal at rest for the split seat ring 180 but does not say which path is the purge gap. [0178] says nothing about the purge gap or the seal for the tapered seat ring 190. Claim 1's closed-at-rest limitation is therefore disclosed in combination with none of these three forms; the combination rests only on the general statement at [0163].

Least narrowing action. Add, for each of the three forms, one sentence stating where the sealing edge is, which flow path is the purge gap, and what closes it at rest and through the normal stroke. Requires new disclosure (only if within the inventor's conception, see Q1); target P4.

### F-28-4 [medium] [support] Two-piece seat: purge gap 146 and the route of the purge shoulder to the wiper ring are not described

Attack. Numeral 146 does not occur in the description. [0181]–[0183] and [0293]–[0295] say that the purge shoulder "bears on the wiper ring 144 and deflects it outward" but never say that the deflection opens a purge gap, where that gap lies, or that it is distinct from the main passage. Only the figure instructions (FIG. 9B: "passing through or past the rigid seat") address it. Claim 1 read on the two-piece seat therefore has only the genus definitions [0092], [0099] as support.

Least narrowing action. Add to [0182] a sentence stating the location of purge gap 146 (for example between the wiper ring 144 and the core body bore 22), that it is distinct from the main passage 26, and the path by which the purge shoulder 44 reaches the wiper ring. Requires new disclosure; target P4.

### F-28-5 [medium] [enablement] The snap-back wipe cannot occur in the sequence the description gives

Claims 32, 56 and 76: "the seat ring moves across the sealing face of the poppet in a snap-back wipe".

Attack. [0137] gives the order on return: the purge shoulder lifts, "The seat ring 30 undergoes shape recovery ... and the purge gap 60 closes. The poppet 40 then returns through the normal stroke 114 and the sealing face 42 seats again". When the seat ring recovers, the sealing face is therefore spaced from it by at least the normal stroke (0.6 mm in the prototype, [0235]), and cannot be swept by it. [0138] states the mechanism only as "It is believed", and [0140] and [0289] say the test does not isolate it. An examiner will object that the claimed movement is not described in a way the skilled person can see happening; a defendant will say it is unprovable.

Least narrowing action. State, from the inventor's observation, what surface the recovering seat ring actually moves across and when the sealing face is in contact with it; or recast claims 32, 56 and 76 to what the description does support (shape recovery of a superelastic seat ring on removal of the axial load). Requires new disclosure; target P4.

### F-28-6 [medium] [support] Claim 24 dependent on claim 22 combines two contradictory statements

Claim 24: "The valve core of claim 21 or claim 22, wherein the at least one convolution is a single outward bow"; claim 22: "the axial load flattens the at least one convolution".

Attack. [0172] says of the single outward bow that "The bow increases under axial load". Claim 24 read on claim 22 requires the same bow to flatten. The combination is not disclosed and is contradicted.

Least narrowing action. Make claim 24 depend on claim 21 only, unless F-28-1 is resolved in a way that reconciles the two. No new disclosure; target P5.

### F-28-7 [medium] [support] Ranges and prototype values generalised from the seat ring to every radially deflectable element

Claim 19: "a purge gap width of from 0.10 mm to 0.25 mm"; claim 33: "an over-travel distance of from 0.4 mm to 0.9 mm"; claim 34: "the normal stroke is 0.6 mm and the over-travel distance is 0.5 mm". None depends on claim 20.

Attack. [0236] defines the over-travel distance range as "the travel during which the purge shoulder 44 bears on the seat ring 30"; [0237] ties the purge gap width to purge gap 60 and to "flattening of the convolution 36"; [0230] says the ranges are those of "the valve core 5 described under 'Principal embodiment: structure'"; statement 1.23 [0320] attaches the ranges only to statements 1.6 onwards, which require the seat ring. Nothing discloses the ranges with the wiper ring, the slotted sleeve, the split seat ring or the tapered seat ring. At EPO an amendment into claim 1 of any of these ranges without the seat ring would be an intermediate generalisation; in the US the written-description question is the same.

Least narrowing action. If the inventor agrees, add a sentence to "Parameters and ranges" that the over-travel distance, the purge gap width and the pressure ranges apply to each form of the radially deflectable element; otherwise make claims 19, 33 and 34 depend on claim 20, giving up the ranges for the other forms. Requires new disclosure (inventor statement); target P4.

### F-28-8 [high] [formal] Background cites a publication that the saved prior art records as fictional

[0007]: "US2019/0123456A1 describes a valve core having a bore that is enlarged ...".

Attack. The prior-art index lists this document only as requester-supplied reference S1, "Marked in the file as a fictional test reference ... it is not a real publication and was not searched for or fetched". A filed description that describes a non-existent publication as prior art is a misstatement of the background art (EPO Rule 42(1)(b)) and, in the US, a statement to the Office that is not true. The examiner will not find the document; the opponent will quote it.

Least narrowing action. Delete [0007], or replace it with a real, fetched reference if the teaching it describes is needed; the human confirms which (Q2). No new disclosure; target in-place.

### F-28-9 [medium] [formal] FIG. 13A and numerals 146 and 220 have no description

[0049]: "FIG. 13B is an axial section of an embodiment in which the valve core is integral with the valve stem."

Attack. The figure plan and figure instructions include FIG. 13A (Schrader-pattern core body 220), and the registry lists 146 and 220. The brief description of the drawings lists FIG. 13B alone, and neither FIG. 13A, 146 nor 220 appears in the description. A figure with no description and reference signs not mentioned in the description are formal objections (EPO Rule 42(1)(d), Rule 46(2)(i); 37 CFR 1.74, 1.84(p)(5)). Claim 1 uses 146.

Least narrowing action. Either describe FIG. 13A and purge gap 146 (see F-28-4), or remove FIG. 13A, renumber FIG. 13B as FIG. 13, and remove 146 and 220 from the claims and the registry. No new disclosure if removed; target in-place.

### F-28-10 [medium] [formal] FIG. 1 instructions place the main passage against the core body bore

FIG. 1 instructions: "the main passage (26) as the annular flow path between the poppet (40) and the core body bore (22)".

Attack. [0064], [0123] and [0126] define the main passage as running "between the sealing edge 32 and the sealing face 42" and "between the poppet 40 and the seat ring 30". The purge gap is bounded by the core body bore 22 ([0124]). A drawing that labels the main passage against the core body bore blurs the one distinction claim 1 depends on ("distinct from the main passage"), and a US examiner or court may read the drawing against the text.

Least narrowing action. Change the FIG. 1 instruction so that the lead line for 26 ends at the path between the sealing face 42 and the sealing edge 32. No new disclosure; target in-place.

### F-28-11 [low] [support] Claim 2 recites closing of the purge gap without the shape recovery that the description always pairs with it

Claim 2: "returning the poppet to the closed position, so that the axial load is removed and the purge gap closes".

Attack. [0014], [0021], [0137], [0272] and statement 3.1 [0329] always state that the purge gap closes by shape recovery of the radially deflectable element. Claim 2 is broader. The claim as filed is its own basis, so this is not an added-matter point; it is a US written-description and EPO Art. 84 support weakness if the claim is read to cover closing by some other means.

Least narrowing action. Add a sentence to "Use: cleaning during an inflation cycle" that the purge gap closes on removal of the axial load, whether by the shape recovery alone or assisted, for example by the garter spring of [0211]. Requires new disclosure (short); target P4.

### F-28-12 [low] [support] Claim 4 claims shape-setting alone; every method passage includes laser cutting and quenching

Claim 4: "shape-setting (512) the seat ring in a fixture that holds the load-converting geometry".

Attack. [0022], [0252] and statement 5.1 [0340] present the method as laser cutting, shape-setting and quenching (and in 5.1 electropolishing). Only the definition [0098] and [0170] describe shape-setting on its own. A priority or amendment challenge would say the standalone step is not disclosed as a method.

Least narrowing action. Add a sentence that the seat ring of NiTi alloy may be made by a method comprising shape-setting in the fixture, the other steps of FIG. 7 being optional. Requires new disclosure (short, within [0098]); target P4.

### F-28-13 [medium] [enablement] Claim 79: laser cutting "to a profile of the convoluted wall" is not explained, and conflicts with [0170] and [0254]

Claim 79: "the laser cutting cuts the seat ring to a profile of the convoluted wall".

Attack. The convolution is a wave in the section of the wall ([0062]). [0170] and [0254] say the convolution is formed by pressing a plain wall in the shape-setting fixture and that "laser cutting serves only to trim the ends". [0253] says the laser cutting is "to the profile of the convoluted wall 34". A laser cutting a tube cuts an outline through the wall; how it produces a sectional wave is not described. Claim 79 therefore rests on a sentence the rest of the description contradicts.

Least narrowing action. State in [0253] what "profile" means (for example the axial outline of the blank), or delete claim 79. Requires new disclosure (clarification); target P4.

### F-28-14 [low] [formal] Claim 68 recites a convolution amplitude when it may depend on claim 3 alone

Claim 68: "The seat ring of any one of claims 3 and 62 to 67, having at least one of: ... a convolution amplitude of from 0.08 mm to 0.20 mm".

Attack. Claim 3 does not require a convolution; the load-converting geometry may be a strut pattern, a helical slot or a taper ([0171]–[0173], [0178]). Read on claim 3, the convolution amplitude has no antecedent and no meaning. Claim 69 has the same problem.

Least narrowing action. Make the convolution-amplitude alternative of claim 68 and all of claim 69 depend on claim 62. No new disclosure; target in-place.
