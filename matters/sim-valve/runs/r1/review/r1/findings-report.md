#### F-29-1 — Disclosed alternatives lie outside every claim, one by the description's own statement

**Severity** high · **Area** design-around and detectability · **Claims** 1, 6 · **Specification** [0148], [0151], [0155], [0179], [0180], [0216]

**Raised by** ip-reviewer-b (review step 29)

**The objection.** [0179] describes a disc spring that opens a purge gap and says its deflection is not a radial expansion, which tells a competitor how to avoid claim 1. The detent and spring-loaded stop are outside claim 6. Unclaimed disclosed subject matter is dedicated to the public in the United States.

**Least narrowing fix.** Add a generic claim to a deflectable element whose deflection opens the purge gap, with claim-support statements and a figure, and a generic term for the threshold-separating return arrangement. Nothing is given up.  
*This fix needs disclosure the application does not yet contain.*


**Ruling** upheld — Correct on the text, and the most important design-around finding in this batch. [0179] describes a disc spring that opens a purge gap and states that 'the deflection is axial … and its deflection is not a radial expansion'. [0180] adds deformations that are 'radial, axial, tilting or a combination'. Claim 1 requires 'a radially deflectable element' and 'a radial expansion'. The description therefore tells a competitor how to avoid claim 1. In the US, subject matter disclosed but not claimed is dedicated to the public and cannot be recaptured under the doctrine of equivalents (Johnson & Johnston v. R.E. Service, Fed. Cir. 2002, en banc). Drafter's best defence: [0099] and [0106] give the generic terms a functional reading. But claim 1 says 'radial' expressly, and [0179] says the disc spring is not radial. On the return bias, the detent of [0148] and the spring-loaded stop of [0151] are covered by claim 5, which recites the crack pressure and over-travel differential without a mechanism, so that part of the finding is weaker. The claim-1 point alone justifies high. Counsel should note that a broader deflectable-element claim needs its own prior-art check, since axial-deflection seats appear in the saved art (US4044791A at [0008]). Whether the inventor conceived the disc-spring form should be confirmed, because [0179] says 'No figure shows this form and no test of it is reported'.

#### F-32-7 — The axial and face-gap forms are described, stated not to be a radial expansion, and claimed nowhere

**Severity** high · **Area** design-around and detectability · **Claims** 1, 2, 3 · **Specification** [0014], [0179], [0180]

**Raised by** ip-reviewer-b (review step 32)

**The objection.** [0179] teaches a working alternative: a disc spring whose rim seals on a face, flattened by the load-applying surface in over-travel so that a purge gap opens at the face. The description then says in terms that this is not a radial expansion. Every independent claim requires a radial expansion of a radially deflectable element. A competitor builds [0179]: a catalogue disc spring, no NiTi, no shape-setting, and by [0179] the same part supplies the second spring stage. The product sells on the same promise, a second flow path opened by over-travel. Subject matter described and left unclaimed is open to the argument that it was given to the public, and an equivalents case has to overcome the applicant's own sentence.

**Least narrowing fix.** Add an independent claim, or broaden claim 1 and move radial expansion to a dependent claim, to a deflectable element whose deflection by the load-applying surface in the over-travel opens the purge gap, on the basis of [0179] and [0180]. Add a figure for the disc-spring form, since none exists, and conform [0014]. Nothing is narrowed.  
*This fix needs disclosure the application does not yet contain.*


**Ruling** upheld — [0179] describes a working alternative: a conical disc spring whose rim 'seals on a face at rest', flattened by the load-applying surface in over-travel so that 'a purge gap opens at the face', and then states that 'its deflection is not a radial expansion'. [0180] adds that the deformation may be 'radial, axial, tilting or a combination'. Every independent claim requires 'a radial expansion of the radially deflectable element' (claims 1, 2) or 'of the wall' (claim 3). No passage brings [0179] back within the claims, and the applicant's own sentence works against an equivalents argument; in the US, subject matter disclosed and not claimed is exposed to dedication. High stands because the fix requires a new or broadened independent claim and cannot be made after filing. The provenance of [0179] is not recorded in the package ('No figure shows this form'), so it should be confirmed with the inventor together with F-30-7 before it is claimed.

#### F-33-2 — Axially deflecting element taught in [0179]-[0180] avoids 'radially deflectable element' and 'radial expansion'

#### F-33-3 — Contact from lift-off avoids 'spaced ... throughout the normal stroke'

**Severity** high · **Area** design-around and detectability · **Claims** 1, 2 · **Specification** [0071], [0072], [0015], [0148], [0132], [0239]

**Raised by** ip-reviewer, ip-reviewer-b (review step 33) · **both reviewers agreed**

**The objection.** The normal stroke ends where the load-applying surface first meets the element ([0071]) and the over-travel is the travel in which, and only in which, it bears on the element ([0072]). A competitor sets the collar at zero clearance or light contact from lift-off and makes the purge gap open only above a load threshold (stiffer convolution, detent). The commercial effect is kept but no travel exists in which the surface is spaced from the element, so claim 1 is not literally met, and [0015] presents the spacing as essential, weakening equivalents.

**Least narrowing fix.** Add a parallel claim bounding the travel ranges by pressure and gap state: purge gap closed while the pressure differential is between the crack pressure and the over-travel differential and opened when it exceeds the over-travel differential ([0071] last sentence, [0120], [0132], [0239]); this needs a disclosed embodiment in which the load-applying surface contacts or preloads the element through the normal stroke and the gap opens only above a load threshold, if within the inventor's conception.  
*This fix needs disclosure the application does not yet contain.*


**Ruling** upheld — Correct; both reviewers raised it. [0071] ends the normal stroke where the load-applying surface 'first meets' the element, and [0072] has it bear 'in which, and only in which', the over-travel. A valve whose surface touches or preloads the element from lift-off, with the purge gap opening only above a load threshold, has no normal stroke of the claimed kind and is outside claims 1 and 2 literally. [0015] presents the spacing as one of 'two features' that 'act together', which weakens equivalents. Best defence: the pressure-bounded definition in [0071] (last sentence) and [0120], [0239] could support a parallel claim, but only with a disclosed embodiment in which contact precedes opening, and none is described. This is the same design-around as F-32-3 in another batch; one remedy answers both, subject to the inventor's conception.

#### F-28-3 — Outward movement of the wall is said to open a gap to the surrounding bore in which the ring is interference fitted

**Least narrowing fix.** Add disclosure of the prototype construction at the outer surface of the seat ring: extent of the fit, clearance at rest, closing line, how expansion unseats it, entry and exit of the purge flow. No claim narrowing.

**Ruling** upheld — Correct on the text. [0081] defines radial expansion as an 'outward movement of the wall'. [0124] says the purge gap 60 is 'an annular clearance between the outer surface of the seat ring 30 and the core body bore 22', closed at rest and open after radial expansion. The text never explains how a wall moving outward against a surrounding bore opens, rather than closes, a clearance to that bore. Drafter's best defence: [0186]-[0187], where the free portion 37 expands into a relief 202 and the gap closes at rest 'by line contact at one end of the core body bore relief 202'. This goes to enablement and sufficiency of the annular-clearance form behind claims 16-17, 26, 58 and 65. The fix needs the inventor's prototype record (disclosure request). High is right.
