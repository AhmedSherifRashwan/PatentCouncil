## Findings

### FTO Issue List

Patentability does not establish freedom to operate. A patent gives its owner a right to stop others; it gives no right to make or sell the owner's own product. A valve core that is new and inventive can still fall inside an earlier, broader claim that belongs to someone else, for example a claim to a tyre valve assembly, to a metal seat, to a material or to a manufacturing step. Nothing in this list says that any third-party claim is or is not infringed, and nothing in it clears the product. It is a list of targets for a professional search and for claim charts prepared by qualified patent counsel, which is who decides these questions.

Basis of the list. The contemplated product is taken from the package: the principal embodiment of [0107] to [0128] as built for Example 1 ([0279] to [0281]), with the product forms of [0157] to [0162]. The only third-party documents named are those saved in the run's `prior-art/refs/` and listed in `prior-art/INDEX.md`, and two continuations that the index itself names. That status is not a legal conclusion and must be verified on the official registers. Family members in the EPO and the United Kingdom were not checked for any document. The two requester-supplied references S1 and S2 are marked in the index as fictional test references and are left out.

Jurisdictions of interest: the United States, the EPC states (European patents with unitary effect and national validations) and the United Kingdom, as the default for this matter. The place of manufacture is not stated in the package and must be added to the search (Q2).
