### F-40a-1 [medium] [formal] US multiple-dependency form defect

37 CFR 1.75(c) and MPEP 608.01(n) prohibit a multiple dependent claim from serving as a basis for any other multiple dependent claim, directly or indirectly. Claim 11 is a valid, first-level multiple dependent claim (its range, claims 5–10, contains no other MDC). From claim 13 onward, every "any one of claims 1 and 5 to N" or "claim X or claim Y" range that includes an earlier MDC repeats the violation, and it cascades through most of the claim set from claim 13 through claim 86. This is a formal objection to claim form, not a rejection on the merits: the claimed subject matter is unaffected, but the claim set as filed in the US will be objected to and require a renumbered submission (with USPTO fee recalculation) before substantive examination can proceed. EPO and UK practice carry no equivalent prohibition, so this row applies to the US filing only. Full claim list and restructuring approach: see the companion Prompt 37 reply, finding F-37-1.

### F-40a-2 [medium] [formal] Registered figure/numeral with no corresponding description text

Two items registered in figures.md/registry.md have no matching text in description.md: FIG. 13A and numeral 220 (Schrader-pattern core body) are entirely absent from the "Brief description of the drawings" and the body text, and numeral 146 (purge gap, two-piece seat), used in claim 1 itself, is never introduced in the description's prose. Both a US examiner and an EPO/UK examiner check that every numeral appearing in the claims and drawings is supported by the description; as it stands, the package would draw an objection on this ground in any of the three jurisdictions. See the Prompt 38 reply (F-38-3, F-38-5) for the substantive analysis and proposed corrections.

### F-40a-3 [low] [formal] Non-standard dependent-claim format

Claims 47, 61, 77 and 86 embed a cross-reference to a range of other claims inside the claim body or preamble rather than in the standard "The X of claim Y" introductory clause. This is a drafting-format irregularity rather than a substantive defect, but it obscures true claim category and dependency at a glance, and (for claim 61 especially) compounds the multiple-dependency defect above by incorporating a very large claim range inside a "wherein" clause. See the Prompt 37 reply, F-37-4.

### F-40a-4 [low] [formal] Description grammatical mismatch

[0049] reads "FIGS. 13B is an axial section...", pairing the plural "FIGS." with a single figure reference and a singular verb. A minor drafting slip, noted here because it sits at the same location as the missing FIG. 13A entry (F-40a-2) and should be corrected in the same pass.

### F-40a-5 [low] [formal] EPO two-part claim form not used

Claims 1 and 3 (the two principal independent claims) are drafted in single-part US-style form throughout, without a preamble/characterising-portion split under Rule 43(1) EPC. This is not a filing blocker — the EPO commonly accepts single-part claims at filing and raises the point, if at all, during examination — but is recorded here as an EPO-specific formality to have ready. No correction is proposed at filing; a two-part fallback of claim 1 against the closest prior art (US4044791A, US3473561A per the prior-art index) is recommended as prosecution preparation rather than as an immediate correction.
