## Deliverable

### Definitions

Every definition below is consistent with every embodiment in `CONFIRMED.md`: the NiTi prototype (N6), the polymer seat ring (Alt-A), the two-piece seat (Alt-B), the thermal variant (Alt-C), and the genus alternatives of the equivalent architecture library (e.g. slotted sleeve, split seat ring, tapered seat ring, laser-cut strut pattern, purge flow through the seat ring wall). Where a locked term's functional description in `terminology/LOCKED.md` is narrower than the confirmed genus alternatives, the definition is stated at the level of the genus and the locked functional description is kept as the preferred species, per `LOCKED.md` note 3. Where the record does not support broadening a definition (most prominently, whether shape recovery must be superelastic to produce a snap-back wipe — 01 (m) M1, M9; 03 Q1), the definition is left at the narrower, disclosed scope rather than asserted broad.

#### Component and structural terms

As used herein, **"seat ring"** means, generically, a radially compliant seat element retained in the core body, whose sealing edge forms the closure against the sealing face of the poppet at rest, and whose wall converts axial load applied by the purge shoulder during over-travel into radial expansion that opens the purge gap, the element then undergoing shape recovery toward its at-rest form when the load is removed. The preferred species is a one-piece tube having a convoluted wall (the superelastic NiTi seat ring of the prototype and the polymer seat ring of some embodiments); in other embodiments the radially compliant seat element takes the form of a slotted sleeve, a split seat ring, or a tapered seat ring, each converting axial load into radial expansion by a different geometry, or the sealing and purge/wipe functions are provided by separate elements, as in the two-piece seat.

As used herein, **"sealing edge"** means the surface of the seat ring on which the sealing face of the poppet seats to close the valve at rest. In the two-piece seat, the sealing edge is provided by the rigid seat rather than by the element that opens the purge gap.

As used herein, **"convoluted wall"** means a wall of the seat ring that is not straight in section, such that axial load applied to the sealing edge (or, in the two-piece seat, to the wiper ring) translates into radial expansion of the wall. A convoluted wall having one convolution and a convoluted wall having two convolutions are both within this definition; the convolution or convolutions may be formed by shape-setting a plain tube wall into a wave, or cut as a pattern, as the specification's manufacturing description states for a given embodiment.

As used herein, **"convolution"** means a single wave in the section of a convoluted wall.

As used herein, **"purge shoulder"** means a load-applying surface of the poppet that contacts the seat ring, or another radially compliant seat element, only during over-travel, and that applies the axial load producing radial expansion. In some embodiments the purge shoulder is a flat annulus on the poppet; in other embodiments it is conical, to engage a slotted sleeve, split seat ring, or tapered seat ring, or the load-applying surface is provided by a separate collar on the poppet or by a member of the return spring bearing on the seat ring.

As used herein, **"two-rate characteristic"** means force–travel behaviour of the return spring having a lower rate (the first spring stage) through the normal stroke and a higher rate (the second spring stage) that must be overcome for the poppet to reach over-travel. A two-rate characteristic may be produced by a single variable-pitch spring, by two springs in series or nested in parallel, by a spring together with an elastomeric bumper or a disc spring engaged at the end of the normal stroke, or by a detent, as further described; "return spring" refers to whichever construction produces the two-rate characteristic in a given embodiment.

As used herein, **"purge gap"** means a secondary flow path, distinct from the main passage, that is closed at rest and is opened by radial expansion of the seat ring so that air at pump pressure flows across a surface on which sealant film accumulates and into the tyre chamber. The preferred species is an annular clearance between the outer surface of the seat ring and the core body bore; in other embodiments the purge gap is a flow path through the wall of the seat ring (for example through slots or perforations that are closed at rest and open when the wall deforms), or a non-annular path at one or more locations around the seat ring, or is bounded in part by the valve stem bore rather than the core body bore.

As used herein, **"main passage"** means the flow path by which inflation air flows into the tyre chamber when the poppet is lifted off the sealing edge, as distinct from the purge gap.

#### Threshold, pressure and travel terms

As used herein, **"pressure differential"** means the difference between the pressure upstream of the poppet, applied by a pump head, and the pressure in the tyre chamber.

As used herein, **"crack pressure"** means the pressure differential across the poppet at which the poppet first lifts off the sealing edge against the first spring stage, beginning the normal stroke.

As used herein, **"normal stroke"** means the travel of the poppet away from the sealing edge that occurs once the pressure differential exceeds the crack pressure and before the poppet reaches over-travel.

As used herein, **"over-travel differential"** means the pressure differential across the poppet, greater than the crack pressure, that must be exceeded for the poppet to reach over-travel; in the preferred embodiment it is set by the second spring stage of a two-rate return spring. In some embodiments an equivalent activation differential is produced other than by a two-rate return spring, including by the seat ring's own axial stiffness once the purge shoulder bears on it, or by a spring-loaded stop, a variable-pitch spring, two springs, a spring and a bumper, or a detent, each described elsewhere in this specification.

As used herein, **"over-travel"** means the travel of the poppet beyond the normal stroke, reached only when the pressure differential exceeds the over-travel differential, during which the purge shoulder (or other load-applying surface) bears on the seat ring. In some embodiments over-travel is produced by displacement rather than solely by the pressure differential, for example by a push rod or adapter that drives the poppet mechanically; in such embodiments the over-travel differential is understood as the corresponding force or displacement threshold at which over-travel begins.

#### Material and property terms

As used herein, **"superelastic"** means the property of a material that recovers large strain on unloading at temperatures above its austenite finish temperature, distinct from a thermally triggered shape-memory response in which recovery is produced by a change in temperature rather than by unloading.

As used herein, **"austenite finish temperature"** means the temperature above which a shape-memory alloy such as NiTi alloy is fully austenitic and behaves superelastically. In the preferred embodiment the seat ring is specified with an austenite finish temperature below 5°C so that it remains superelastic across riding temperatures; in the thermal variant, the seat ring is specified with an austenite finish temperature of approximately 35°C to 40°C so that warming above riding temperature triggers recovery.

As used herein, **"martensite start temperature"** means the temperature below which a shape-memory alloy such as NiTi alloy begins to transform toward martensite and correspondingly loses superelastic recovery.

As used herein, **"radial compliance"** means the capacity of a radially compliant seat element to expand radially under axial load applied by the purge shoulder and to return substantially to its at-rest radial dimension on removal of that load.

As used herein, **"shape recovery"** means the return of a radially compliant seat element toward its at-rest shape when the axial load applied by the purge shoulder is removed, closing the purge gap. Shape recovery is superelastic recovery in the preferred (NiTi) embodiment, and elastic recovery of a different character, for example of a polymer or spring-tempered metal, in other embodiments; the rate and stiffness of recovery vary correspondingly among embodiments, as does whether a snap-back wipe results (see "snap-back wipe" below).

#### Cleaning-action terms

As used herein, **"air purge"** means the first cleaning action: the flow of air at pump pressure through the open purge gap that carries sealant film present on a swept surface into the tyre chamber.

As used herein, **"snap-back wipe"** means a second, mechanical cleaning action, present in embodiments in which shape recovery of the seat ring is sufficiently rapid and stiff to move the seat ring, or a part of it, across the sealing face of the poppet and shear off partially cured sealant film. The snap-back wipe is produced in the preferred embodiment by superelastic recovery of the NiTi seat ring; the specification does not assert that every radially compliant seat element produces a snap-back wipe of the same magnitude, and an embodiment employing a seat element with slower or weaker recovery (for example a polymer seat ring) is described as still providing the air purge, whether or not it also provides a comparably effective wipe.

#### Fluid, chamber and cycle terms

As used herein, **"sealant"** means a liquid, for example a latex emulsion containing ammonia and suspended particles, introduced into the tyre chamber through the tyre valve to seal punctures.

As used herein, **"sealant film"** means a layer of sealant drawn into the valve core that dries and cures on a surface of the valve core between inflation cycles.

As used herein, **"tyre chamber"** means the pressurised volume, for example a bicycle inner tube or a tubeless tyre and rim cavity, into which the tyre valve admits air.

As used herein, **"inflation cycle"** means one application and removal of a pressure differential across the poppet by a pump head.

#### Alternative-embodiment terms

As used herein, **"two-piece seat"** means an arrangement in which the sealing edge is provided by a rigid seat and the functions of opening the purge gap and, where present, the snap-back wipe are provided by a separate wiper ring below the rigid seat.

As used herein, **"wiper ring"** means, in a two-piece seat, a thin, radially compliant ring positioned below the rigid seat and deflected outward by the purge shoulder during over-travel to open a purge flow path, and that undergoes shape recovery on removal of the load.

As used herein, **"thermal variant"** means an embodiment in which the seat ring is trained with two-way shape memory and has an austenite finish temperature of approximately 35°C to 40°C, such that warming the seat ring, rather than a pressure-driven over-travel of the poppet, opens the purge gap.

#### Dimensional and process terms

As used herein, **"convolution amplitude"** means the height of a convolution of the convoluted wall, measured from the mean line of the wall.

As used herein, **"purge gap width"** means the radial size of the purge gap when fully open, in the preferred (annular) embodiment of the purge gap; where the purge gap of a given embodiment is other than annular, the corresponding dimension is the transverse opening of that embodiment's purge gap at its narrowest point when fully open.

As used herein, **"shape-setting"** means a heat treatment applied to a formed seat ring, in a fixture holding the intended convolution or other load-converting geometry, that fixes that geometry into a shape-memory or superelastic material such as NiTi alloy.

## Rationale

"Seat ring" is defined at the genus level directed by `LOCKED.md` note 3, because Gate 1 ran unattended and Q1 (01 (m) M1; 03 Q1) — whether superelastic recovery is essential — remains open. The genus covers the confirmed equivalent-architecture library entries (slotted sleeve, split seat ring, tapered seat ring) and the two-piece seat without asserting any of them is equivalent in performance to the NiTi prototype; the species language keeps the convoluted-wall, one-piece form as preferred.

"Purge gap" is defined broadly enough to cover the confirmed through-wall and non-annular alternatives (design-around DA-5 and equivalent-architecture library item "purge flow through the seat ring wall"), using "secondary flow path", the genus already present in `LOCKED.md`'s Broader column for this term, so the definition does not introduce an undeclared synonym. The annular, core-body-bore-bounded form is kept as the preferred species because it is the only one with the prototype's dimensional support (N6); the boundary conflict between the interference fit and the outer-surface purge gap (01 (m) M2; 03 Q2) is not resolved by this definition and is carried to Open Questions.

"Over-travel differential" and "over-travel" are broadened to cover the confirmed equivalent-architecture library's displacement-actuated and seat-ring-stiffness alternatives, using "activation differential", the genus already present in `LOCKED.md` for this term, so the pressure-differential species remains the default reading everywhere the broader alternative is not in play.

"Snap-back wipe" is deliberately *not* broadened to assert that every radially compliant seat element produces it: the bench test does not separate the air purge from the wipe (01 (m) M9; 03 Q1), and Alt-A is disclosed only as giving a "weaker" wipe, not a confirmed absent or present one. Asserting a uniform wipe across all seat ring species would describe unconfirmed breadth as enabled, which the Specification Drafter's own enablement discipline forbids; the definition instead ties the wipe to embodiments where recovery is "sufficiently rapid and stiff", leaving the boundary of that class to the enablement audit (Prompt 17) and to inventor confirmation.

"Purge gap width" keeps its disclosed radial, annular meaning as the primary definition (matching the `LOCKED.md` functional description and the only parameter with prototype data) and adds a narrow fallback reading for non-annular embodiments, rather than redefining the parameter itself, since no dimensional data exists for any non-annular species.

No new terms were introduced; every genus phrase used to broaden a definition ("radially compliant seat element", "secondary flow path", "activation differential", "load-applying surface") is already present in `terminology/LOCKED.md`'s Synonyms or Broader (genus) columns for the corresponding preferred term, and each definition states the preferred term itself alongside the genus phrase.
