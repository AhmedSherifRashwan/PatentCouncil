## Rationale

**Claim 1 re-cast on F2.** `P5/24.md` made the re-cast conditional on the description defining the two travel ranges without pressure. It does: [0071] ends the normal stroke where the load-applying surface first meets the radially deflectable element, and [0072] calls the pressure trigger "the preferred actuation". Claim 1 therefore recites the two pillars of PF-1 (two travel ranges of one poppet with the element loaded in the second; a purge gap that is a secondary flow path distinct from the main passage) and the mechanical load path of PF-2, and nothing else. The cost is recorded, not hidden: without the thresholds the distinction over ref-02 claim 4 rests on the spacing throughout the normal stroke and on the separate flow path. Claim 5 restores the thresholds as the first fallback.

**"Spaced from" and not "only".** Lesson L1. The spacing throughout the normal stroke is a positive, observable condition ([0071], [0102], [0299]).
