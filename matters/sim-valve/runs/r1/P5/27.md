## Rationale

Selection. A concept is listed only where it has value apart from the four claims chosen in Prompt 21 (valve core, method of use, seat ring, method of manufacture of the seat ring): a different defendant (concepts 1, 11, 13), a design-around the differentiation matrix names (concepts 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 14), a threshold or material fallback (concepts 2, 9, 10, 15, 17), or a separate invention (concept 16). The replacement valve core of `P2/10` item 2 is not listed because the lead valve core claim already reads on it.

Concepts 3 and 5 matter most for the first filing. `P2/09` Q2 says the lead distinction is not enabled until the interference fit and the purge gap are reconciled. The relieved core body bore and axial clamping are the two answers the blueprint carries, both class C. If the inventor ratifies either, the purge gap has an enabled form and both become amendment positions.

Concept 10 is drafted without the purge gap deliberately. With the purge gap it is only a dependent claim of the lead claim; without it, it is a second invention. At the EPO and in the UK, claiming it later from a description that only ever shows the wipe together with the air purge risks an intermediate-generalisation objection (Art. 123(2) EPC; s 76 Patents Act 1977), and in the US a written-description objection. So the passage must be in the first filing, and it can only go in if the inventor says the wipe works alone. I did not soften this into "supported".

Concept 8 follows the blueprint, not `P4/19` or the registry. The three disagree on whether the two-piece seat has a purge path. The blueprint's position (none described, held) is the one traceable to the inventor's words, so the concept claims deflection and wipe only.

`P4/19` also defines over-travel and the over-travel differential broadly enough to include displacement actuation and seat ring stiffness, both of which the blueprint holds. I treated them as held. A definition cannot supply support that no embodiment paragraph gives.

Declined limitations. No spring rates (none disclosed). No purge shoulder dimensions. No ratio between convolution amplitude and wall thickness: the numbers would allow one to be computed, but the inventor never stated a relationship, and a computed ratio would be an invented fact. No assembly step for the poppet or return spring. No statement of where the matching taper of the tapered seat ring sits.

True omnibus claims ("substantially as described with reference to the drawings") are not proposed. My understanding is that they are not accepted in the US, are excluded at the EPO save where absolutely necessary (Rule 43(6) EPC), and have been disallowed in the UK for applications since 2017; counsel to confirm. "Omnibus" here means the collected sub-combinations.

Terminology. No term from the lock's avoid list is used. Class C component names are taken from `registry.json` and the `new_terms` of `P4/16`.
