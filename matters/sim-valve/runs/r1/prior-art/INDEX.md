## Coverage — read this before relying on the set

- **No reference found reads on C1 + C2 + C3 together**, and none in the tyre-valve field discloses a deformable seat
  that opens a secondary flow path, a two-rate return spring, or a superelastic seat. Nothing scored 4 or 5. That is
  a finding about *this search*, not evidence that such art does not exist.
- The search is thin. It ran through a general web-search engine restricted to patents.google.com plus direct
  page fetches by number; Espacenet, the USPTO full-text system, CNIPA, DEPATISnet and J-PlatPat were not queried,
  so Chinese, German, Japanese and Taiwanese utility models on bicycle valve cores — where this art is most likely
  to sit — are essentially unsearched. No non-patent literature (component catalogues, product pages for
  "anti-clog" or sealant-resistant cores, cycling trade press) was reviewed.
- C4 (wipe) and C3 (air purge) are covered only by industrial valves from the 1960s–70s; C5 only by downhole and
  engine seals; C1 only by fluid-power check valves. An examiner's combination attack will most likely be built
  from these fields plus the tyre-valve problem references (#6, #7).
- **A professional search is warranted before filing**, prioritising B60C29/F16K15/20 utility models in CN/TW/DE
  and commercial valve-core products marketed as sealant-resistant.
