## Deliverable

### Inventive Nucleus

Sources: `intake/disclosure.md` (cited §n), `runs/r1/P1/01.md` (cited 01 (x)), `intake/data/bench-test.md` (cited bench data). Prompt 02 did not exist when this was written and is not relied on. This is a drafting and analysis aid, not a view on patentability; qualified patent counsel signs off on anything filed.

**N0 — Nucleus (minimum combination that appears to produce the technical effect).** A valve core for a sealant-charged tyre in which:

1. the poppet has a normal opening stroke, reached above the crack pressure, and a further over-travel, reached only when the pump applies a higher over-travel differential (§2; §3 item 5; 01 (b) feature 1);
2. during over-travel the poppet bears on a radially deflectable element, which converts that axial load into radial deflection and so opens a purge gap, a secondary flow path beside the main passage, through which air at pump pressure sweeps the surfaces where sealant collects and carries the film into the tube (§2; §3 items 3, 6; 01 (b) feature 2);
3. when the differential collapses the poppet returns, the element recovers its shape and the purge gap closes, so the valve seals at rest (§2; §3 item 6 "closed at rest"; 01 (b) feature 3).

Neither the material of the element nor the way the over-travel differential is set forms part of N0. The disclosure itself describes a polymer ring (Alt-A) and a two-piece seat with a separate stainless spring wiper ring (Alt-B) (§6), and it gives only one way of setting the threshold (the two-rate return spring) without saying that it is the only way (01 (m) M6). Both points are provisional; see A2, A3, Q1, Q3.

**Ladder of narrower versions** (each rung includes every rung above it):

- **N1 — one-piece seat.** The radially deflectable element is the seat ring itself: the sealing face of the poppet seats on its upper edge, so one part both seals and opens the purge gap (§3 items 3, 4). Excludes Alt-B.
- **N2 — geometry.** The seat ring is a short tube with one or two shallow convolutions in its wall, so axial load on its upper edge becomes radial expansion; the purge gap is the annular clearance between the seat ring outer surface and the core body bore, closed at rest (§3 items 3, 6). Covers the NiTi prototype and Alt-A.
- **N3 — mechanism.** A purge shoulder on the poppet, below the sealing face, contacts the seat ring only during over-travel; a two-rate return spring gives a soft first stage for normal opening and a stiffer second stage that the over-travel differential must overcome (§3 items 4, 5).
- **N4 — material and second cleaning action.** The seat ring is superelastic NiTi with an austenite finish temperature below 5 °C, so it is superelastic at riding temperatures, and its fast, stiff recovery produces the snap-back wipe across the poppet face (§2; §4; §5). Excludes Alt-A.
- **N5 — operating window.** The inventor's believed ranges: over-travel 0.4–0.9 mm beyond the normal stroke; over-travel differential 2.5–5.0 bar; crack pressure 0.8–1.5 bar; purge gap 0.10–0.25 mm radial when fully open; seat ring inner diameter 2.0–2.6 mm, wall 0.15–0.30 mm, free length 2.5–4.0 mm, convolution amplitude 0.08–0.20 mm (§4). Only the prototype value in each range is built; the endpoints are unconfirmed (01 (m) M8).
- **N6 — prototype.** 55.8 wt% Ni NiTi seat ring laser-cut from 2.7 mm OD × 0.20 mm wall tube, shape-set at 500 °C for 10 minutes, water-quenched and electropolished; pressed into the core body with about 0.03 mm diametral interference against an internal shoulder; brass CW614N core body in a 6 mm Presta-pattern valve stem; over-travel 0.5 mm, over-travel differential 3.5 bar, crack pressure 1.0 bar, purge gap 0.15 mm (§3, §4, §5, §7). This is the only configuration with test data (§8; bench data).

The alternative ladder branch through Alt-B (N0 → two-piece seat: rigid sealing seat plus a separate deflectable wiper ring below it, deflected outward by the purge shoulder) is described by the inventor but not built, and its purge path is not described (§6; Q4).

## Rationale

N0 is stated as function, not structure, because the disclosure itself carries three physically different ways of doing it (NiTi ring, PEEK ring, two-piece seat with a stainless wiper). Anything common to all three and needed for the observed effect went into N0; anything present in only some went down the ladder.

Recovery to a closed purge gap is placed in N0 even though the inventor stresses recovery chiefly for the wipe: a gap that does not close is a leak, which is one of the two failure modes the test measures.

The threshold means is essential only as a function. The two-rate spring is the sole disclosed means, so it carries an enablement flag; it is ranked exemplary because the inventor gives no reason it must be a spring.

The ladder orders geometry (N2) above material (N4) because Alt-A keeps the convoluted wall and changes only the material, so the convolution covers more of the inventor's conception than NiTi does.

The superelastic material and the snap-back wipe are provisional advantageous, not essential, because the bench test cannot tell whether the air purge alone would have given the result. If the inventor says both actions are needed, N4 moves up and Alt-A becomes a weaker fallback.

No new structural terms were coined beyond "radially deflectable element", which is the genus needed to cover the seat ring and the Alt-B wiper ring together.

## Assumptions

- A1 — *Inferred from §3 item 6 ("closed at rest") and §1 (leak-down failure):* recovery of the element to close the purge gap is essential to the effect. Not blocking.
- A2 — *Inferred from §6 Alt-A and Alt-B:* the inventor regards a non-superelastic element as able to produce at least part of the effect, so superelasticity is provisionally advantageous. Blocks P3 and P5 scope until Q1 is answered.
- A3 — *Inferred from §3 item 5 and the absence of any statement of exclusivity:* the two-rate spring is one way of setting the over-travel differential, not the only way. Blocks P3 and P5 scope until Q3 is answered.
- A4 — *Inferred from §6 Alt-B ("separates the sealing function from the purge/wipe function"):* in Alt-B the wiper ring's outward deflection opens a purge flow path. Blocks the Alt-B branch in P3 until Q4 is answered.
- A5 — Carried from 01 A1: the purge gap is bounded by the core body bore, and §2's "stem bore" is loose wording. Blocks P4 description of the purge gap until Q2 is answered.
- A6 — The matter is a fictional test fixture; treated as a real disclosure. Not blocking.

## Open Questions

- Q1 — Does the effect need both the air purge and the snap-back wipe, or does either alone keep the valve sealing and opening? This decides whether superelastic material belongs in N0 or N4. (01 Q1, Q9.) Blocks P2, P3, P5.
- Q2 — How does the seat ring carry an interference fit on its outer surface and also open a clearance there on expansion; which bore bounds the purge gap? Without this the N2 geometry may not be enabled as described. (01 Q2.) Blocks P3, P4, P5.
- Q3 — Could the over-travel differential be set by anything other than a two-rate return spring (for example the seat ring's own stiffness)? If the spring is the only means, it moves from exemplary to essential. (01 Q6.) Blocks P3, P5.
- Q4 — In Alt-B, does the wiper ring's outward deflection open a purge flow path, and where? If not, Alt-B is outside N0. Blocks P3, P5.
- Q5 — Did the inventor contemplate any seat ring geometry other than a convoluted wall for converting axial load into radial expansion? If not, the convolution is the only disclosed mechanism for N1. (01 Q3.) Blocks P3, P5.
- Q6 — Is over-travel intended on every inflation with an ordinary floor pump, or only above a certain pressure? This fixes whether the second threshold is essential as drafted in N0. (01 Q7.) Blocks P2, P3, P5.
- Q7 — Is the concept limited to Presta-pattern, removable cores? (01 Q13.) Blocks P2, P3, P5.
- Q8 — Is any §4 range endpoint supported by a build, a calculation or a failed sample? Governs N5. (01 Q8.) Blocks P3, P4, P5.
- Q9 — Does electropolishing contribute to the clean sealing edge? If so it moves from exemplary to advantageous. (01 Q16.) Blocks P3, P4.
