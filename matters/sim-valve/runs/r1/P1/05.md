## Deliverable

### Inventor Interview Questionnaire

Every question folds in an unresolved item from `01` (m), a provisional classification from `03`, or a scope question from `04`; the source is cited after each question. Preferred terms are used verbatim from `terminology/LOCKED.md`. Answers to be recorded in `intake/gate-1-answers.md`. This questionnaire is a drafting and analysis aid; qualified patent counsel signs off on anything filed.

**A — What the effect actually needs (seat ring recovery vs air purge)**

1. Does the self-purging effect require the snap-back wipe, the air purge, or both, to keep the valve sealing and opening over many sealant-loaded inflation cycles? *Why it matters:* decides whether shape recovery is part of the inventive nucleus (N0) or only an advantageous addition (N4) — the single largest driver of independent-claim breadth. (01 M1/Q1; 03 A2/Q1; 04 Q1.) **[blocks P2, P3, P5]**
2. Is a non-superelastic but elastically recovering seat ring (for example the polymer seat ring of Alternative A) an embodiment you intend to pursue as part of the core invention, not only as a lower-cost fallback line? *Why it matters:* determines whether the seat ring limitation in the independent claim can be genus-level (radially compliant, elastically recovering) or must specify superelastic recovery. (01 M1; 03 A2.) **[blocks P3, P5]**
3. Is there any way to test or estimate the air purge's contribution separately from the snap-back wipe's contribution? *Why it matters:* without separation, any claim resting on the wipe alone, or the purge alone, is unsupported by the bench data. (01 M9/Q9.) **[blocks P4, P5]**

**B — Purge gap geometry and the interference fit**

4. Which surface bounds the purge gap — the core body bore or the valve stem bore — and how does the seat ring's interference fit coexist with a purge gap that opens on radial expansion of the same outer surface? *Why it matters:* the disclosure is internally inconsistent on this point (§2 vs §3 item 6) and the geometry cannot be described, drawn or claimed until it is resolved. (01 M2/Q2; 03 Q2; 04 Q2.) **[blocks P3, P4, P5]**

**C — Convolution geometry and manufacture**

5. How many convolutions did the prototype seat ring have, and does the convolution run circumferentially or axially around the convoluted wall? *Why it matters:* fixes the structure that can be drawn and claimed as built, rather than left as "one or two" and undirected. (01 M3/Q3; 04 Q3.) **[blocks P3, P4, P5]**
6. Is the convolution formed by the laser cutting itself (a shaped cut through the wall) or by the shape-setting fixture deforming a plain-walled tube into the wave? Is the seat ring wall continuous or slotted? *Why it matters:* the manufacturing method claim (commercial value map row 4) cannot be drafted correctly until the forming step is known. (01 M3/Q3.) **[blocks P4]**
7. Was any seat ring geometry other than a convoluted wall considered for converting axial load into radial expansion? *Why it matters:* a "yes" lets the independent claim use a functional wall limitation with the convoluted wall as a dependent species, closing a design-around; a "no" keeps the convoluted wall itself as the disclosed and claimable structure. (03 Q5.) **[blocks P3, P5]**
8. Does electropolishing of the seat ring contribute to the clean sealing edge, or is it solely for removing oxide and the heat-affected edge from the laser cut? *Why it matters:* decides whether electropolishing moves from an exemplary process step to an advantageous one worth its own dependent claim. (01 M16/Q16; 03 Q9.) **[blocks P3, P4]**

**D — The snap-back wipe mechanism**

9. Which surface of the seat ring contacts which surface of the poppet during shape recovery, and in what direction does that contact move across the poppet? *Why it matters:* the wipe cannot be described or claimed as a step or structure without a defined contact path; only the outcome (clean surfaces at cycle 40) is currently evidenced. (01 M5/Q5.) **[blocks P3, P4, P5]**

**E — Purge air flow path**

10. Where does purge air enter and leave the purge gap, and which surfaces — sealing edge, sealing face, poppet stem, seat ring outer surface — does it sweep on the way through? *Why it matters:* the cured film found on the seat ring's outer surface at cycle 40 suggests the purge gap region itself is not fully self-cleaning; the flow path needs describing before the specification can explain that observation. (01 M4.) **[blocks P4, P5]**

**F — The over-travel trigger**

11. Could the over-travel differential be set by something other than the disclosed two-rate characteristic of the return spring — for example by the seat ring's own resistance to further poppet travel, or a mechanical stop? *Why it matters:* a "yes" lets the threshold be claimed as a functional limitation not tied to a spring, closing the design-around the commercial value map already flags (row 5) as the most exposed sub-mechanism; a "no" keeps the two-rate return spring as an essential limitation. (01 M6/Q6; 03 Q3; 04 Q4.) **[blocks P3, P5]**
12. How is the two-rate characteristic of the return spring actually produced — variable pitch, two springs in series or nested, a spring plus a mechanical stop, or some other construction — and what are the first and second spring stage rates? *Why it matters:* needed to enable the two-rate return spring as built, independent of whether it turns out to be the only disclosed means. (01 M6/Q6.) **[blocks P4]**

**G — Over-travel versus real pumps**

13. Is over-travel intended to occur on every normal inflation with an ordinary floor pump, or only above a certain tyre pressure or with certain pump types (floor pump versus mini-pump versus compressor)? *Why it matters:* an unqualified "occurs during inflation" claim limitation needs this to be true of ordinary use; if not, the claim and any "in use" functional language must be qualified by pressure or pump type. (01 M7/Q7; 03 Q6.) **[blocks P2, P3, P5]**

**H — Basis for the operating ranges**

14. For each dimension and pressure range in §4 (seat ring inner diameter, wall thickness, free length, convolution amplitude, over-travel distance, purge gap width, crack pressure, over-travel differential), is the stated range endpoint supported by a build, a calculation, or belief only? *Why it matters:* endpoints without a stated basis are candidates the claims can carry only as "about" the built value, not as a supported range, until confirmed. (01 M8/Q8; 03 Q8.) **[blocks P3, P4, P5]**
15. Is there an intended range for the normal stroke (currently only the single prototype value of 0.6 mm is given)? *Why it matters:* every other operating parameter has a range; the normal stroke is the one gap in the ladder at N5. (01 M8, table row.) **[blocks P4]**

**I — Fatigue life**

16. What is the target fatigue life for the seat ring in a commercial product (the disclosure says "several thousand" cycles are needed — is there a specific number), and is there any data or engineering estimate for ring behaviour, or for whether the purge gap stays closed at rest, beyond the 40 over-travel cycles tested? *Why it matters:* fatigue life bears on the enablement of a long-service embodiment and is a direct item in the Prosecution Assessment's deficiency table regardless of claim scope. (01 M10/Q10.) **[blocks P3, P4]**

**J — Cold behaviour and CO₂ inflation**

17. Is the specified austenite finish temperature below 5 °C believed to give a sufficient margin for CO₂ cartridge inflation, and what is the martensite start temperature of the material used? Does a cold-shocked seat ring recover once it warms, or stay deformed until then? *Why it matters:* CO₂ inflation is a named but untested use case; without an answer the specification can only flag it as a known limitation, not claim a cold-tolerant embodiment. (01 M11/Q11.) **[blocks P3, P4]**

**K — Sealant chemistry**

18. Is the self-purging effect expected to work with ammonia-free sealants, which cure differently from the ammonia-bearing latex emulsion tested? *Why it matters:* the tested sealant chemistry is not the only one on the market; an unqualified "sealant" claim term needs this answered or flagged as untested scope. (01 M12/Q12.) **[blocks P3, P4]**

**L — Valve standard and removability**

19. Is the invention limited to a Presta-pattern, removable valve core, or does it also cover other valve standards (for example Schrader) and/or a non-removable, integral core construction? *Why it matters:* the answer sets the genus for "valve stem" and "valve core" in the independent claim; a broader answer closes an easy design-around by valve pattern. (01 M13/Q13; 03 Q7; 04 Q5.) **[blocks P2, P3, P5]**

**M — Alternative B (two-piece seat)**

20. In the two-piece seat of Alternative B, does the wiper ring's outward deflection actually open a purge flow path for air, and if so where does that path run relative to the rigid seat? *Why it matters:* without a described flow path, Alternative B cannot be written up as a supported alternative embodiment; the design-around it might otherwise close stays open. (03 A4/Q4.) **[blocks P3, P5]**

**N — Alternative C (thermal, hand-warm variant)**

21. Should the two-way shape-memory, hand-warm purge variant of the thermal variant be developed as a claimed embodiment, kept only as discussed background, or dropped from the application entirely? *Why it matters:* decides whether a separate, pump-free claim family (commercial value map row 9) is pursued, and how much of Specification Drafter's and Claims Architect's effort goes to it. (01 M14/Q14.) **[blocks P3, P4]**

**O — Missing dimensions and the stainless variant**

22. What are the poppet dimensions, the sealing face cone angle, the purge shoulder geometry, the core body bore dimensions, and the return spring dimensions and tolerances? *Why it matters:* these are the dimensions a drawing and a fully enabled description need that the disclosure does not give at all. (01 M15/Q15.) **[blocks P4]**
23. Was the stainless core body variant tested with sealant, or only machined? *Why it matters:* untested, it can be described only as a considered material substitution, not as a working alternative with its own evidence. (01 M17/Q17.) **[blocks P4]**

**P — Commercial intent (affects claim-family priority, not technical scope)**

24. Is the commercial plan to sell complete branded valve cores, to license the seat ring technology to existing core manufacturers, or both? *Why it matters:* changes whether the component claim (seat ring alone) or the process claim (seat ring manufacture) is the higher-priority claim family. (02 A2/Q1.) **[blocks P5]**
25. Are the stainless core body variant and the thermal variant actual planned products, or purely exploratory? *Why it matters:* decides whether they are prioritised for embodiment expansion now or left as candidates pending Gate 2/3. (02 Q2.) **[blocks P3, P5]**
