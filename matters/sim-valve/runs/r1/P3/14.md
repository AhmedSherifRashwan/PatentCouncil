1. Electropolishing (§5, §7) is disclosed as a step for a metal seat ring. At the generic seat ring rung, material is not yet specified, so compatibility depends on the species later selected: compatible for a metal species (superelastic NiTi seat ring), not credible for the polymer seat ring — see note 2.
2. Electropolishing is an electrochemical process for removing the oxide layer and the heat-affected edge from a metal seat ring (§7); the disclosure gives no basis, and none is technically credible, for electropolishing a polymer seat ring.
3. The two-piece seat's wiper ring is described as stainless steel and its rigid seat as brass (§6), both polishable metals, but the disclosure does not state whether either is electropolished. Needs inventor confirmation (see Q3).
4. No data pairs a hard-anodised aluminium sealing face with the polymer seat ring; the disclosure says nothing about this specific interaction. Flagged for inventor confirmation, not asserted (see Q4).
5. The convoluted wall, and so its convolution count, is not part of the generic seat ring rung; the feature applies once the convoluted-wall geometry is adopted.
6. The disclosure does not describe the wall geometry of the two-piece seat's wiper ring, including whether it has any convolution (`03` Q4, Q5). Applying a one- or two-convolution wall to the wiper ring is undescribed and needs inventor confirmation (see Q1).
7. Austenite finish temperature is a transformation-temperature property of a shape-memory alloy; it applies only where the species selected at this rung is a NiTi alloy.
8. The polymer seat ring (PEEK) is not a shape-memory alloy and has no austenite finish temperature (§6).
9. The two-piece seat's wiper ring is described as stainless steel, not NiTi (§6); as disclosed, the two-piece seat has no component with an austenite finish temperature.
10. The thermal variant requires a seat ring trained with two-way shape memory (§6); this applies only where the species at this rung is a NiTi alloy.
11. The thermal variant is described as a NiTi ring with two-way shape memory, distinct from the superelastic, one-way-trained NiTi seat ring of the main embodiment (§6). Whether a single ring can be trained to show both the pump-driven over-travel behaviour and the hand-warm two-way behaviour, or whether these are mutually exclusive species, is not stated (`03` M14/Q14; see Q2).
12. Two-way shape memory is a shape-memory-alloy property; the disclosure gives no basis for a polymer ring exhibiting it.
13. The disclosure does not state whether the two-piece seat's wiper ring could be made of a two-way-trained NiTi alloy in place of stainless steel, or whether the thermal variant's actuation could open the two-piece seat's purge path. Undescribed (see Q2).

**Commercially important or patentably distinct combinations**

1. Superelastic NiTi seat ring + electropolishing + stainless-steel core body. The seat-ring/mechanism combination with bench data behind it (§7, §8), paired with the disclosed corrosion-resistant body variant (§5) for ammonia-bearing sealant. Primary claimed combination and its most-disclosed fallback.
2. Superelastic NiTi seat ring + lock nut. The ordinary sellable product configuration; a commercial core is expected to carry a lock nut for storage and transport (§3 item 7). Should be the default dependent combination under the main embodiment.
3. Polymer seat ring + core body of stainless steel. The inventor's own named lower-cost line (§6, Alt-A) combined with the corrosion-resistant body variant (§5, Alt-D). Patentably distinct because it substitutes both the seat-ring material and the body material away from the tested NiTi/brass configuration, giving a design-around-resistant fallback family independent of row 1.
4. Convoluted-wall seat ring (either convolution count) + superelastic NiTi seat ring + sealing face of hard-anodised aluminium. Combines the disclosed geometry choice with a poppet-side material variant (§5); a dependent-claim combination a competitor cannot avoid simply by changing the poppet material.
5. Two-piece seat + core body of stainless steel. A materially independent fallback family (Commercial Value Map row 7): protects the two-piece design-around route the inventor has already named (§6) even where the one-piece seat ring claim is narrowed or lost, and the body-material variant is orthogonal to which seat architecture is used.

## Rationale

Columns follow `03`'s nucleus ladder rungs plus its two named alternatives rather than inventing new embodiment groupings, so the matrix stays traceable to what Phase 1 already tagged essential/advantageous/exemplary. Rows are the features `03` classifies advantageous or exemplary, plus the lock nut (included from `01(e)` despite its "conventional" tag, as a completeness check that a feature genuinely independent of the seat-ring choice reads as all-compatible). Cells are marked `conditional` rather than `compatible` wherever the combination depends on an unresolved open question from `01`/`03` (material species, Alt-B's undescribed geometry, the thermal variant's relationship to the main embodiment), and `incompatible` only where the disclosure gives a material or physical reason a combination cannot work (a shape-memory-specific property applied to a polymer, an electrochemical finishing process applied to a polymer). The draft specification sentences cover only the `compatible` cells, so no sentence asserts a permutation the matrix itself flags as conditional or incompatible.
