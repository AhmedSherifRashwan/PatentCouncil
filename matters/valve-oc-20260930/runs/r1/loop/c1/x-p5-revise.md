## Rationale

The design-around findings (F-32-4, F-33-4) and the light-contact finding (F-33-2) were answered by broadening, not by narrowing: the purge state is now a position of the poppet, and the normal-open state is defined by what matters (main passage open, purge gap closed). The pressure trigger, the depressor and the deflation-side flow became sibling dependents, so old claim 1 survives verbatim as claims 1 plus 2. The recovery clause is tied to the return of the poppet from over-travel, which is how [0060], [0078] and [0098] describe it, and keeps the clause valid for every actuation route. "When dependent on claim 2" was used for the threshold and pressure-range rungs so that the crack pressure and over-travel threshold keep an antecedent without collapsing those rungs to single dependency.

For the enablement findings on the illustrated embodiment (F-28-2, F-28-3, F-30-6, F-32-20, F-36-1, F-gate14-2, F-gate14-4) the claims cannot supply what the inventor has not yet stated; the rulings say so and route the fix to the description. At the claims level I did two things that need no new disclosure: I made the outer-surface purge gap claim state the only kinematics by which it can open ([0103]), and I added an inward-deflection rung ([0054]) beside the outward one so that whichever kinematics the inventor confirms has a claim. Claims 4, 5, 22 and 33 keep the description's own wording; the axial layout they rest on is a disclosure need listed in claims-needs.md.

F-gate14-5: claim 61 recites the wipe-only conception of [0065] with the retained-ring qualifier of [0020] and [0052], and takes rungs from the existing ladder so that the two valve core families share their species. F-32-1, F-32-2 and F-30-7 ask for narrowing of claims 79 and 105; the task reserves narrowing to the human, so those go to narrowing_suggested, and the non-narrowing fallbacks the findings themselves name (the matched kit of [0141], assembly into the valve core of claim 1) were added as claims 91 and 118. The existing ladder under claim 79 already contains the continuous sealing edge, superelastic alloy and austenite finish temperature rungs (83, 85, 86) the finding would fold into the independent.

Reference numerals: the 37 and 37b corrections conflict on the deflectable ring element (remove 30 from claim 1; add 30 in claim 79). Removing it everywhere is the consistent resolution because the wiper ring (132) and the stem-integrated seat ring (730) are also deflectable ring elements. Numerals in claim 92 follow the first-mention practice of claims 1–60.
