## Gaps (concepts with no close art found)

1. **N1 in its valve role.** No superelastic or shape-memory seat that a moving poppet seals against. The NiTi seal art found is static (#1, #5).
2. **N3+N4.** No valve in which extended poppet travel loads the seat so that it opens a purge gap closed at rest. Sequential two-path opening exists (#13, #14), but through separate seats or apertures, not a deforming seat ring.
3. **N6 as disclosed.** No seat whose elastic recovery wipes the valve member. Wiping exists only by separate wipers (#3, #7), by a maintenance movement (#4) or by rotation (#12).
4. **Not read, worth reading in a follow-up pass:** CA 2129330 (radially expanding seat ring); US 5,478,047 and US 5,069,240 (flexible metal seats); US 5,120,488 (memory-metal sealing sleeve); US 11,493,139 (tubeless inflation valve); EP 3924195 A1 (possible family of #2); US 12,097,671 (flow tube for sealant); CN 222848756 U (bicycle nozzle); US 11,157,026 and US 3,921,665 (overtravel/two-stage poppets); EP 1001202 A1 and US 4,164,236 (seat self-cleaning); the rudmet.ru article on NiTi disc valves (NPL).
5. **Not searched:** Espacenet and CNIPA classification browsing (F16K 15/20, B60C 29/00, F16K 29/00); JP and KR literature; NPL on NiTi superelastic fatigue and low-temperature (CO2 inflation) behaviour; publication numbers behind commercial "patented"/"patent-pending" tubeless valves.
