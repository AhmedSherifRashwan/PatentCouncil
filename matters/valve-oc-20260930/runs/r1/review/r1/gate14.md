### F-gate14-1 [high] [enablement] The illustrated embodiment's outward deflection is said to open a clearance at the outer surface, which the description itself says requires inward movement

- **Cited.** [0020] "In the illustrated embodiment the radial deflection is outward". [0040] "the purge gap 60 is an annular radial clearance between the outer surface of the seat ring 30 and the core body bore 22". [0053] and [0054] "The radial deflection may be radially outward, as described for the embodiment of FIG. 3". [0093] and FIG. 10B: the wiper ring is deflected "radially outward, opening the purge gap (60) ... between the outer surface of the wiper ring (132) and the core body bore (22)". Contrast [0103]: "Such a purge gap (60) opens where the bounding portion of the outer surface of the seat ring (30) moves away from the core body bore (22). That movement may be an inward movement". The FIG. 1 instructions have the free region "drawn with a small clearance to the core body bore (22) even in the closed state", which contradicts "the purge gap 60 is closed" ([0040]). The registry flags FIG. 6 as resting on "a drafting assumption ... not a confirmed mechanism". Claims affected: claim 29 ("radially outward"), claim 30 (the gap at the outer surface), claim 9 read with [0093], claim 72.
- **Attack.** Moving a surface outward towards a bore narrows the clearance between them; it cannot open it. The description of the only built embodiment, and FIG. 3 and FIG. 10B as instructed, therefore depict a mechanism that the specification's own [0103] rules out. An examiner raises clarity and support (EPC Art. 84; 35 U.S.C. 112(a)/(b)) against claims 29 and 30 and asks how Example 1 works. An opponent argues that the reader cannot tell how the tested prototype opened its purge gap, which undercuts the evidential weight of Example 1 for inventive step. Claim 1 survives on the coherent alternatives in [0103]–[0106], so this is high, not critical.
- **Least narrowing action.** Get the inventor to confirm which surface of the P-1 to P-3 seat ring moves, in which direction, and where the purge gap opens. Then conform [0020], [0040], [0053], [0054], [0093] and the FIG. 1/3/6/10B instructions to that account. If the gap is at the outer surface and opens by inward movement, [0103] already supplies the wording. No claim narrowing is needed. Claim 29 stays valid for the inner-surface arrangement ([0105]).
- **New disclosure required:** yes (inventor confirmation of the prototype kinematics, and drawings).

### F-gate14-2 [high] [enablement] The text does not allow the axial layout of the seat ring, the sealing edge, the internal shoulder and a chamber-side purge shoulder to be reconstructed

- **Claim language.** Claim 3: "a purge shoulder (44) of the poppet on the chamber side of the sealing face". Claim 4: "bears axially on the deflectable ring element in over-travel". Claim 8: a seat ring "having a sealing edge (34) on which the sealing face rests".
- **Cited.** [0046]: the poppet "moves ... towards the chamber side, lifting the sealing face (42) from the sealing edge (34)", so the sealing face lies on the chamber side of the sealing edge. [0088]: the seat ring inner diameter (2.3 mm, tube-cut, [0089]) "admits the poppet stem (41)". [0038]: the purge shoulder lies "on the chamber side of the sealing face". [0116]: "the chamber-side end of the seat ring (30) ... remote from the sealing edge (34)". [0061]: the ring wall "returns radially across the surface of the poppet (40) as the poppet (40) passes it".
- **Attack.** On opening, every point of the poppet moves towards the chamber. A shoulder that sits behind the sealing face, on the chamber side, can only bear on the seat ring if part of the ring lies further towards the chamber than the shoulder. The description never says how that happens when the ring's bore only admits the stem, and [0116] places the sealing edge at the supply-side end. With no drawing sheets, an examiner or court reading the words alone cannot build the preferred embodiment of claims 3 and 4. The result is a clarity and enablement objection (Art. 83/84; 112(a)) and, at litigation, a construction fight over "on the chamber side of".
- **Least narrowing action.** Add one paragraph, from the inventor's own sketch FIG-1.png, stating the axial order of the parts: supply-side end to chamber-side end, which ring end carries the sealing edge, where the internal shoulder is, and where the purge shoulder lies relative to the poppet head. Draw it. If the shoulder is not in fact on the chamber side of the sealing face, conform [0021], [0038], [0112] and claim 3. No narrowing of claim 1 is needed.
- **New disclosure required:** yes.

### F-gate14-3 [high] [formal] The abstract, the drawing sheets, the brief description of the drawings and the field/background/summary sections are absent, and [0160] refers to a background that does not exist

- **Cited.** The package contains no abstract. The figures exist only as a "Figure Plan" and "Figure Instructions". The description begins at "#### Definitions and conventions" [0001] and has no technical field, background, summary or brief description of FIGS. 1–15. [0160]: "The conventional valve cores thus exhibited both of the failure modes described in the background".
- **Attack.** An abstract is a filing requirement in the US (37 CFR 1.72(b)), at the EPO (Art. 78(1)(e)) and in the UK. A brief description of the drawings is required by 37 CFR 1.74 and EPC Rule 42(1)(d). The description relies on FIGS. 1–15 throughout: if it is filed without the drawings, the EPO applies Rule 56 (redating or deletion of references) and US drawing requirements apply under 35 U.S.C. 113. [0160]'s cross-reference points at nothing.
- **Least narrowing action.** The owning drafter supplies the abstract and the list of figures, the field/background/summary sections, and the drawing sheets made from the instructions stripped as in F-gate14-10. They then either point [0160] at the background once it exists, or reword it to "both failure modes, leak-down and blockage, as defined herein" ([0024], [0029]).
- **New disclosure required:** yes (new sections and drawings, derived from existing content).

### F-gate14-4 [high] [formal] The claim structure is not adapted to the target jurisdictions

- **Claim language.** Claim 10 is "any one of claims 1 to 9" and includes claims 8 and 9, which are themselves multiple dependent ("any one of claims 1 to 7"). Claim 13 is "claim 11 or claim 12", where claim 11 depends on claim 10. Similar chains run through claims 18, 25, 28, 29, 32–36, 41–57, 61 ("according to any one of claims 1 to 57"), 64 ("any one of claims 58 to 63"), 89 and 99. There are three independent product claims in one category: 1, 58 and 67 (plus 64 and 66). There are 102 claims in total.
- **Attack.** US: a multiple dependent claim may not serve as a basis for another multiple dependent claim, directly or indirectly (35 U.S.C. 112(e); 37 CFR 1.75(c)). Such claims are objected to and not treated on the merits, and they add multiple-dependent and excess-claim fees. EPO: more than one independent claim per category needs a Rule 43(2) exception (interrelated products may be argued for claims 1, 58 and 67 but must be argued), and claims fees apply from claim 16. UK: excess-claims fees apply.
- **Least narrowing action.** Counsel prepares a US claim set with single dependencies, carrying the highest-value combinations as separate claims, and prepares the Rule 43(2) interrelated-products justification for the EPO. This does not narrow scope.
- **New disclosure required:** no.

### F-gate14-5 [high] [design-around] The wipe-only embodiment is disclosed but no independent claim covers it

- **Claim language.** Claim 1: "the radial deflection opening the purge gap so that a purge flow path (62), separate from the main passage, extends from the supply side through the purge gap to the chamber side". Claims 58 and 79 have the same requirement. Claim 67 covers a seat ring only within its dimensional window.
- **Cited.** [0065]: "in some embodiments it is provided by the snap-back wipe alone, the deflectable ring element being sealed at its periphery so that no inflation gas flows past it while it is deflected". [0167]: the surfaces found clean are "the surfaces across which the seat ring moves during the snap-back wipe", while film remained in the purge-gap region. [0165]: the data "do not separate" the two actions.
- **Attack.** A competitor seals the periphery of the ring, or omits the purge path, and keeps over-travel deflection and recovery wiping of the sealing face. The package's own inspection result ([0167]) lets that competitor argue it obtains the same result on the sealing surfaces. It escapes claims 1, 58 and 79, and it escapes claim 67 outside the Presta dimensions.
- **Least narrowing action.** Add an independent valve-core claim, supported by [0065], [0124] and [0061], in which the poppet in over-travel radially deflects a deflectable ring element carried by the core body, and the element's shape recovery moves it across a surface of the poppet. The purge gap is not required. Claim 1 stays as it is. No new disclosure is needed; unity with claim 1 is to be weighed by counsel.
- **New disclosure required:** no.

### F-gate14-6 [medium] [design-around] Disclosed routes into over-travel other than the supply-side pressure differential fall outside claims 1, 58 and 79

- **Claim language.** Claim 1: "a purge state, at a pressure differential exceeding the over-travel threshold, in which the poppet is in over-travel ... and produces a radial deflection". Claim 46 adds a depressor only "additionally".
- **Cited.** [0066]: a depressor in the pump head or an adaptor, "whatever the pressure differential ... the second spring stage (54) may be omitted", or pressing by hand. [0067]: "in other embodiments it is reached during deflation only". [0123]: a separate purge actuator, "the poppet (40) performing only its normal stroke". [0137]: a Schrader poppet "may be depressed by the head of the inflation device".
- **Attack.** A Schrader-format competitor, or a Presta core sold with a depressor adaptor, set so that pump pressure alone never loads the ring, arguably has no pressure-defined purge state. Likewise, the purge-actuator design has no poppet over-travel. The result is close, although the depressor-only route loses the "no user action" benefit ([0064]).
- **Least narrowing action.** Add a dependent-free alternative independent claim, or reformulate claim 1's purge state as "in over-travel beyond the normal stroke, produced by a pressure differential exceeding the over-travel threshold or by mechanical depression of the poppet" (support [0066]). A separate claim to the purge-actuator form is supported by [0123].
- **New disclosure required:** no.

### F-gate14-7 [medium] [design-around] [0009] invites a means-plus-function construction of the central genus terms

- **Cited.** [0009]: "'deflectable ring element', 'threshold-setting arrangement' ..., 'deflecting formation' ... Each such term refers to any structure capable of performing the recited function, consistent with the corresponding structures described in this specification and their equivalents". Claim 1 recites "a deflectable ring element". Claim 2 recites "a deflecting formation". Claim 36 recites "a threshold-setting arrangement".
- **Attack.** In US litigation this wording is the applicant's own concession that the terms are function-plus-structure terms limited to the "corresponding structures ... and their equivalents" (35 U.S.C. 112(f)). A competitor's ring of a geometry not described (for example an elastomer-backed metal band) is then argued outside claim 1 even though it performs the function. "Threshold-setting arrangement" already draws a nonce-word challenge.
- **Least narrowing action.** Replace the second sentence of [0009] with a structural definition of each term (an annular element carried by the valve core, and so on, as in [0014], [0021] and [0022]). Delete "corresponding structures ... and their equivalents". Counsel decides whether to recite structural language in claim 36.
- **New disclosure required:** no.

### F-gate14-8 [medium] [support] Multiple dependencies generate combinations the description excludes or does not support

- **Claim language.** Claim 24 ("an integral flexure section of the core body") depends on "any one of claims 1 to 17", which includes claim 8 (a seat ring (30)) and claims 16–17 (seat ring dimensions). Claim 34 (flutes closed by the outer surface "lying on lands") depends on "any one of claims 1 to 33", which includes claim 29 ("radially outward"). Claims 13, 35 and 41–43 read with claims 9, 51 and 52 (through claims 51 and 52 "any one of claims 1 to 46"). Claims 76–77 (a PEEK seat ring) take claim 67's NiTi-derived dimensions.
- **Cited.** [0096]: "There is then no separate seat ring (30)". [0110]: flutes open when the outer surface "moves away from the core body bore". [0091]: "The ranges correspond to the superelastic seat ring (30) with a convoluted wall (32). A person skilled in the art will adapt them to ... a resilient polymer seat ring ... or of a separate wiper ring". [0094] and [0146] say the same.
- **Attack.** An examiner objects under Art. 84 that these claims are inconsistent with a description which says the numbers do not apply to the combination, or that the combination contradicts itself. In the US the same point arises as written description for the range-plus-material combinations.
- **Least narrowing action.** Trim the dependencies: claim 24 to "any one of claims 1 to 7, 9 to 15"; claim 34 to exclude claim 29; claims 13, 35 and 41–43, and claims 76–77, to be read only with the superelastic seat ring. Alternatively, and only if the inventor has the data, add ranges for the polymer and the wiper ring. Trimming loses no scope that the description supports.
- **New disclosure required:** no.

### F-gate14-9 [medium] [design-around] The kit has no claim and the component claim is dimension-bound

- **Claim language.** Claim 67: "the seat ring having a seat ring inner diameter of from 2.0 mm to 2.6 mm and a seat ring wall thickness of from 0.15 mm to 0.30 mm". There is no kit claim.
- **Cited.** [0141] describes a kit of the seat ring, the poppet with its purge shoulder, and the return spring with two stages, "matched so that ... the purge shoulder (44) is spaced from the seat ring (30) throughout the normal stroke and bears on the seat ring (30) only in over-travel". Also FIG. 5 and [0180].
- **Attack.** A supplier sells the matched kit without a core body, or a Schrader-scale seat ring outside the window of claim 67. Claim 1 requires the core body, so only contributory or indirect infringement is available, which is uncertain across the US, EPO states and the UK.
- **Least narrowing action.** Add a kit claim supported by [0141]: the seat ring, the poppet and the return spring matched to give the claim-1 states when assembled into a core body. The dimensional limits of claim 67 are left to counsel's novelty judgement.
- **New disclosure required:** no.

### F-gate14-11 [medium] [formal] "About", "approximately" and "nominally" in claims, where [0007] disclaims any tolerance

- **Claim language.** Claims 20 and 101: "approximately 0.03 mm". Claim 44: "about 1.0 bar ... about 3.5 bar". Claims 49, 75 and 98: "nominally 55.8 wt% nickel content".
- **Cited.** [0007]: "it does not itself define a specific numerical tolerance ... without asserting a measured tolerance band".
- **Attack.** The specification expressly withholds the guidance that EPO practice and US definiteness law look for when construing "about". An examiner objects under Art. 84 and 112(b), and in litigation these fallbacks become a plain point value or indefinite.
- **Least narrowing action.** In these fallback claims, recite the value without the qualifier (the worked-example value is disclosed). Alternatively, add a tolerance only if the inventor has one.
- **New disclosure required:** no.

### F-gate14-12 [low] [support] No ranges for the normal stroke, the interference or the shape-setting window

- **Cited.** [0046]: "normal stroke is 0.6 mm" (single value). [0172]: "The working range of the interference has not been determined". [0148] and [0175]: 500 °C/10 min, "not a process window". Claims 20, 94 and 101 are single-point fallbacks.

- **New disclosure required:** yes.

## Assumptions

A1. Common general knowledge: in a Presta-pattern valve core the poppet head seals from the chamber side and supply pressure opens it towards the chamber. This is consistent with [0046] and is used in F-gate14-2.

A2. Elementary geometry: a clearance bounded by a surface and a bore opens only if that surface moves away from the bore. Used in F-gate14-1.

A3. No priority document was supplied (the `intake/priority/` folder is empty); the application is treated as possibly a first filing.

A4. The abstract, the list of figures and the drawing sheets are expected to be produced later in the pipeline. The gate is answered on the package as it stands.

A5. The jurisdictional formal points (35 U.S.C. 112(e)/(f), 37 CFR 1.72(b), 1.74, 1.75(c); EPC Art. 78, Rules 42, 43(2), 56) are stated from general patent-practice knowledge and were not re-verified by research in this task; counsel is to confirm.

A6. The prior art is characterised from `prior-art/INDEX.md`. The saved references were not re-read in this task.
