### F-40a-1 [low] [formal] Independent claims not in EPO/UK two-part form

Paragraph/claim cited: claim 1 (and independents 58, 67, 79, 90), each drafted throughout as a single-part "comprising ... wherein" claim with no "characterised in that" division between a preamble reciting the closest prior art combination and a characterising portion reciting the inventive contribution.

Attack: Rule 43(1) EPC requires, "wherever appropriate," a two-part form identifying features already disclosed in combination in the prior art, followed by a characterising portion. EPO examiners frequently require this form to be adopted at examination, particularly once a closest prior-art document has been identified through search; UK practice follows the EPC-aligned convention. Omission is not itself a substantive patentability defect and is commonly deferred until the closest prior art is fixed by a search report, but its absence here is a real formal gap against the current text.

Least narrowing corrective action: no correction to substance; recommend addressing this at EPO/UK regional-phase entry (or upon receipt of a search opinion identifying the closest prior art), rather than in the present drafting round, since selecting the correct "characterising" split before search results are known risks an unnecessary and premature narrowing signal.

### F-40a-2 [low] [formal] No express statement that reference signs in the claims are non-limiting

Paragraph cited: [0001], "The reference numerals used in this specification are those of the drawings, and the same numeral denotes the same component in every figure in which it appears." This sentence addresses figure-to-figure consistency but does not state that the numerals appearing in the claims (e.g. "(30)", "(40)") do not limit claim scope.

Attack: the absence of this customary disclaimer sharpens the risk identified in the claims audit at F-37-3 (numeral (30) used for both the claim 1 genus and the claim 8 species, conflicting with claim 9's (132)); without an express non-limiting statement, a court or examiner has less basis to discount the numeral as a mere drawing cross-reference.

Least narrowing corrective action: add a sentence to [0001] or immediately following it: "Reference signs appearing in the claims are included for ease of reference to the drawings only and do not limit the scope of the claims."

### F-40a-3 [low] [formal] Inconsistent reference-numeral citation format within the description

Paragraphs cited: [0031]–[0041] cite numerals without parentheses (e.g. [0031] "a valve stem 10 having a stem bore 12," [0033] "The core body 20 is a turned component"), while [0042] onward cite the same numerals in parentheses (e.g. [0042] "the poppet (40)," [0043] "the sealing face (42) of the poppet (40)").

Attack: EPC Rule 43(7) requires that reference signs, where used, be placed in parentheses; the description's structural section ([0030]–[0041]) does not follow this convention while the operational section ([0042] onward) does. This inconsistency would be flagged on EPO formalities examination and, while not a substantive defect, invites an Article 84-adjacent clarity comment and is simple to avoid.

Least narrowing corrective action: standardise all numeral citations in [0031]–[0041] to the parenthetical form used from [0042] onward, e.g. change "a valve stem 10 having a stem bore 12" to "a valve stem (10) having a stem bore (12)," and similarly throughout [0032]–[0041].

### F-40a-4 [medium] [formal] No problem/solution summary precedes the Definitions section (EPO)

Paragraph cited: `description.md` begins at [0001] under the heading "Definitions and conventions," proceeding directly into interpretive conventions; there is no preceding paragraph stating the technical problem addressed and the general nature of the solution.

Attack: Rule 42(1)(c) EPC requires the description to "disclose the invention ... in such terms that the technical problem ... and its solution can be understood, and state any advantageous effects of the invention with reference to the background art." The current structure supplies this material only implicitly, scattered through the Definitions and later operational sections, with no single summary paragraph an EPO formalities or substantive examiner can point to. US practice does not mandate an equivalent summary, but its absence is a genuine EPO/UK formal gap.

Least narrowing corrective action: add a short summary paragraph (or renumber the Definitions section to begin one paragraph later) stating, at a level no narrower than claim 1, the problem (sealant-induced blockage and leak-down in inflation valves) and the solution (a deflectable ring element deflected by poppet over-travel to open a purge gap and provide an air purge, undergoing shape recovery to close the gap and wipe the poppet). This is additive disclosure, not a narrowing of any claim.
