### F-38-1 [medium] [support] [0078] states no heating is involved in reaching the purge state, contradicting the thermally actuated purge embodiment at [0142]

Paragraph/figure cited: [0078], "The geometry is loaded mechanically by the purge shoulder (44), at a defined location and at service temperature; no heating or other external actuation is involved in reaching the purge state." Compare [0142], "the deflectable ring element is formed from a two-way shape-memory alloy of nickel and titanium having an austenite finish temperature of about 35 °C to 40 °C, so that the deflectable ring element changes shape and opens the purge gap (60) when it is warmed above that temperature."

Attack: [0078] is drafted as an unqualified, general statement about "the purge state" as such, not expressly limited to the over-travel-actuated embodiments. [0142] describes a distinct, later-disclosed embodiment in which the purge gap is opened by warming — i.e., precisely the "heating ... involved in reaching the purge state" that [0078] says does not occur. An examiner or opponent could read [0078] as an admission that the thermally actuated embodiment falls outside "the purge state" as elsewhere defined and claimed, or, more damagingly, could use the contradiction to argue the specification is internally inconsistent about a core operating condition, undermining confidence in the enablement of the purge-state definition generally. Because the thermally actuated purge is only a described concept and not currently claimed, the practical claim-scope risk is low, but the contradiction itself is real and would draw a clarity query.

Least narrowing corrective action: add a qualifying clause to [0078] confining it to the over-travel-actuated embodiments, e.g. "...no heating or other external actuation is involved in reaching the purge state by over-travel, as distinct from the thermally actuated purge described below." This is an added-disclosure clarification, not a narrowing of any claim.

Category: support. Requires new disclosure: no (clarifying language only). Suggested target: P4.

### F-38-2 [low] [novelty] Admission that pre-over-travel operation matches a conventional valve core

Paragraph cited: [0047], "Until the over-travel threshold is reached, therefore, the valve core admits inflation gas and seals in the same manner as a conventional valve core, the seat ring (30) serving only to seat the sealing face (42)."

Attack: this is an express admission that everything up to and including the crack-pressure/normal-stroke operation is conventional. While accurate and generally harmless (the novel contribution is the over-travel/purge mechanism, not the normal-open behaviour), an examiner could read "in the same manner as a conventional valve core" broadly to argue that the seat-ring-as-seat function itself (claim 8's "sealing edge on which the sealing face rests") is admitted prior art, which could be deployed against dependent claims that rely on the seat ring's dual sealing/purging role as inventive (e.g. claim 8's combination with claim 30's purge-gap location). The admission is broader than strictly necessary for the point being made.

Least narrowing corrective action: narrow the admission to the specific comparison intended, e.g. "the valve core admits inflation gas at the crack pressure and seals in the closed state in the same manner as a conventional valve core admits and seals, without addressing the seat ring's purge-gap function," so the comparison cannot be read to concede more than the crack-pressure/sealing behaviour.

Category: novelty. Requires new disclosure: no. Suggested target: P4.
