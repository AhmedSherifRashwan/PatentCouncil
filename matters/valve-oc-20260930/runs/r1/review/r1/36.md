### F-36-1 [high] [enablement] The preferred embodiment says outward deflection opens a gap at the outer surface; outward movement closes it

**Location.** [0040]: "the purge gap 60 is an annular radial clearance between the outer surface of the seat ring 30 and the core body bore 22 ... opened only by radial deflection". [0029]/FIG. 3 per `registry.md`: "seat ring (30) shown deflected radially outward ... purge gap (60) shown open". [0093]: "deflects the wiper ring (132) radially outward, opening the purge gap (60) ... between the outer surface of the wiper ring (132) and the core body bore (22)". Claims 29 and 30 read together; claim 9.

**Attack.** An examiner or opponent will make three points:
- A surface that moves outward, towards the bore, reduces the clearance between itself and the bore. The described mechanism of the only built embodiment, and of Example 3, is therefore not credible as written. `registry.md` itself flags that FIG. 6's convention "rests on a drafting assumption ... not a confirmed mechanism", and instructs that FIG. 1 show "a small clearance to the core body bore even in the closed state". That contradicts the purge gap being closed at rest.
- The purge flow must also pass the interference band at the internal shoulder (24) ([0036], [0053]), which by definition is sealed. No route past it is described.
- Claim 1 survives on the other mechanisms ([0054] inward and ovalising movement, [0103], [0106]). But the tested embodiment, claim 30, and claims 29 and 30 read together are open to insufficiency and Art. 84 attack, and the working example no longer shows how the claimed mechanism was realised.

**Least narrowing action.** Obtain from the inventors the mechanism actually used in P-1 to P-3: which surface moves, in which sense, where the gap opens, and how the flow bypasses the retention band. Rewrite [0040], [0053] and [0093] and the FIG. 3, 6 and 10 instructions to match. If the actual movement is inward at the gap or ovalising, amend "outward" in the illustrated embodiment. This is a correction of the description, not a narrowing of claim 1.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P4.

### F-36-2 [high] [support] No drawings exist: the package holds a figure plan that contains internal working references

**Location.** Description [0030] onward cites FIGs. 1–15. `figures.md` opens "### Figure Plan" and cites "01 Q22", "`14.md`", "DA-7, DA-23, DA-24", "Prompt 16 (blueprint) has not yet run". `registry.md` cites "Assumption A2 in Prompt 20" and "Cross-check against description and claims is deferred".

**Attack.** An application filed with the description referring to drawings that are not filed faces three consequences:
- EPO: re-dating under Rule 56 EPC if missing drawings are filed later, or the references are deemed deleted.
- UK: the equivalent late-filed-drawings provisions.
- US: drawings are required where necessary for understanding (35 USC 113).

The mechanism of FIGs. 3, 4, 6 and 11–14 is hard to follow without them. The figure-plan text cannot be filed at all, because it discloses internal working references.

**Least narrowing action.** Produce FIGs. 1–15 from `registry.md` (after F-36-1 is resolved for FIGs. 1, 3, 6 and 10). Complete the deferred numeral cross-check. Add a Brief Description of the Drawings. Make sure nothing from the figure plan's working text enters the filed application.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P4.

### F-36-3 [medium] [support] The package contradicts itself on whether the ring is touched below the over-travel threshold

**Location.** [0118]: "below the over-travel threshold only the main passage (64) is open and the deflectable ring element is not touched". [0122]: the threshold is "set by the force that the deflecting formation needs to deflect the deflectable ring element", with "an initial elastic rise". [0050]. Claims 1 and 40.

**Attack.** In the claim 40 species the formation must bear on the ring before the threshold is reached. That contradicts [0118] and claim 1's normal-open "the deflectable ring element is in its at-rest form". An examiner raises lack of support or clarity for claim 40 read with claim 1. An opponent uses [0118] to fix a narrow construction (33, F-33-1).

**Least narrowing action.** Add control logic for the ring-stiffness regime: the formation may contact and elastically load the ring below the threshold, and the purge gap stays closed until the threshold. Amend [0118] to "is not deflected so as to open the purge gap". Align claim 1 as proposed in 33, F-33-1.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P4.

### F-36-4 [medium] [support] "Inflatable chamber" is defined as bicycle-only; sealant-filled e-scooter, motorcycle and other tyres are left out

**Location.** [0025]: "an 'inflatable chamber' means the volume ... of a bicycle inner tube or of a tubeless bicycle tyre set-up". [0139]. Claims 58 and 79 ("inflatable chamber").

**Attack.** In 18 months a competitor sells the mechanism in e-scooter or motorcycle tubeless valves, often Schrader-pattern. It argues that the definition confines claims 58 and 79 to bicycle chambers. [0139] offers only a general sentence as basis for any later amendment.

**Least narrowing action.** Redefine the inflatable chamber as any tyre or inner tube inflated through the inflation valve and holding liquid sealant, with the bicycle chambers as the tested example. Name the other chambers the inventors contemplate.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P4.

### F-36-5 [medium] [support] No embodiment purges from first lift (zero normal stroke)

**Location.** [0051]: "the over-travel threshold is set close to the crack pressure, so that the purge state is reached on substantially every opening ... the normal stroke is short". Claim 1 requires a normal-open state.

**Attack.** A competitor product in which the deflecting formation opens the purge gap from the first lift of the poppet has no normal-open state and escapes every independent claim. The package discloses a short normal stroke, not a zero one, so there is no basis for a claim family without the normal-open state.

**Least narrowing action.** If within conception, add an embodiment in which the purge gap opens as the poppet lifts from the seat (single-rate spring, no normal stroke), and an independent claim that does not require the normal-open state. Counsel should weigh novelty against index #14 and #16 before claiming it.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P3.

### F-36-6 [medium] [support] Parameter ranges are disclosed only for the superelastic convoluted seat ring but are claimed for every species

**Location.** [0091]: "The ranges correspond to the superelastic seat ring (30) with a convoluted wall (32). A person skilled in the art will adapt them ...". Claims 35, 41, 42 and 43 depend on "any one of claims 1 to 34" (or later), including polymer rings (claim 52), the wiper ring (claim 9) and Schrader cores (claim 57).

**Attack.** For a PEEK ring or a stainless wiper ring, the package itself says the ranges must be adapted. Claiming them for those species lacks support (EPO Art. 84), and any later amendment combining them is an intermediate generalisation.

**Least narrowing action.** State expressly which ranges do not depend on the material or geometry of the ring: the crack pressure and the over-travel threshold, which [0090] ties to inflation devices. Give ranges, or state the adaptation rule, for the purge gap opening and the over-travel distance for the polymer and wiper-ring species. Otherwise restrict claims 35 and 43 to claim 47 with a convoluted wall.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P4.

### F-36-7 [medium] [support] The cheapest competitor combination is disclosed only by a blanket combination statement

**Location.** [0096], [0110], [0122], [0131], joined only by [0143] ("disclosed in combination with each of the others").

**Attack.** A one-piece injection-moulded polymer core body with an integral flexure section, a single-rate spring whose threshold is set by the flexure's stiffness, and moulded flutes is the lowest-cost product a competitor would launch. At the EPO, a blanket combination statement does not provide a direct and unambiguous basis for selecting this combination from several lists.

**Least narrowing action.** Add a written embodiment (a prophetic example, stated as such) describing this combination and how the threshold is set without a second spring stage.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P3.

### F-36-8 [medium] [enablement] Over-travel by a depressor has no geometry or control logic

**Location.** [0066] ("a depressor in the head of the inflation device, or in an adaptor ... pushes the poppet (40) beyond the normal stroke into over-travel"); [0137]. Claims 46 and 57.

**Attack.** The package gives no relation between depressor travel, the normal stroke (0.6 mm) and the over-travel distance (0.5 mm). Nor does it say how a Schrader core, whose pin is depressed by every head, keeps a normal-open state. The Schrader and adaptor variants, and any independent claim built on [0066] (33, F-33-2), are at risk on enablement and support.

**Least narrowing action.** Add the depressor geometry: travel greater than the normal stroke plus a stated fraction of the over-travel distance. Describe the Schrader variant's operating sequence, with or without a normal-open state.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P4.

### F-36-9 [medium] [support] No embodiment opens the purge path by out-of-plane (axial) flexure of the ring

**Location.** [0020] ("not a bodily displacement"), [0082] (a conical disc whose rim moves radially). Every claim recites "radial deflection".

**Attack.** A diaphragm ring flexing axially to lift a lip from an axial face opens a purge path only in over-travel and escapes all claims (33, F-33-4).

**Least narrowing action.** If within conception, add the embodiment and a generic statement that the elastic deformation opening the purge gap may be radial or axial.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P3.

### F-36-10 [low] [support] The diameter measurement convention fails for a wall convoluted along its whole length

**Location.** [0028]: "measured at a portion of the wall free of convolutions". Claims 16 and 67.

**Attack.** With no convolution-free portion, the inner-diameter limitation cannot be measured, and the defendant argues that it is not met.

**Least narrowing action.** Add a fallback convention to [0028], for example the diameter at the sealing edge (34), or the mean of crest and root diameters.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P4.

### F-36-11 [low] [support] No fallback ladder for the austenite finish temperature

**Location.** [0073]: "No value below 5 °C is specified herein"; [0171]. Claims 48 and 74.

**Attack.** If cold-weather or CO2-chilling performance becomes the inventive-step battleground, there is no narrower supported value to retreat to.

**Least narrowing action.** If the inventors have target values, add a nested ladder (for example below 0 °C, below −10 °C) and any martensite start temperature they specify. Do not add values they have not chosen.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P4.

### F-36-12 [low] [enablement] Any test data the inventors hold beyond Example 1 should be added before filing

**Location.** [0159], [0165]: 40 cycles, one sealant, one pump, 22 °C; tubeless use, cold and Examples 2 to 4 untested.

**Attack.** Durability, cold performance and the PEEK and wiper variants are where inventive step and plausibility will be argued. Data held but not filed carries less weight later.

**Least narrowing action.** Ask the inventors whether any further results exist, including P-1 to P-3 run beyond 40 cycles. Add them as actual examples with their scope stated.

**Requires new disclosure:** yes. Target P4.

## Assumptions

A1. EPC Rule 56, the corresponding UK late-filed-drawings provisions and 35 USC 113 are relied on as general knowledge of the rules, not as checked authority.

A2. That an outward-moving surface cannot open a clearance between itself and a surrounding bore is a common-general-knowledge geometric premise.

A3. That a blanket combination statement is weak basis at the EPO for a specific selection from several lists is a general-knowledge premise of EPO practice.

A4. Conception is inferred only from the text. "Appears within conception" does not confirm that the inventors conceived the matter. Every addition is a candidate until the inventor confirms it at a gate.

A5. This analysis is a drafting and analysis aid and does not clear the application for filing; qualified patent counsel signs off.
