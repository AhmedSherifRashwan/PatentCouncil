### F-40a-1 [medium] [formal] Multiple dependent claims improperly built on other multiple dependent claims (US)

**Claim language quoted:** Claim 10: "The valve core of any one of claims 1 to 9, wherein the deflectable ring element has a wall..." — claims 8 and 9, both within that range, are themselves multiple dependent claims ("The valve core of any one of claims 1 to 7...").

**Attack.** 37 CFR 1.75(c) and MPEP 608.01(n)(iii) provide that a multiple dependent claim shall not serve as a basis for any other multiple dependent claim, directly or indirectly. Fifty-three of the 102 claims (listed in the table above) violate this rule by referencing, in the alternative, a range that includes an earlier multiple dependent claim. This is a mechanical objection a US examiner will raise before reaching the merits of any claim in the affected set, and it will delay substantive examination of the application until corrected.

**Least narrowing action.** Restructure the claim dependencies so that no multiple dependent claim's range includes another multiple dependent claim, restoring any combinations that would otherwise be lost as separate claims. No scope need be surrendered; see `37b.md` F-37-1 for the full analysis and the illustrative claim 10 correction.

**Requires new disclosure:** No. **Suggested target:** P5 (claim reformulation).

### F-40a-2 [low] [formal] Independent claims not in EPO two-part form

**Claim language quoted:** Claim 67 (representative): "A seat ring (30) for a valve core of an inflation valve, the seat ring being an annular element having a sealing edge (34)... the wall comprising a convoluted wall (32)..." — drafted throughout as a single "comprising" recitation with no preamble/"characterised in that" split.

**Attack.** Rule 43(1) EPC and the EPO Guidelines (F-IV, 2.2–2.3) expect an independent claim, where the invention is a further development of a known device or process, to be split into a preamble reciting features already disclosed in combination in the closest prior art and a characterising portion introduced by "characterised in that" or "characterised by," reciting the features the application adds. None of the seven independent claims (1, 58, 64, 66, 67, 79, 90) is drafted this way. An EPO examiner will typically invite this recast at the first communication if it is not done on entry.

**Least narrowing action.** Recast each independent claim into two-part form once the closest prior-art document is selected at EPO entry; no scope change. This cannot be finalised now because the reference used to draft the characterising portion has not been chosen — `prior-art/INDEX.md` identifies candidates (e.g. #2 US 11,904,644 B2 or #17 US 12,215,796 B2 for the inflation-valve claims; #1 US 9,982,818 B2 for the seat-ring claim) but states its own coverage is thin and not a professional search.

**Requires new disclosure:** No. **Suggested target:** P5, deferred to EPO regional-phase entry.

### F-40a-3 [low] [formal] Inconsistent reference-sign practice across the claim set

**Claim language quoted:** See `37b.md` F-37-2 (claims 79–89 omit reference numerals present throughout claims 1, 58, 67, 90–102).

**Attack.** While reference signs in claims are optional in every named jurisdiction, using them for some claims and omitting them for structurally parallel claims describing the same numbered components is inconsistent and avoidable, and increases the risk of confusion in claim construction.

**Least narrowing action.** Add the existing reference numerals to claims 79–89 at first mention, as in claim 1; see Correction 3.

**Requires new disclosure:** No. **Suggested target:** in-place.

## Assumptions

A1. Default jurisdictions US, EPO and UK are assessed per the matter's standing instruction, since no `matter.json` override was supplied with this package.

A2. The EPO two-part-form assessment applies the general expectation under Rule 43(1) EPC/EPO Guidelines F-IV, 2.2–2.3; two-part form can be dispensed with where inappropriate (e.g., for a claim to a genuinely new use or a first disclosure with no meaningful single closest prior-art combination), which is a judgment call for EPO counsel once prosecution begins, so this is reported as a likely formal expectation rather than an absolute requirement.

A3. No physical drawing sheets were supplied in this package (only the figure plan and figure/reference-numeral instructions), so drawing-formality requirements (sheet size, margins, figure placement) could not be assessed and are marked not applicable/cannot assess rather than pass or fail.
