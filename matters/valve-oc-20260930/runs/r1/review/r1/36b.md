### F-36-1 [high] [enablement] Load path of the purge shoulder and through-route of the purge flow past the retention band are not stated for the illustrated embodiment

Claim 1: "a purge flow path (62), separate from the main passage, extends from the supply side through the purge gap to the chamber side"; claim 4: "an annular shoulder integral with the poppet which bears axially on the deflectable ring element in over-travel". [0038]: the purge shoulder is "on the chamber side of the sealing face 42" and "in the closed state ... spaced axially from the seat ring 30". [0036] and [0053]: the interference fit "may be confined to an axial band at the internal shoulder (24)". [0056]: gas flows "from the supply side through the purge gap (60) along the purge flow path (62) to the chamber side of the seat ring (30)". Attack: the description does not say which end of the seat ring carries the sealing edge relative to the internal shoulder, which surface of the ring the purge shoulder bears on, or how a formation on the chamber side of the sealing face, moving in the opening direction, comes to load the ring axially; and it does not say how gas in an annular clearance at the ring's outer surface passes the interference band that seats the ring against the internal shoulder at one end. The registry's own flag 3 records that the FIG. 6 interference-band convention "rests on a drafting assumption ... not a confirmed mechanism". The inventor's sketches exist by name only ("no image has been seen", figure plan). An examiner reading the words alone will raise sufficiency and clarity; an opponent will argue the through-flow of claim 1 is not enabled for the FIG. 1 embodiment. Least narrowing action: add to [0034], [0038] and [0056] a plain statement of the axial arrangement (which end of the ring is the sealing edge, where the internal shoulder and band sit, which ring surface the shoulder contacts) and of the route by which the purge flow enters the annular purge gap at the supply side and leaves at the chamber side (for example past the free end of the ring and through the region freed of interference, or through purge ports 262 or flutes 362 where the band would otherwise block). Requires new disclosure taken from the inventor's sketches. Target P4.

### F-36-2 [high] [support] The wipe-only embodiment of [0065] has no claim family

Claim 25: "as the deflectable ring element undergoes shape recovery ... the deflectable ring element moves across a surface of the poppet and shears sealant film from that surface", dependent on claim 1, which requires "the radial deflection opening the purge gap so that a purge flow path (62) ... extends from the supply side through the purge gap to the chamber side". [0065]: "in some embodiments it is provided by the snap-back wipe alone, the deflectable ring element being sealed at its periphery so that no inflation gas flows past it while it is deflected". Attack: a competitor builds a superelastic seat ring that is deflected by the poppet in over-travel and snaps back across the sealing face, with its periphery sealed. It has no purge gap and no purge flow path, infringes no claim, and gets the benefit that [0167] attributes to the surfaces the ring wipes. Least narrowing action: add an independent valve core claim reciting the deflectable ring element carrying the sealing edge, deflected radially by the poppet in over-travel and moving across the sealing face on shape recovery, without the purge gap, with the purge gap as a dependent claim; add a corresponding method claim. Supported by [0061], [0065], [0092], FIG. 4. Requires the new claims and preferably one figure of the sealed-periphery form. Target P5.

### F-36-3 [medium] [support] Two-state, depressor-actuated and deflation-only operating modes have no independent claim

Claim 1: "a normal-open state, at a pressure differential at or above the crack pressure and not exceeding an over-travel threshold higher than the crack pressure"; claim 79: "raising the pressure differential above an over-travel threshold". [0051]: "the purge state is reached on substantially every opening ... the second spring stage (54) may be omitted"; [0066]: over-travel "by mechanical depression of the poppet ... whatever the pressure differential"; [0067]: "in other embodiments it is reached during deflation only". Attack: each is a product outside every independent claim. Least narrowing action: an independent claim defining the purge state by poppet displacement beyond a first displacement, with the pressure threshold, the depressor and the deflation purge as dependent alternatives, supported by [0012], [0051], [0066], [0067], FIG. 7, FIG. 15A. Requires new claims, not new description. Target P5.

### F-36-4 [medium] [support] No ranges for the normal stroke, the rest clearance of the purge shoulder, or the spring-stage rate ratio

[0046] and [0090]: "the normal stroke is 0.6 mm" (single value); [0038]: the axial clearance "is such that the purge shoulder 44 remains spaced from the seat ring 30 throughout the normal stroke" (no value); [0039], [0119]: the second spring stage "has a higher rate" (no ratio). Claims 41–44 give pressure ranges only. Attack: the parameters a competitor's core will differ in are the stroke and the clearance, and there is no numeric fallback to amend to; the relationship at [0088] supports a qualitative amendment only. Least narrowing action: add ranges for the normal stroke, the rest clearance (expressed as at least the normal stroke plus a margin) and the ratio of second to first spring-stage rate, bracketing the tested 0.6 mm stroke and 0.5 mm over-travel, as ranges "within which the mechanism is expected to operate" in the manner of [0006] and [0088]. Requires new disclosure from the inventors. Target P4.

### F-36-5 [medium] [support] No generic definition of a "closed" purge gap and no tolerance on "at-rest form"

[0018]: the purge gap "is closed in the closed state and in the normal-open state"; [0105] for one location only: "closed, or is sufficiently small to carry no significant flow"; [0013]: shape recovery is "return towards its at-rest shape"; claims 1, 58, 67 and 79: "shape recovery to its at-rest form"; [0099]: "residual set of the ring element after many cycles". Attack: strict construction of "closed" and "to" gives a competitor two escapes and gives the applicant support for a cure at only one purge gap location. Least narrowing action: add to [0018] that the purge gap is "closed" when closed or carrying no significant flow relative to the main passage, for every location, and to [0013] that shape recovery is return towards the at-rest form sufficient to close the purge gap, residual set notwithstanding. Requires new disclosure (one sentence each). Target P4.

### F-36-6 [medium] [support] Claimed and disclosed features without a figure

Claim 45 "an over-travel stop" ([0049]); claim 28 "a second formation ... which meets the deflectable ring element on a return movement" ([0099]); claim 24 "an integral flexure section of the core body" ([0096]); claim 31 purge gap "bounded by an inner surface of the seat ring and the poppet" ([0105]); claim 32 "one or more openings through a wall" ([0106]); claim 46 "a depressor in a head of an inflation device" ([0066]); and the unclaimed return-spring retainer and purge sleeve ([0116]), conical annular disc ([0082]), purge actuator ([0123]), inverted arrangement ([0117]), fixed wiping land and helical wipe ([0126]), sealed-periphery wipe-only ring ([0065]). None appears in FIGs. 1–15. Attack: in the US, 37 CFR 1.83(a) requires every claimed feature to be shown, and a drawing objection after filing cannot be cured by a figure that adds matter; at the EPO and UK an undrawn alternative is the first to be called an unsupported generalisation. Least narrowing action: add schematic partial sections, in the style of FIGs. 11–14, for the claimed features at least (claims 24, 28, 31, 32, 45, 46) and for the wipe-only and purge-actuator forms; the description already gives the structure. Requires new figures. Target P4.

### F-36-7 [medium] [enablement] How the band-limited interference fit is made is not disclosed

Claim 21: "the interference fit is provided over an axial band of the deflectable ring element adjacent the internal shoulder, and a remainder of an outer surface ... is not in interference with the core body bore"; [0036], [0053], [0133], [0151] repeat the arrangement; none says whether the band is produced by a relief in the core body bore, by a reduced outer diameter on the ring's free portion, or by a step in either, and the ring is drawn from plain tube of 2.7 mm outer diameter ([0089]). Attack: the examiner asks how a 0.20 mm wall ring pressed from plain tube can be in interference over one band and clear elsewhere; the registry's flag 3 concedes the convention is a drafting assumption. Least narrowing action: add one sentence at [0036] stating the means (bore relief, ring relief, or both) and, if known, the axial length of the band. Requires new disclosure; the inventors must confirm which. Target P4.

### F-36-8 [medium] [enablement] Behaviour on top-up inflation is not described

[0011]: the pressure differential is "the supply-side pressure less the chamber-side pressure across the poppet"; [0157]: every tested inflation event ran "from fully deflated to 6 bar"; [0068]: "The two cleaning actions therefore occur once per inflation event"; [0069] and [0179] address only devices that produce a low differential. Attack: on a top-up from 5 bar the differential across an open poppet is the flow-induced drop plus the spring equivalent, not the gauge pressure; whether the 3.5 bar threshold is reached on a floor-pump stroke into an already pressurised chamber is not stated, and the "once per inflation event" statement will be read against the applicant. Least narrowing action: add a paragraph after [0069] stating whether the purge state is reached on top-up inflation, on what basis (transient differential during the pump stroke, or not at all), and that the tested event is inflation from fully deflated; if unknown, say so in the manner of [0165]. Requires new disclosure. Target P4.

### F-36-9 [medium] [support] No claims to non-bicycle sealant-holding chambers, to the kit, or to the inflation system with a depressor head

Claims 64–66: "a bicycle inner tube or a tubeless bicycle tyre set-up"; no kit claim; no claim to an inflation device or adaptor. [0139]: "the same mechanism may be applied to inflation valves for other inflatable chambers that hold liquid sealant"; [0141]: "The seat ring (30) may further be supplied as a kit together with a poppet (40) ... and a return spring (50)"; [0066]: "a depressor in the head of the inflation device, or in an adaptor". Attack: motor-vehicle and motorcycle sealant markets, the parts supplier and the pump-head maker are each outside the claims and cannot be added after filing. Least narrowing action: add a dependent-turned-independent chamber claim generic to "an inflatable chamber holding liquid sealant" with the bicycle forms dependent; a kit claim per [0141]; and an inflation system claim (inflation valve plus inflation device head or adaptor with a depressor sized to move the poppet into over-travel) per [0066]. Requires new claims only. Target P5.

### F-36-10 [low] [support] Springless poppet closed by chamber pressure is not disclosed

Claims 1, 58, 79: "a return spring (50) biasing the poppet towards the closed state"; [0122]: the threshold set by ring stiffness "with a return spring (50) of a single rate"; [0009], [0022]: "biasing member" genus. Attack: the cheapest design-around has no cover. Least narrowing action: if within the inventors' conception, add that in some embodiments the poppet is biased towards the closed state by chamber-side pressure alone and the over-travel threshold is set by the deflectable ring element; otherwise record the boundary. Requires new disclosure and inventor confirmation; evidence of conception is weak. Target P3.

### F-36-11 [low] [support] Inverted arrangement and purge actuator have no fallback claim

[0117]: "the deflectable ring element is a thin skirt carried by the poppet (40), a fixed formation in the core body (20) deflects the skirt"; [0123]: "the deflecting formation is carried not by the poppet (40) but by a separate purge actuator". Claim 1 requires the ring "carried by the core body" and the poppet to produce the deflection. Attack: both are outside every claim. Least narrowing action: dependent or alternative independent formulations in which the ring is carried by the core body or the poppet, and in which a deflecting member movable in response to the pressure differential produces the deflection. Requires new claims only. Target P5.

None.

## Assumptions

A1. The inventor's sketches FIG-1.png and FIG-2.png exist and show the closed state and full over-travel with air-flow arrows, as the figure plan records from the intake README; their content has not been seen and F-36-1 assumes they resolve the axial arrangement.
A2. In a Presta-pattern core the poppet opens by moving towards the chamber side under supply pressure ([0046]); the geometric difficulty in F-36-1 is stated on that basis.
A3. 37 CFR 1.83(a) requires the drawings to show every feature specified in the claims; common general knowledge of US practice, premise of F-36-6.
A4. Cyclists top up tyres more often than they inflate from fully deflated; common general knowledge, premise of F-36-8.

## Open Questions

Q1. Which end of the seat ring carries the sealing edge, where does the internal shoulder sit, and by what route does the purge flow pass the interference band (F-36-1)? Only the inventor's sketches or the inventors can answer.
Q2. Is the springless variant of F-36-10 within the inventors' conception?
Q3. Is the purge state reached on a floor-pump top-up from riding pressure (F-36-8)? Has this been observed, even informally, by the audible snap?
Q4. How is the interference band physically produced (F-36-7)?
Q5. Are the adjacent markets of [0139] (motor-vehicle and motorcycle sealant-holding chambers) commercially intended? If so, F-36-9's generic chamber claim should be added before filing.
