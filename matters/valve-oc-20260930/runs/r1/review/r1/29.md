## Findings

### Amendment Paths

Assumed rejection: independent claims 1, 58, 67, 79 and 90 are rejected in their broadest form. Every path below uses only features expressly disclosed in the package as it stands. "Risk" is the risk of an intermediate-generalisation or added-matter objection to the amended claim, judged as the EPO would under the direct-and-unambiguous standard (A1). The US and UK positions are no stricter on these facts. Paths 1 to 8 amend claim 1. The same wording transposes to claim 58, and the same risk applies. Path 9 amends claim 67 and path 10 amends claim 79.

**Path 1: one-piece seat ring that wipes its own sealing face.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the deflectable ring element is a seat ring (30) having a sealing edge (34) on which the sealing face rests in the closed state, and wherein, as the seat ring undergoes shape recovery when the pressure differential falls, the seat ring moves across the sealing face and shears sealant film from the sealing face."
- **Combination support.** Claims 8, 25, 26; [0015], [0060]–[0062], [0092] (the one-piece form in which "the part that seals is also the part that wipes"), [0124]; FIG. 4.
- **Risk.** None for the combination as such: [0092] states it for the one-piece seat ring without tying it to NiTi, and [0063] and [0124] extend the wipe to a PEEK seat ring. The weakness is evidential, not formal. The wipe's strength depends on the material ([0063]), and the only statement that it occurs whatever the material sits in the operation section, not in [0092].
- **Addition.** F-29-6.

**Path 2: purge shoulder bearing axially on a convoluted seat ring.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the poppet has a purge shoulder (44), being an annular shoulder integral with the poppet on the chamber side of the sealing face, which is spaced from the deflectable ring element in the closed state and throughout the normal stroke and bears axially on the deflectable ring element in over-travel, and wherein the deflectable ring element is a seat ring (30) having a sealing edge (34) on which the sealing face rests in the closed state and a convoluted wall (32) which converts the axial load into the radial deflection."
- **Combination support.** Claims 2–4, 8, 10, 11; [0034], [0038], [0052], [0078], [0079], [0112].
- **Risk.** Possible. The combination is the illustrated embodiment and is disclosed together in [0112]. But the text does not state which face of the seat ring the shoulder bears on, and the stated axial layout does not show how contact occurs (F-28-2). A further fallback such as "bearing on the end of the seat ring carrying the sealing edge" has no basis at all.
- **Addition.** F-29-5.

**Path 3: superelastic seat ring with low austenite finish temperature.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the deflectable ring element is a seat ring (30) having a sealing edge (34) on which the sealing face rests in the closed state, the seat ring being formed from a superelastic alloy having an austenite finish temperature below 5 °C."
- **Combination support.** Claims 8, 47, 48; [0035], [0072], [0145] (which states this combination in terms).
- **Risk.** None. Adding "nickel-titanium" is also direct from [0145] ("such as an alloy of nickel and titanium"). Adding "nominally 55.8 wt%" is direct from [0035] and [0072].
- **Addition.** None needed.

**Path 4: two-stage return spring as the threshold-setting arrangement.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the return spring has a first spring stage (52) of lower rate which alone resists the normal stroke and sets the crack pressure, and a second spring stage (54) of higher rate than the first spring stage which comes into action when the normal stroke is complete and sets the over-travel threshold."
- **Combination support.** Claims 36, 37; [0022], [0039], [0046], [0048], [0119]. The further fallback "the return spring being a single compression spring whose close-pitched coils become solid at the end of the normal stroke" is in [0050] and [0119].
- **Risk.** None.
- **Addition.** None needed.

**Path 5: seat-ring dimensions.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the deflectable ring element is a seat ring (30) formed from a superelastic alloy, having a sealing edge (34) on which the sealing face rests in the closed state and a convoluted wall (32) having one or two convolutions of a convolution amplitude of from 0.08 mm to 0.20 mm, a seat ring inner diameter of from 2.0 mm to 2.6 mm, a seat ring wall thickness of from 0.15 mm to 0.30 mm and a seat ring free length of from 2.5 mm to 4.0 mm."
- **Combination support.** Claims 8, 11–13, 16, 17, 47; [0028], [0088], [0089], [0091].
- **Risk.** None if "superelastic alloy" and "convoluted wall" are both kept, since [0088] and [0091] tie the ranges to exactly that ring. Likely if either is dropped (F-28-3). Also possible if the tiers are mixed, for example the middle inner-diameter tier with the outer wall-thickness tier. [0089] gives each parameter its own nested list, with no statement that any intermediate tiers belong together, and [0006]'s sub-range boilerplate does not supply that link.
- **Addition.** F-29-2.

**Path 6: retention at the internal shoulder, with the deflecting wall left free.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the core body has a core body bore (22) and an internal shoulder (24), the deflectable ring element being retained against the internal shoulder by an interference fit provided over an axial band of the deflectable ring element adjacent the internal shoulder, a remainder of an outer surface of the deflectable ring element, including a portion of its wall which undergoes the radial deflection, not being in interference with the core body bore, the radial deflection being an elastic deformation of the wall while the deflectable ring element remains retained in the core body."
- **Combination support.** Claims 18, 19, 21; [0020], [0036], [0052], [0053], [0092], [0133], [0151]; FIG. 6A.
- **Risk.** Possible. The axial band appears only for the seat ring (30) and only "in some embodiments" ([0036], [0133], [0151]). The genus wording "deflectable ring element" rests on [0094] ("every reference herein to the deflectable ring element includes both"), which an examiner may regard as too general for this specific feature. The package also flags the feature as an unconfirmed drafting convention (F-28-7).
- **Addition.** F-29-4.

**Path 7: purge gap at the outer surface, with flutes.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the deflectable ring element is a seat ring (30) having a sealing edge (34) on which the sealing face rests in the closed state, the purge gap is an annular radial clearance between an outer surface of the seat ring and a core body bore (22) of the core body, the radial deflection moving a bounding portion of the outer surface away from the core body bore, and the purge flow path includes one or more flutes (362) formed as axial grooves in the core body bore on whose lands the outer surface lies in the closed state and in the normal-open state."
- **Combination support.** Claims 8, 30, 34; [0040], [0103], [0110]; FIG. 13B.
- **Risk.** Likely. The illustrated embodiment is described as opening this gap by outward deflection ([0020], [0053], [0054]), which [0103] and [0110] contradict (F-28-1). An examiner can say the application discloses no single coherent embodiment in which the outer-surface gap opens by movement away from the bore together with the flutes. [0103] offers inward movement only as "may be", in a list of three mechanisms.
- **Addition.** F-29-3.

**Path 8: operating pressures and over-travel distance.**
Amended claim 1 adds: "wherein the crack pressure is from 0.8 bar to 1.5 bar, the over-travel threshold is from 2.5 bar to 5.0 bar, and in over-travel the poppet moves beyond an end of the normal stroke by an over-travel distance of from 0.4 mm to 0.9 mm."
- **Combination support.** Claims 41–43; [0011], [0012], [0090]. The fallback "the over-travel threshold being exceeded on inflation of the inflatable chamber by a floor pump" is in [0011], [0051] and [0090].
- **Risk.** Possible. [0088] and [0091] confine these ranges to the superelastic convoluted seat ring. The broadest tiers of all three parameters sit together in [0090], but the intermediate and narrow tiers are separate nested lists (as in path 5). The worked-example values of claim 44 ("about 1.0 bar", "about 3.5 bar") taken without the example's seat ring are an intermediate generalisation from Example 1 ([0156]).
- **Addition.** F-29-2.

**Path 9: seat-ring claim 67 restricted to the superelastic continuous ring.**
Amended claim 67 adds: "the seat ring being a continuous ring whose sealing edge is continuous around its circumference, formed from a superelastic alloy having an austenite finish temperature below 5 °C, the convoluted wall having one convolution or two convolutions."
- **Combination support.** Claims 68, 71, 73, 74; [0034], [0035], [0072], [0079], [0085], [0089], [0145].
- **Risk.** None. It also cures the support objection of F-28-3 for claim 67.
- **Addition.** None needed.

**Path 10: method, floor-pump inflation with the seat ring wiping the sealing face, repeated.**
Amended claim 79 adds: "wherein the deflectable ring element is a seat ring having a sealing edge on which the sealing face rests in the closed state, the inflation device is a floor pump which produces the pressure differential exceeding the over-travel threshold, and, as the seat ring undergoes shape recovery, it moves across the sealing face and shears sealant film from it, the coupling, the raising and the removing being repeated over a plurality of inflation events with the air purge and the shear occurring in each inflation event in which the pressure differential exceeds the over-travel threshold."
- **Combination support.** Claims 80, 83, 87; [0060]–[0062], [0068], [0070], [0092], [0157], [0158].
- **Risk.** None with "seat ring" included. Without it, possible: for the wiper ring, [0125] says only "a surface of the poppet", not the sealing face.
- **Addition.** F-29-6.

### F-29-1 [medium] [support] The only basis for most multi-feature fallbacks is the generic combination statement in [0143]

Paragraphs cited: [0143]: "Each of the alternative locations …, the alternative purge flow paths …, the alternative deflecting formations … is disclosed in combination with each of the others and with each of the three principal embodiments". Claims affected: 1, 58, 67, 79 on amendment.

**Attack.** Paths 2, 6, 7 and 8, and any fallback that picks one species from each of two or more lists, rely on [0143]. The lists include deflecting formation, wall geometry, purge-gap location, purge flow path, threshold arrangement, material and retention. At the EPO a blanket statement that every alternative is combinable with every other is commonly given little weight when an amendment selects from several lists (A2). The examiner asks whether the particular combination is individually disclosed. Only [0145] spells out specific combinations, and it covers material, core body and wall only.

**Least-narrowing action.** Add, before filing, a short "preferred combinations" section (P4) on the model of [0145]. Each combination should be stated in terms, one sentence each, for paths 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10. Useful additions are: purge shoulder + convoluted seat ring + two-stage spring; superelastic seat ring + interference band + outer-surface gap + flutes; seat ring + floor-pump threshold; fixed seat + stainless wiper ring + purge ports. The combinations are already asserted by [0143], so this adds no new technical matter but gives each fallback its own text. Requires new disclosure: yes (new text).

### F-29-2 [medium] [support] The nested ranges are separate lists: intermediate tiers are not linked to one another or to any ring other than the NiTi convoluted seat ring

Paragraphs cited: [0089], [0090], [0091]. Claims affected: 13, 16, 17, 35, 41–43, 67, 69, 70, 82.

**Attack.** Each parameter in [0089] and [0090] has three tiers, "in some embodiments" each, and no passage links the middle tier of one parameter to the middle tier of another. An amendment to "inner diameter 2.1–2.5 mm and wall thickness 0.16–0.24 mm" therefore combines selections from two lists. The same goes for crack pressure 0.8–1.2 bar with threshold 2.75–4.25 bar. [0006] declares sub-ranges disclosed, but does not disclose their combination (A2). All tiers are tied to the superelastic convoluted seat ring ([0088], [0091]), so none gives basis for a PEEK or wiper-ring claim (F-28-3).

**Least-narrowing action.** Add sentences (P4) stating the tiers together. For example: "In a preferred embodiment the seat ring inner diameter is from 2.1 mm to 2.5 mm, the seat ring wall thickness from 0.16 mm to 0.24 mm, the seat ring free length from 2.6 mm to 3.5 mm and the convolution amplitude from 0.08 mm to 0.16 mm", with the same for the narrowest tier and for the pressure/stroke parameters. Add the inventor's answer on whether the ranges hold for other materials (F-28-3). The values are already disclosed, so no new values are introduced. Requires new disclosure: yes (new text).

### F-29-3 [medium] [support] No coherent embodiment gives basis to amend to an outer-surface purge gap

Paragraphs cited: [0020], [0053], [0054], [0103], [0110]. Claims affected: 30, 34.

**Attack.** Path 7 is the fallback that most directly reads on a competitor's teardown of the illustrated product. Its only textual basis for the gap opening by the outer surface moving "away from the core body bore" is [0103], which offers inward movement as one of three "may be" mechanisms. The illustrated-embodiment passages say the opposite (outward deflection). An examiner can refuse the amendment as a combination not disclosed as a working embodiment. This is the amendment-basis consequence of F-28-1.

**Least-narrowing action.** Once the inventor confirms the mechanism (F-28-1), add a paragraph (P4) describing the illustrated embodiment end to end. It should cover: the outer-surface gap, the direction of movement of its bounding portion, how the flutes (362) of FIG. 13B are uncovered by that movement, and the interference band leaving that portion free. Requires new disclosure: yes.

### F-29-4 [medium] [support] The interference band is disclosed only for the seat ring, only "in some embodiments", and is flagged in the package as unconfirmed

Paragraphs cited: [0036], [0133], [0151]; FIG. 6A. Claims affected: 21, and the path 6 amendment of claim 1.

**Attack.** Path 6 recites the band for a generic "deflectable ring element". The band is disclosed for "the seat ring 30" only. Extending it to the wiper ring (132) and to rings retained by crimp or sleeve depends on [0094]'s general equivalence statement, which is an intermediate-generalisation target. The package's own figure instructions describe the band as "not an asserted inventor fact" (F-28-7).

**Least-narrowing action.** After inventor confirmation, add a sentence (P4): "Whatever the form of the deflectable ring element, including the wiper ring (132), the ring retention may act over an axial band adjacent the internal shoulder (24) or other locating formation, the remainder of the outer surface, including the portion that undergoes radial deflection, being free of the core body bore (22)." Requires new disclosure: yes.

### F-29-5 [medium] [support] The face of the seat ring on which the purge shoulder bears is never stated

Paragraphs cited: [0038], [0052], [0112], [0116]. Claims affected: 3, 4, and path 2.

**Attack.** The natural fallback against deflecting-element art is to say where the load is applied, for example "on the end of the seat ring carrying the sealing edge" or "on the sealing edge region". It has no basis. [0038] and [0052] say only that the purge shoulder "bears on the seat ring". [0116] implies, by contrast, that in the illustrated embodiment the contact is not at the end remote from the sealing edge, but never says where it is. Combined with F-28-2, path 2 cannot be narrowed further than claim 4 without new matter.

**Least-narrowing action.** Add, with the inventor's layout (F-28-2), a sentence (P4) stating the face or region of the seat ring that the purge shoulder contacts in the illustrated embodiment, and its relation to the sealing edge (34) and the convolutions (32a, 32b). Requires new disclosure: yes.

### F-29-6 [low] [support] The wipe of the sealing face is not stated to occur for every material of one-piece seat ring

Paragraphs cited: [0061], [0062], [0063], [0092], [0124]. Claims affected: 26, 80, and paths 1 and 10.

**Attack.** The wipe of the sealing face itself is described for the illustrated NiTi seat ring ([0061], [0062], [0092]). For PEEK, [0063] and [0124] say the recovering ring's movement "across the surface of the poppet" is a snap-back wipe, but do not say that surface is the sealing face. An examiner could treat path 1 or 10 without "superelastic" as generalising the sealing-face wipe from the NiTi example.

**Least-narrowing action.** Add one sentence (P4), if the inventor agrees: "In every one-piece seat ring (30), whatever its material, the region of the sealing edge (34) moves across the sealing face (42) during shape recovery." Requires new disclosure: yes.

## Assumptions

A1. The EPO's direct-and-unambiguous test for amendments is applied as the strictest of the three offices. It is stated as general practice, not as cited authority. US and UK positions are no stricter on these facts.

A2. General EPO practice, not cited authority: a blanket statement that all disclosed alternatives may be combined is often given limited weight where an amendment selects features from two or more lists. A declaration that sub-ranges are disclosed does not by itself disclose the combination of sub-ranges of different parameters.

A3. The package is judged as it stands: no drawings, no abstract. The paths use description text and claims as filed only.
