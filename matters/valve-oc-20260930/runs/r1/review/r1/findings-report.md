#### F-32-4 — Every independent claim ties the purge to over-travel of the poppet caused by the pressure differential

**Severity** high · **Area** design-around and detectability · **Claims** 1, 46, 57, 58, 79 · **Specification** [0066], [0112], [0123], [0137]

**Raised by** ip-reviewer (review step 32)

**The objection.** Depressor or hand over-travel [0066], a separate purge actuator with the poppet on its normal stroke only [0123], and Schrader cores opened by the head pin [0137] all fall outside claims 1, 58 and 79; claim 57 describes a combination a competitor would not build; if F-32-2 shows depression, the tested embodiment is outside the claims too.

**Least narrowing fix.** Define the purge state by the poppet or a deflecting formation moving with it being beyond the normal stroke, with the pressure-differential trigger and mechanical depression as sibling dependent claims; or add an independent claim with a deflecting formation driven by the poppet or a pressure-responsive purge actuator.


**Ruling** upheld — Claims 1 and 58 define the purge state as occurring 'at a pressure differential exceeding the over-travel threshold, in which the poppet is in over-travel', and claim 79 requires 'raising the pressure differential above an over-travel threshold, whereby the poppet moves into over-travel'. The description discloses three routes outside that wording: over-travel by a depressor or by hand 'whatever the pressure differential', with the second spring stage omitted ([0066]); a separate purge actuator carrying the deflecting formation 'the poppet (40) performing only its normal stroke' ([0123]); and Schrader poppets depressed by the pump head ([0137]). A competitor with a single-rate spring and pin-driven over-travel, or with an actuator, escapes claims 1, 58 and 79. In the US, disclosed but unclaimed subject matter risks dedication to the public. Claim 46 adds depression only as an extra to the pressure-driven device, and claim 57 keeps the pressure trigger. High is right. The proposed remedy keeps the normal-stroke/over-travel structure. The purge-actuator limb of F-32-6 is carried here.

#### F-32-7 — The test evidence does not show that the pressure differential drives over-travel
