### FTO Issue List

Patentability does not establish freedom to operate. A granted patent on the valve core of claim 1 gives the right to exclude others from that valve core; it gives no right to make, sell or import it, and the product described at [0031]–[0041] and [0136]–[0141] may practise the live claims of third parties whose patents cover a component, a material, a manufacturing step or the valve stem into which the core is fitted. Patentability is judged against the whole prior art; freedom to operate is judged only against claims that are in force in the jurisdiction of manufacture, import or sale. The two analyses overlap in their source documents and in nothing else.

No third-party patent claims are in front of me beyond the references indexed by the Prior-Art Analyst and their saved texts, which were gathered for patentability, not for clearance.
