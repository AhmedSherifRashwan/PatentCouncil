**Inventive nucleus used as the yardstick (from the disclosure, §2–§3).**
- N1 A seat ring (30) against which the poppet (40) seals, the seat ring being made of a superelastic NiTi alloy.
- N2 The seat ring has a thin convoluted (wave) wall so that axial load on its upper edge becomes radial expansion.
- N3 Poppet over-travel beyond the normal opening stroke, reached only above a pressure-differential threshold (two-rate return spring (50)).
- N4 During over-travel the purge shoulder (44) loads the seat ring, which flexes radially and opens a purge gap (60) that is closed at rest.
- N5 Air at pump pressure flows through the purge gap across the surfaces where sealant accumulates.
- N6 On pressure release the seat ring recovers with a snap and wipes across the poppet face, shearing partly cured sealant.
- N7 Context: Presta-pattern inner-tube or tubeless valve used with latex liquid sealant.

## Rationale

The nucleus was split into seven concepts (N1–N7) before any query was written, so that each track tests a separable element and the combination is searched separately. The invention's danger zones are two mechanisms that exist widely outside tyre valves: pressure-driven deposit purging, and wiping by a component that moves as the valve closes. So Track 3 (functional equivalents) and Track 6 (adjacent fields) were given as much weight as the tyre-valve tracks: an examiner will combine a tyre-valve reference with a wiper or SMA-seal reference, and the search has to find those second references first.

The superelastic material was searched separately from the wave geometry because they are separately claimable and appear in different fields (medical, aerospace static seals). The manufacturing and use tracks were given lighter coverage because the prototype process (§7) is standard NiTi practice and is unlikely to carry the claims. The thermal two-way variant (§6) was left to Track 2 vocabulary and not given its own track, because the inventors doubt it.

Query vocabulary was kept generic: no query combined bicycle-valve context with NiTi, wave geometry and over-travel, so no single query restated the invention.
